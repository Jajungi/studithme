Ch.13 용액 · Solutions

용어 · 용해

  • Solvent / solute; saturated = dissolution ⇌ crystallization (dynamic equilibrium). Supersaturated is metastable.용매·용질. 포화 = 용해↔석출 동적 평형. 과포화는 불안정.
  • Like dissolves like: match polarity / IMF (dispersion, dipole–dipole, H-bond, ion–dipole).비슷한 극성·IMF끼리 잘 녹음.
  • Solids: $T$↑ often solubility↑. Gases: $T$↑ solubility↓; $P$↑ (Henry) solubility↑.고체는 보통 $T$↑ 용해도↑. 기체는 $T$↑↓, 부분압↑↑.

농도

  • Mass %, ppm, mole fraction $x$, molarity $M=\mathrm{mol/L_{soln}}$, molality $m=\mathrm{mol/kg_{solvent}}$. Density often needed for conversions.$M$은 용액 L, $m$은 용매 kg. 환산에 밀도 자주 필요.
  • $M$ changes with $T$ (volume); $m$ essentially does not.온도↑ → 부피↑ → $M$↓; $m$은 거의 불변.

총괄성

  • Depend on particle count: vapor-pressure lowering, $\Delta T_b$, $\Delta T_f$, $\Pi$. Use $i$ for electrolytes (ideal: nonelectrolyte 1, NaCl 2, CaCl₂ 3; real $i$ often lower).입자 수에만 의존. 전해질은 $i$.
  • $\Delta T$: molality $m$. $\Pi=iMRT$: molarity $M$.$\Delta T$→$m$, $\Pi$→$M$ — 섞지 말 것.

Ch.15 평형 · Equilibrium

동적 평형 · K

  • Forward rate = reverse rate; concentrations constant, reactions continue (e.g. N2O4⇌2NO2\mathrm{N_2O_4\rightleftharpoons 2NO_2}N2​O4​⇌2NO2​).정·역 속도 같음. 농도는 일정해도 반응은 계속.
  • Write $K_c$ / $K_p$ from stoichiometry; omit pure solids/liquids. Homogeneous vs heterogeneous.고체·순수 액체 제외. 균일/불균일 구분.
  • $K\\gg 1$ mostly products; $K\\ll 1$ mostly reactants. $K$ is for a written equation at fixed $T$.$K$≫1 생성물 우세, ≪1 반응물 우세.

Q · ICE · K 조작

  • $Q$ uses current amounts → compare to $K$ for direction.$Q$ vs $K$로 이동 방향.
  • ICE for equilibrium $[\\ ]$ or $K$; check approximations (e.g. 5% rule).ICE로 평형 농도·$K$. 근사 타당성 확인.
  • Reverse → $1/K$; ×n → $K^n$; add reactions → multiply $K$ values.역·배수·합성 시 $K$ 조작.

Le Châtelier

  • Concentration / $P$–$V$ (gases): shift position, $K$ unchanged (fixed $T$).
  • Temperature: only factor that changes $K$ (endo: $T$↑ ⇒ $K$↑).
  • Catalyst: faster equilibration, same $K$ and composition.
  • Inert gas at constant $V$: usually no shift.

농도·압력은 이동만, $K$는 그대로. 온도만 $K$ 변경. 촉매는 도달만 빠르게. 부피 일정 불활성 기체 → 보통 이동 없음.

Ch.16 산–염기 · Acid–Base

정의

TheoryAcidBase
Arrhenius↑[H⁺] in water↑[OH⁻]
Brønsted–LowryH⁺ donorH⁺ acceptor
Lewise⁻-pair acceptore⁻-pair donor

Water amphiprotic. Conjugate pairs differ by one H⁺. Every Brønsted acid is Lewis; not every Lewis acid is Brønsted.물 = 양쪽성. 짝쌍 = H⁺ 하나 차이. Brønsted ⊂ Lewis(산 쪽).

Kw · 강/약 · ICE

  • 25 °C: $K_w=10^{-14}$, pH + pOH = 14. Strong → complete ionization (no ICE). Weak → ICE with $K_a$ or $K_b$.강전해질은 ICE 없음. 약은 ICE.
  • % ionization ≈ $100\\sqrt{K_a/C}$ (weak acid, same $C$: smaller $K_a$ → lower %). Dilution ↑ % ionization.같은 $C$면 $K_a$ 작을수록 %↓. 묽히면 %↑.
  • $K_a K_b = K_w$ for a conjugate pair.짝쌍에서만 $K_a K_b=K_w$.

염 수용액 pH

  1. Split into cation + anion.
  2. Cation: Na⁺, K⁺, Ba²⁺… → spectator; NH₄⁺ → acidic; Fe³⁺, Al³⁺… → acidic (hydrolysis).
  3. Anion: Cl⁻, NO₃⁻, ClO₄⁻… → spectator; CH₃COO⁻, CN⁻, CO₃²⁻… → basic; HCO₃⁻, H₂PO₄⁻… → compare $K_a$ vs $K_b$.
  4. One active ion wins; both spectators → neutral; both active → compare strengths.

예: NH₄Br 산성, FeCl₃ 산성, Na₂CO₃ 염기성, KClO₄ 중성. 강산·강염기 목록을 먼저 외우면 빠름.
