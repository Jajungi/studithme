Initial-rate law for HgCl₂ / C₂O₄²⁻

Worked solution
Answer
Determine m,n from table; then k and predicted rate
한글
표에서 m,n → k → 예측 속도
  1. Find orders. Use pairs of experiments that change only [HgCl2][\mathrm{HgCl_2}][HgCl2​] or only [C2O42−][\mathrm{C_2O_4^{2-}}][C2​O42−​] and compare rate ratios.
  2. (a) Rate law. Write rate=k[HgCl2]m[C2O42−]n\mathrm{rate}=k[\mathrm{HgCl_2}]^m[\mathrm{C_2O_4^{2-}}]^nrate=k[HgCl2​]m[C2​O42−​]n with integers m,nm,nm,n from the data.
  3. (b) 0. Solve using any experiment; include concentration units consistent with overall order.
  4. (c) Predict. Substitute the given concentrations into the rate law with your kkk.
한글 해설
  1. 차수. [HgCl2][\mathrm{HgCl_2}][HgCl2​]만 또는 [C2O42−][\mathrm{C_2O_4^{2-}}][C2​O42−​]만 바뀐 쌍으로 m,nm,nm,n을 정한다.
  2. (a) rate=k[HgCl2]m[C2O42−]n\mathrm{rate}=k[\mathrm{HgCl_2}]^m[\mathrm{C_2O_4^{2-}}]^nrate=k[HgCl2​]m[C2​O42−​]n.
  3. (b) 한 실험으로 kkk를 구하고 전체 차수에 맞는 단위를 붙인다.
  4. (c) 구한 kkk에 주어진 농도를 넣어 속도를 예측한다.
