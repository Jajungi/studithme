pH after mixing Ca(OH)₂ and H₂SO₄

Ca(OH)₂–H₂SO₄ 혼합 pH

A solution is made by adding 1.000 g Ca(OH)2(s)\mathrm{Ca(OH)_2}(s)Ca(OH)2​(s), 100.0 mL of 0.10 M H2SO4\mathrm{H_2SO_4}H2​SO4​, and enough water to make a final volume of 350.0 mL. Assuming all solid dissolves, what is the pH of the final solution?
한글
Ca(OH)₂ 1.000 g + 0.10 M H₂SO₄ 100.0 mL → 350.0 mL. 최종 pH는?

Hints
  1. Both are strong electrolytes for H⁺/OH⁻ bookkeeping: Ca(OH)2\mathrm{Ca(OH)_2}Ca(OH)2​ supplies 2 OH⁻; H2SO4\mathrm{H_2SO_4}H2​SO4​ supplies 2 H⁺ (first fully; second strong in dilute).
  2. Net excess OH⁻ or H⁺ after neutralization / final volume.
한글 힌트
  1. OH⁻·H⁺ mol 수지 후 최종 부피.
Worked solution
Answer
pH≈12.3\mathrm{pH}\approx 12.3pH≈12.3
  1. Moles of base. n(Ca(OH)2)=1.000/74.09=0.01350 moln(\mathrm{Ca(OH)_2})=1.000/74.09=0.01350\,\mathrm{mol}n(Ca(OH)2​)=1.000/74.09=0.01350mol ⇒ n(OH−)=0.02700 moln(\mathrm{OH^-})=0.02700\,\mathrm{mol}n(OH−)=0.02700mol.
  2. Moles of acid. n(H2SO4)=0.100×0.10=0.0100 moln(\mathrm{H_2SO_4})=0.100\times 0.10=0.0100\,\mathrm{mol}n(H2​SO4​)=0.100×0.10=0.0100mol ⇒ n(H+)≈0.0200 moln(\mathrm{H^+})\approx 0.0200\,\mathrm{mol}n(H+)≈0.0200mol (2 H⁺ per H₂SO₄).
  3. Neutralization. Excess OH−=0.02700−0.0200=0.0070 mol\mathrm{OH^-}=0.02700-0.0200=0.0070\,\mathrm{mol}OH−=0.02700−0.0200=0.0070mol.
  4. Final [OH−][\mathrm{OH^-}][OH−]. 0.0070/0.350=0.020 M0.0070/0.350=0.020\,\mathrm{M}0.0070/0.350=0.020M ⇒ pOH=1.70\mathrm{pOH}=1.70pOH=1.70, pH=12.30\mathrm{pH}=12.30pH=12.30.
한글 답
pH≈12.3\mathrm{pH}\approx 12.3pH≈12.3
한글 해설
  1. OH⁻ 0.0270 mol, H⁺ 0.0200 mol → 과량 OH⁻ 0.0070 mol.
  2. [OH−]=0.020 M[\mathrm{OH^-}]=0.020\,\mathrm{M}[OH−]=0.020M → pH≈12.3\mathrm{pH}\approx 12.3pH≈12.3.
