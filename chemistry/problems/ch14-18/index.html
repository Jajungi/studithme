A→B time table — rates and order

A→B 표 — 속도·차수

Hypothetical A(aq)→B(aq)\mathrm{A(aq)\rightarrow B(aq)}A(aq)→B(aq). A flask is charged with 0.065 mol A in 100.0 mL. Use the data table in the figure.

(a) Moles of B at each time (no B at t=0t=0t=0; clean conversion).
(b) Average rate of disappearance of A for each 10 min interval (M/s).
(c) Average rate of appearance of B from 0 to 30 min (M/s).
(d) Determine the reaction order w.r.t. A from appropriate graphs.
(e) Time to consume 90% of A initially present.

Answer
Use table; order from linear plot; t from integrated law
  1. (a) nB=0.065−nAn_B=0.065-n_AnB​=0.065−nA​ at each time.
  2. (b)–(c) Convert to M, compute −Δ[A]/Δt-\Delta[A]/\Delta t−Δ[A]/Δt (and +Δ[B]/Δt+\Delta[B]/\Delta t+Δ[B]/Δt).
  3. (d) Test linear [A][A][A], ln⁡[A]\ln[A]ln[A], 1/[A]1/[A]1/[A] vs ttt.
  4. (e) Use the integrated law matching the order for 90% consumption.
