N₂ + O₂ ⇌ 2NO stresses

N₂+O₂⇌2NO 교란

Nitrogen and oxygen react at high temperature: N2(g)+O2(g)⇌2NO(g)\mathrm{N_2}(g)+\mathrm{O_2}(g)\rightleftharpoons 2\mathrm{NO}(g)N2​(g)+O2​(g)⇌2NO(g) (endo; see figure).

(a) Write KcK_cKc​
(b) Effect of adding O₂
(c) Effect of increasing TTT
(d) Effect of removing N₂
(e) Effect of adding NO
(f) Effect of increasing PPP by reducing VVV
(g) Effect of adding a catalyst

Hints
  1. Only TTT changes KKK; catalyst changes rate, not KKK or position.
한글 힌트
  1. 온도만 KKK 변경; Δng=0\Delta n_g=0Δng​=0.
Worked solution
Answer
(a) [NO]²/([N₂][O₂]); (b)→NO (c)→NO, K↑ (d)← (e)← (f) no shift (g) rate only
  1. (a) Kc=[NO]2/([N2][O2])K_c=[\mathrm{NO}]^2/([\mathrm{N_2}][\mathrm{O_2}])Kc​=[NO]2/([N2​][O2​]).
  2. (b) Add O₂. Forward shift to consume O₂; [NO][\mathrm{NO}][NO]↑, [N2][\mathrm{N_2}][N2​]↓ at new eq; KKK unchanged.
  3. (c) Increase TTT. Endothermic forward ⇒ KKK↑ and equilibrium shifts toward NO.
  4. (d) Remove N₂. Reverse shift to replenish N₂; [NO][\mathrm{NO}][NO]↓.
  5. (e) Add NO. Reverse shift; [N2][\mathrm{N_2}][N2​], [O2][\mathrm{O_2}][O2​]↑.
  6. (f) Reduce VVV (↑PPP). Δng=0\Delta n_g=0Δng​=0 ⇒ no shift of position (concentrations all scale, QQQ unchanged for this stoichiometry).
  7. (g) Catalyst. Reaches eq faster; same KKK and same equilibrium composition.
