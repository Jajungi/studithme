Molar mass from vapor-pressure lowering (ethanol)

에탄올 증기압 내림 → 몰질량

Normal boiling point of ethanol is 78.4∘C78.4^\circ\mathrm{C}78.4∘C. When 3.26 g of a soluble nonelectrolyte is dissolved in 100.0 g ethanol at that temperature, the vapor pressure of the solution is 100 kPa. What is the molar mass of the solute? (Use P∘P^\circP∘ of ethanol at its normal boiling point = 101.325 kPa and Raoult’s law.)
