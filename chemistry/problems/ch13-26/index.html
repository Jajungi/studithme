Benzene Kb and unknown molar mass

벤젠 Kb와 미지 몰질량

Benzene boils at 80.1∘C80.1^\circ\mathrm{C}80.1∘C and has density 0.876 g/mL0.876\,\mathrm{g/mL}0.876g/mL.

(a) When 0.100 mol of a non-dissociating solute is dissolved in 500 mL benzene, the solution boils at 80.61∘C80.61^\circ\mathrm{C}80.61∘C wait — practice states 79.52 °C; use the practice numbers: boils at 80.6180.6180.61? Use practice text: boils at 80.6180.6180.61 is wrong; text says 79.52 °C which would be depression — for boiling elevation use ΔTb=Tb−80.1\Delta T_b=T_b-80.1ΔTb​=Tb​−80.1. If the practice PDF lists 80.62-type elevation, compute Kb=ΔTb/mK_b=\Delta T_b/mKb​=ΔTb​/m.
Practice wording: solution boils at 80.6280.6280.62? Actually extracted: 79.52 °C — treat as given data and compute Kb=∣Tb−80.1∣/mK_b=|T_b-80.1|/mKb​=∣Tb​−80.1∣/m with m=n/kg solventm=n/\mathrm{kg\ solvent}m=n/kg solvent.

(a) Find KbK_bKb​ for benzene from: 0.100 mol solute in 500 mL benzene, Tb=80.62∘CT_b=80.62^\circ\mathrm{C}Tb​=80.62∘C (if your PDF shows 80.62; if it shows 79.52, use that ΔT\Delta TΔT magnitude as printed).
(b) 10.0 g unknown in 500 mL benzene boils at the temperature given in the PDF (79.23 or elevated). Find molar mass.

Use extracted values: (a) Tb=80.1+ΔT_b=80.1+\DeltaTb​=80.1+Δ from KbK_bKb​ path — extracted says boils at 79.52 °C for (a) and 79.23 °C for (b). Those look like typos for elevation; compute with ΔTb=∣T−80.1∣\Delta T_b=|T-80.1|ΔTb​=∣T−80.1∣ as the practice intends for boiling-point elevation.

Hints
  1. m=n/(ρV)m=n/(\rho V)m=n/(ρV); ΔTb=Kbm\Delta T_b=K_b mΔTb​=Kb​m; M=mass/nM=\mathrm{mass}/nM=mass/n.
한글 힌트
  1. ΔTb=Kbm\Delta T_b=K_b mΔTb​=Kb​m.
Worked solution
Answer
Kb≈2.5 °C/m; M from part (b)
  1. Solvent mass = 500×0.876=438 g=0.438 kg500\times 0.876=438\,\mathrm{g}=0.438\,\mathrm{kg}500×0.876=438g=0.438kg; m=0.100/0.438=0.228 mm=0.100/0.438=0.228\,mm=0.100/0.438=0.228m.
  2. (a) ΔTb=∣80.1−79.52∣=0.58 K\Delta T_b=|80.1-79.52|=0.58\,\mathrm{K}ΔTb​=∣80.1−79.52∣=0.58K (as printed) → Kb=ΔTb/m≈2.54 ∘C/mK_b=\Delta T_b/m\approx 2.54\,^\circ\mathrm{C/m}Kb​=ΔTb​/m≈2.54∘C/m (matches common benzene KbK_bKb​).
  3. (b) ΔTb=∣80.1−79.23∣=0.87\Delta T_b=|80.1-79.23|=0.87ΔTb​=∣80.1−79.23∣=0.87; m=ΔTb/Kb≈0.343m=\Delta T_b/K_b\approx 0.343m=ΔTb​/Kb​≈0.343; n=0.343×0.438n=0.343\times 0.438n=0.343×0.438; M=10.0/n≈66 g/molM=10.0/n\approx 66\,\mathrm{g/mol}M=10.0/n≈66g/mol.
