(CH₃)₃AuPH₃ mechanism

금 화합물 메커니즘

(CH3)3AuPH3(CH_3)_3AuPH_3(CH3​)3​AuPH3​ decomposes to C2H6\mathrm{C_2H_6}C2​H6​ and (CH3)AuPH3(CH_3)AuPH_3(CH3​)AuPH3​. Use the mechanism in the figure.

(a) Overall reaction
(b) Intermediates
(c) Molecularity of each step
(d) Rate-determining step
(e) Rate law predicted
(f) Effect of adding PH3\mathrm{PH_3}PH3​ on the rate

Hints
  1. Cancel intermediates; RDS is the slow step; fast equilibrium before RDS affects rate law.
한글 힌트
  1. 중간체 소거; RDS+선행 평형.
Worked solution
Answer
Net ethane + (CH3)AuPH3; PH3 slows via eq.
  1. (a) (CH3)3AuPH3→C2H6+(CH3)AuPH3(CH_3)_3AuPH_3 \rightarrow C_2H_6 + (CH_3)AuPH_3(CH3​)3​AuPH3​→C2​H6​+(CH3​)AuPH3​.
  2. (b) Intermediates: (CH3)3Au(CH_3)_3Au(CH3​)3​Au, (CH3)Au(CH_3)Au(CH3​)Au (not PH₃ if regenerated).
  3. (c) Uni / uni / bi as written on steps.
  4. (d) Slow step = RDS.
  5. (e) Rate law from RDS + equilibrium expression for intermediate.
  6. (f) Extra PH₃ shifts fast equilibrium left → usually slows the rate.
