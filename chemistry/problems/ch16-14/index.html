Strongest conjugate acid from Kb table

Kb 표에서 가장 센 짝산

Using the data in the table, which of the conjugate acids below is the strongest acid?

(A) HClO\mathrm{HClO}HClO
(B) HCO3−\mathrm{HCO_3^-}HCO3−​
(C) H2S\mathrm{H_2S}H2​S
(D) NH3CH3+\mathrm{NH_3CH_3^+}NH3​CH3+​
(E) H2S\mathrm{H_2S}H2​S and HClO\mathrm{HClO}HClO
한글
표의 자료를 이용해, 아래 짝산 중 가장 센 산은?

(A) HClO\mathrm{HClO}HClO
(B) HCO3−\mathrm{HCO_3^-}HCO3−​
(C) H2S\mathrm{H_2S}H2​S
(D) NH3CH3+\mathrm{NH_3CH_3^+}NH3​CH3+​
(E) H2S\mathrm{H_2S}H2​S와 HClO\mathrm{HClO}HClO

Hints
  1. Weakest base ↔ strongest conjugate acid.
  2. Smallest KbK_bKb​ → weakest base.
한글 힌트
  1. 염기가 약할수록 짝산은 세다.
  2. KbK_bKb​가 가장 작은 염기를 고른다.
Worked solution
Answer
(C)
  1. Link base and conjugate acid. The weaker the base (smaller KbK_bKb​), the stronger its conjugate acid.
  2. Read the table. Smallest KbK_bKb​ among the listed bases is for HS−\mathrm{HS^-}HS− (1.8×10−71.8\times 10^{-7}1.8×10−7), slightly smaller than ClO−\mathrm{ClO^-}ClO− (3.3×10−73.3\times 10^{-7}3.3×10−7).
  3. Conjugate acid of HS−\mathrm{HS^-}HS−. That is H2S\mathrm{H_2S}H2​S.
  4. Conclusion. Strongest conjugate acid → (C) H2S\mathrm{H_2S}H2​S.
한글 답
(C)
한글 해설
  1. 관계. 염기 KbK_bKb​↓ → 짝산 셈.
  2. 표 읽기. KbK_bKb​ 최소는 HS−\mathrm{HS^-}HS− (1.8×10−71.8\times 10^{-7}1.8×10−7).
  3. 짝산. HS−\mathrm{HS^-}HS−의 짝산은 H2S\mathrm{H_2S}H2​S.
  4. 답. 가장 센 짝산 → (C).
