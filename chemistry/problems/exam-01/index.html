H₃PO₃ — structure, titration, Ka

H₃PO₃ — 구조·적정·Ka

Consider phosphorous acid (H3PO3\mathrm{H_3PO_3}H3​PO3​) — see figure.

(a) Draw the Lewis structure and identify the acidic hydrogens (diprotic or triprotic).
(b) A 25.0 mL sample of H3PO3\mathrm{H_3PO_3}H3​PO3​ titrated with 0.102 M NaOH requires 23.3 mL of NaOH to neutralize both acidic protons. What is the molarity of H3PO3\mathrm{H_3PO_3}H3​PO3​?
(c) The original solution from (b) has pH=1.59\mathrm{pH}=1.59pH=1.59. Calculate the percent ionization and Ka1K_{a1}Ka1​ (Ka1≫Ka2K_{a1}\gg K_{a2}Ka1​≫Ka2​).
(d) How does the osmotic pressure of 0.050 M HCl compare qualitatively with that of 0.050 M H3PO3\mathrm{H_3PO_3}H3​PO3​? Explain.

아인산(H3PO3\mathrm{H_3PO_3}H3​PO3​)을 생각하시오(그림).

(a) Lewis 구조를 그리고 산성 수소를 표시하시오(이양성자/삼양성자).
(b) H3PO3\mathrm{H_3PO_3}H3​PO3​ 25.0 mL를 0.102 M NaOH로 적정할 때 산성 양성자 둘을 중화하는 데 23.3 mL가 필요했다. 몰농도는?
(c) (b)의 원래 용액 pH=1.59\mathrm{pH}=1.59pH=1.59이다. 이온화%와 Ka1K_{a1}Ka1​을 구하시오(Ka1≫Ka2K_{a1}\gg K_{a2}Ka1​≫Ka2​).
(d) 0.050 M HCl과 0.050 M H3PO3\mathrm{H_3PO_3}H3​PO3​의 삼투압을 정성적으로 비교하고 설명하시오.

Hints
  1. Phosphorous acid is diprotic (two OH on P; one H bonded to P).
  2. (b) n(H+)=2 n(H3PO3)=n(NaOH)n(\mathrm{H^+})=2\,n(\mathrm{H_3PO_3})=n(\mathrm{NaOH})n(H+)=2n(H3​PO3​)=n(NaOH).
  3. (c) [H+]≈10−pH[\mathrm{H^+}]\approx 10^{-\mathrm{pH}}[H+]≈10−pH; %=x/C×100\%=x/C\times 100%=x/C×100; Ka≈x2/(C−x)K_a\approx x^2/(C-x)Ka​≈x2/(C−x).
한글 힌트
  1. 아인산은 이양성자( P에 OH 둘, P–H 하나).
  2. (b) n(H+)=2 n(H3PO3)=n(NaOH)n(\mathrm{H^+})=2\,n(\mathrm{H_3PO_3})=n(\mathrm{NaOH})n(H+)=2n(H3​PO3​)=n(NaOH).
  3. (c) [H+]≈10−pH[\mathrm{H^+}]\approx 10^{-\mathrm{pH}}[H+]≈10−pH; %=x/C×100\%=x/C\times 100%=x/C×100; Ka≈x2/(C−x)K_a\approx x^2/(C-x)Ka​≈x2/(C−x).
Worked solution
Answer
(a) diprotic (b) ≈0.048 M (c) ≈54%; Ka1≈0.030 (d) Π(HCl)>Π(H₃PO₃)
  1. (a) Structure has two acidic O–H and one P–H → diprotic.
  2. (b) n(NaOH)=0.102×0.0233=0.00238 moln(\mathrm{NaOH})=0.102\times 0.0233=0.00238\,\mathrm{mol}n(NaOH)=0.102×0.0233=0.00238mol = acidic H⁺ mol → n(H3PO3)=0.00119 moln(\mathrm{H_3PO_3})=0.00119\,\mathrm{mol}n(H3​PO3​)=0.00119mol; M=0.00119/0.0250≈0.0476 MM=0.00119/0.0250\approx 0.0476\,\mathrm{M}M=0.00119/0.0250≈0.0476M.
  3. (c) [H+]=10−1.59≈0.0257 M[\mathrm{H^+}]=10^{-1.59}\approx 0.0257\,\mathrm{M}[H+]=10−1.59≈0.0257M; %≈(0.0257/0.0476)×100≈54%\%\approx (0.0257/0.0476)\times 100\approx 54\%%≈(0.0257/0.0476)×100≈54%; Ka1≈x2/(C−x)≈0.030K_{a1}\approx x^2/(C-x)\approx 0.030Ka1​≈x2/(C−x)≈0.030.
  4. (d) HCl is strong → i≈2i\approx 2i≈2; H3PO3\mathrm{H_3PO_3}H3​PO3​ is weak → iii closer to 1 → HCl has higher Π\PiΠ at the same nominal MMM.

한글 해설

  2. (b) n(NaOH)=0.00238 moln(\mathrm{NaOH})=0.00238\,\mathrm{mol}n(NaOH)=0.00238mol → n(H3PO3)=0.00119n(\mathrm{H_3PO_3})=0.00119n(H3​PO3​)=0.00119 → M≈0.0476 MM\approx 0.0476\,\mathrm{M}M≈0.0476M.
  3. (c) [H+]≈0.0257 M[\mathrm{H^+}]\approx 0.0257\,\mathrm{M}[H+]≈0.0257M → 이온화% ≈ 54%; Ka1≈0.030K_{a1}\approx 0.030Ka1​≈0.030.
