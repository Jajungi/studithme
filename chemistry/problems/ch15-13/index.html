(a) If Kc=1.3×10−2K_c=1.3\times 10^{-2}Kc​=1.3×10−2, find KpK_pKp​ at the same TTT.
(b) Does equilibrium favor NO and Br₂, or NOBr?
(c)–(d) Calculate KcK_cKc​ for the related equations shown in the figure.

  2. Reverse ↔ 1/K1/K1/K; multiply coefficients by nnn ↔ KnK^nKn.
한글 힌트
  1. Kp=Kc(RT)ΔnK_p=K_c(RT)^{\Delta n}Kp​=Kc​(RT)Δn; 역·배수 조작.
Worked solution
Answer
Kp≈1.07K_p\approx 1.07Kp​≈1.07; favors NOBr; related KcK_cKc​ from reverse/power rules
  1. Gas-mole change. For 2NOBr⇌2NO+Br22\mathrm{NOBr}\rightleftharpoons 2\mathrm{NO}+\mathrm{Br_2}2NOBr⇌2NO+Br2​, Δng=(2+1)−2=+1\Delta n_g=(2+1)-2=+1Δng​=(2+1)−2=+1.
  2. (a) Convert. Kp=Kc(RT)Δng=(1.3×10−2)(0.08206×1000)1=1.07K_p=K_c(RT)^{\Delta n_g}=(1.3\times 10^{-2})(0.08206\times 1000)^{1}=1.07Kp​=Kc​(RT)Δng​=(1.3×10−2)(0.08206×1000)1=1.07 (atm).
  3. (b) Side favored. Kc≪1K_c\ll 1Kc​≪1 ⇒ equilibrium lies toward NOBr (reactants in the as-written equation).
  4. (c)–(d) Algebra on KKK. Use the figure’s reversed / halved / doubled forms: reverse → 1/Kc1/K_c1/Kc​; double → Kc2K_c^2Kc2​; half → Kc\sqrt{K_c}Kc​​.
