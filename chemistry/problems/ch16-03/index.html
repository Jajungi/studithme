pH of ethylamine solution

에틸아민 용액 pH

Calculate the molar concentration of OH−\mathrm{OH^-}OH− in a 0.050 M solution of ethylamine (C2H5NH2\mathrm{C_2H_5NH_2}C2​H5​NH2​; Kb=6.4×10−4K_b=6.4\times 10^{-4}Kb​=6.4×10−4). Calculate the pH of this solution.

Worked solution
Answer
[OH⁻]≈5.4×10⁻³ M; pH≈11.7
  1. Weak base equilibrium. C2H5NH2+H2O⇌C2H5NH3++OH−\mathrm{C_2H_5NH_2 + H_2O \rightleftharpoons C_2H_5NH_3^+ + OH^-}C2​H5​NH2​+H2​O⇌C2​H5​NH3+​+OH−; Kb=[C2H5NH3+][OH−]/[C2H5NH2]K_b = [\mathrm{C_2H_5NH_3^+}][\mathrm{OH^-}]/[\mathrm{C_2H_5NH_2}]Kb​=[C2​H5​NH3+​][OH−]/[C2​H5​NH2​].
  2. ICE (small xxx). Initial C=0.050 MC = 0.050\,\mathrm{M}C=0.050M; Kb≈x2/CK_b \approx x^2/CKb​≈x2/C if x≪Cx \ll Cx≪C.
  3. Estimate x=[OH−]x = [\mathrm{OH^-}]x=[OH−]. x≈(6.4×10−4)(0.050)=5.66×10−3x \approx \sqrt{(6.4\times 10^{-4})(0.050)} = 5.66\times 10^{-3}x≈(6.4×10−4)(0.050)​=5.66×10−3; quadratic gives x≈5.35×10−3 Mx \approx 5.35\times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{M}x≈5.35×10−3M.
  4. pOH and pH. pOH=−log⁡[OH−]≈2.27\mathrm{pOH} = -\log[\mathrm{OH^-}] \approx 2.27pOH=−log[OH−]≈2.27; pH=14−pOH≈11.73\mathrm{pH} = 14 - \mathrm{pOH} \approx 11.73pH=14−pOH≈11.73 (basic solution).
