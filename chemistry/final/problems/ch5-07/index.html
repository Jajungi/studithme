Bomb calorimetry heat of combustion of phenol

폭탄 열량계로 측정한 페놀의 연소열

A 2.20 g2.20\,\mathrm{g}2.20g sample of phenol (C6H5OH\mathrm{C_6H_5OH}C6​H5​OH) was burned in a bomb calorimeter whose total heat capacity is 11.90 kJ/∘C11.90\,\mathrm{kJ/^\circ C}11.90kJ/∘C. The temperature of the calorimeter plus contents increased from 21.50∘C21.50^\circ\mathrm{C}21.50∘C to 27.50∘C27.50^\circ\mathrm{C}27.50∘C.

(a) Write a balanced chemical equation for the bomb calorimeter reaction.

(b) What is the heat of combustion per gram and per mole of phenol? (M=94.11 g/molM=94.11\,\mathrm{g/mol}M=94.11g/mol)
한글
총 열용량이 11.90 kJ/∘C11.90\,\mathrm{kJ/^\circ C}11.90kJ/∘C인 폭탄 열량계에서 페놀 C6H5OH\mathrm{C_6H_5OH}C6​H5​OH 2.20 g2.20\,\mathrm{g}2.20g을 연소시켰더니 열량계와 내용물의 온도가 21.50∘C21.50^\circ\mathrm{C}21.50∘C에서 27.50∘C27.50^\circ\mathrm{C}27.50∘C로 올랐다.

(a) 폭탄 열량계에서 일어난 반응의 균형 반응식을 쓰시오.

(b) 페놀 1 g당, 1 mol당 연소열은 얼마인가? (M=94.11 g/molM=94.11\,\mathrm{g/mol}M=94.11g/mol)

Hints
  1. A bomb calorimeter uses qrxn=−CcalΔTq_{\mathrm{rxn}}=-C_{\mathrm{cal}}\Delta Tqrxn​=−Ccal​ΔT, not mCsΔTmC_s\Delta TmCs​ΔT.
  2. Complete combustion of a C, H, O compound gives CO2(g)\mathrm{CO_2(g)}CO2​(g) and H2O(l)\mathrm{H_2O(l)}H2​O(l).
한글 힌트
  1. 폭탄 열량계는 mCsΔTmC_s\Delta TmCs​ΔT가 아니라 qrxn=−CcalΔTq_{\mathrm{rxn}}=-C_{\mathrm{cal}}\Delta Tqrxn​=−Ccal​ΔT를 쓴다.
  2. C, H, O로 된 화합물의 완전 연소 생성물은 CO2(g)\mathrm{CO_2(g)}CO2​(g)와 H2O(l)\mathrm{H_2O(l)}H2​O(l)이다.
Worked solution
Answer
(a) C6H5OH(s)+7O2(g)→6CO2(g)+3H2O(l)\mathrm{C_6H_5OH(s)+7O_2(g)\rightarrow 6CO_2(g)+3H_2O(l)}C6​H5​OH(s)+7O2​(g)→6CO2​(g)+3H2​O(l); (b) −32.5 kJ/g-32.5\,\mathrm{kJ/g}−32.5kJ/g, −3.06×103 kJ/mol-3.06\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}−3.06×103kJ/mol
  1. Part (a): balance the combustion. Six carbons give 6CO26\mathrm{CO_2}6CO2​ and six hydrogens give 3H2O3\mathrm{H_2O}3H2​O: C6H5OH(s)+7O2(g)→6CO2(g)+3H2O(l)\mathrm{C_6H_5OH(s)+7O_2(g)\rightarrow 6CO_2(g)+3H_2O(l)}C6​H5​OH(s)+7O2​(g)→6CO2​(g)+3H2​O(l). Counting oxygen confirms it: 1+14=151+14=151+14=15 on the left and 12+3=1512+3=1512+3=15 on the right.
  2. Part (b): identify the calorimeter. A total heat capacity in kJ/∘C\mathrm{kJ/^\circ C}kJ/∘C is given, so use qrxn=−CcalΔTq_{\mathrm{rxn}}=-C_{\mathrm{cal}}\Delta Tqrxn​=−Ccal​ΔT and leave the solution specific heat alone.
  3. Find the heat released. ΔT=27.50−21.50=6.00∘C\Delta T=27.50-21.50=6.00^\circ\mathrm{C}ΔT=27.50−21.50=6.00∘C, so qrxn=−(11.90 kJ/∘C)(6.00∘C)=−71.4 kJq_{\mathrm{rxn}}=-(11.90\,\mathrm{kJ/^\circ C})(6.00^\circ\mathrm{C})=-71.4\,\mathrm{kJ}qrxn​=−(11.90kJ/∘C)(6.00∘C)=−71.4kJ.
  4. Convert to a per-gram basis. (−71.4 kJ)/(2.20 g)=−32.5 kJ/g(-71.4\,\mathrm{kJ})/(2.20\,\mathrm{g})=-32.5\,\mathrm{kJ/g}(−71.4kJ)/(2.20g)=−32.5kJ/g.
  5. Convert to a per-mole basis. (−32.5 kJ/g)(94.11 g/mol)=−3.06×103 kJ/mol(-32.5\,\mathrm{kJ/g})(94.11\,\mathrm{g/mol})=-3.06\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}(−32.5kJ/g)(94.11g/mol)=−3.06×103kJ/mol.
  6. Takeaway. The bomb runs at constant volume, so this measured heat is strictly ΔE\Delta EΔE; for combustion the difference from ΔH\Delta HΔH is small but worth stating.
