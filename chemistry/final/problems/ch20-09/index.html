Worked solution
Answer
(a) E∘=+0.16 VE^\circ=+0.16\,\mathrm{V}E∘=+0.16V, K=2.5×105K=2.5\times10^{5}K=2.5×105; (b) E∘=+0.28 VE^\circ=+0.28\,\mathrm{V}E∘=+0.28V, K=2.9×109K=2.9\times10^{9}K=2.9×109; (c) E∘=+0.44 VE^\circ=+0.44\,\mathrm{V}E∘=+0.44V, K≈2×1074K\approx2\times10^{74}K≈2×1074
  1. Part (a): E°. Ni2+^{2+}2+ is reduced, Fe oxidized: E∘=−0.28−(−0.44)=+0.16 VE^\circ=-0.28-(-0.44)=+0.16\,\mathrm{V}E∘=−0.28−(−0.44)=+0.16V, n=2n=2n=2.
  2. Part (a): K. log⁡K=(2)(0.16)0.0592=5.41\log K=\dfrac{(2)(0.16)}{0.0592}=5.41logK=0.0592(2)(0.16)​=5.41, so K=2.5×105K=2.5\times10^{5}K=2.5×105.
  3. Part (b): E°. H+\mathrm{H^+}H+ is reduced (0.00), Co oxidized (−0.28-0.28−0.28): E∘=0.00−(−0.28)=+0.28 VE^\circ=0.00-(-0.28)=+0.28\,\mathrm{V}E∘=0.00−(−0.28)=+0.28V, n=2n=2n=2.
  4. Part (b): K. log⁡K=(2)(0.28)0.0592=9.46\log K=\dfrac{(2)(0.28)}{0.0592}=9.46logK=0.0592(2)(0.28)​=9.46, so K=2.9×109K=2.9\times10^{9}K=2.9×109.
  5. Part (c): E°. MnO4−\mathrm{MnO_4^-}MnO4−​ is reduced (+1.51+1.51+1.51), Br−\mathrm{Br^-}Br− oxidized (+1.07+1.07+1.07): E∘=1.51−1.07=+0.44 VE^\circ=1.51-1.07=+0.44\,\mathrm{V}E∘=1.51−1.07=+0.44V. Electrons: 2Mn(+7→+2)2\mathrm{Mn(+7\rightarrow+2)}2Mn(+7→+2) gives n=10n=10n=10.
  6. Part (c): K. log⁡K=(10)(0.44)0.0592=74.3\log K=\dfrac{(10)(0.44)}{0.0592}=74.3logK=0.0592(10)(0.44)​=74.3, so K≈2×1074K\approx2\times10^{74}K≈2×1074.
  7. Takeaway. KKK grows exponentially with nE∘nE^\circnE∘: even modest E∘E^\circE∘ with many electrons gives enormous KKK.
