Entropy of vaporization of mercury

수은의 증발 엔트로피

The normal boiling point of mercury (Hg) is 356.7 ∘C356.7\,^\circ\mathrm{C}356.7∘C, and its molar enthalpy of vaporization is ΔHvap=59.11 kJ/mol\Delta H_{\mathrm{vap}}=59.11\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}ΔHvap​=59.11kJ/mol.

(a) When Hg boils at its normal boiling point, does its entropy increase or decrease?

(b) Calculate the value of ΔS\Delta SΔS when 2.00 mol2.00\,\mathrm{mol}2.00mol of Hg is vaporized at 356.7 ∘C356.7\,^\circ\mathrm{C}356.7∘C.
한글
수은(Hg)의 정상 끓는점은 356.7 ∘C356.7\,^\circ\mathrm{C}356.7∘C이고 몰 증발 엔탈피는 ΔHvap=59.11 kJ/mol\Delta H_{\mathrm{vap}}=59.11\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}ΔHvap​=59.11kJ/mol이다.

(a) Hg가 정상 끓는점에서 끓을 때 엔트로피는 증가하는가, 감소하는가?

(b) 356.7 ∘C356.7\,^\circ\mathrm{C}356.7∘C에서 Hg 2.00 mol2.00\,\mathrm{mol}2.00mol이 증발할 때의 ΔS\Delta SΔS를 구하시오.

  2. Convert the temperature to kelvin.

  2. 온도를 켈빈으로 바꾼다.
Worked solution
Answer
(a) increases; (b) ΔS=+188 J/K\Delta S=+188\,\mathrm{J/K}ΔS=+188J/K (1.88×102 J/K1.88\times10^{2}\,\mathrm{J/K}1.88×102J/K)
  1. Part (a): direction. Liquid → gas disperses the atoms into a much larger volume, so entropy increases (ΔS>0\Delta S>0ΔS>0).
  2. Part (b): temperature in kelvin. T=356.7+273.15=629.9 KT=356.7+273.15=629.9\,\mathrm{K}T=356.7+273.15=629.9K.
  3. Part (b): use the phase-change formula. At the boiling point the process is reversible, so ΔS=n ΔHvapT\Delta S=\dfrac{n\,\Delta H_{\mathrm{vap}}}{T}ΔS=TnΔHvap​​.
  4. Part (b): evaluate. ΔS=(2.00 mol)(59110 J/mol)629.9 K=+187.7 J/K\Delta S=\dfrac{(2.00\,\mathrm{mol})(59110\,\mathrm{J/mol})}{629.9\,\mathrm{K}}=+187.7\,\mathrm{J/K}ΔS=629.9K(2.00mol)(59110J/mol)​=+187.7J/K.
  5. Takeaway. A boiling or melting step at its transition temperature has ΔS=nΔH/T\Delta S=n\Delta H/TΔS=nΔH/T; remember to use kelvin and joules.

  2. (b) 켈빈 온도. T=356.7+273.15=629.9 KT=356.7+273.15=629.9\,\mathrm{K}T=356.7+273.15=629.9K.
