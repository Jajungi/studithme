Absorption wavelength of [MoI6]3- from Δ

Δ로부터 [MoI₆]³⁻의 흡수 파장 구하기

The crystal-field splitting Δ\DeltaΔ for [MoI6]3−\mathrm{[MoI_6]^{3-}}[MoI6​]3− is 198.58 kJ/mol198.58\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}198.58kJ/mol. Calculate the wavelength of the ddd–ddd absorption. Is it in the visible range?

(h=6.626×10−34 J sh=6.626\times10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\,s}h=6.626×10−34Js, c=2.998×108 m/sc=2.998\times10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}c=2.998×108m/s, NA=6.022×1023 mol−1N_A=6.022\times10^{23}\,\mathrm{mol^{-1}}NA​=6.022×1023mol−1)
한글
[MoI6]3−\mathrm{[MoI_6]^{3-}}[MoI6​]3−의 결정장 갈라짐 Δ\DeltaΔ는 198.58 kJ/mol198.58\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}198.58kJ/mol이다. ddd–ddd 흡수의 파장을 구하시오. 가시광선 영역에 있는가?

(h=6.626×10−34 J sh=6.626\times10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\,s}h=6.626×10−34Js, c=2.998×108 m/sc=2.998\times10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}c=2.998×108m/s, NA=6.022×1023 mol−1N_A=6.022\times10^{23}\,\mathrm{mol^{-1}}NA​=6.022×1023mol−1)

Hints
  1. Convert kJ/mol to J per ion before using E=hc/λE=hc/\lambdaE=hc/λ.
  2. Visible light spans roughly 400400400–700 nm700\,\mathrm{nm}700nm.
한글 힌트
  1. E=hc/λE=hc/\lambdaE=hc/λ를 쓰기 전에 kJ/mol을 이온 하나당 J로 바꾼다.
  2. 가시광선은 대략 400400400–700 nm700\,\mathrm{nm}700nm이다.
Worked solution
Answer
λ≈602 nm\lambda\approx602\,\mathrm{nm}λ≈602nm (orange), in the visible region
  1. Energy per ion. E=198 580 J/mol6.022×1023 mol−1=3.298×10−19 JE=\dfrac{198\,580\,\mathrm{J/mol}}{6.022\times10^{23}\,\mathrm{mol^{-1}}}=3.298\times10^{-19}\,\mathrm{J}E=6.022×1023mol−1198580J/mol​=3.298×10−19J.
  2. Wavelength. λ=hcE=(6.626×10−34)(2.998×108)3.298×10−19=6.02×10−7 m=602 nm\lambda=\dfrac{hc}{E}=\dfrac{(6.626\times10^{-34})(2.998\times10^{8})}{3.298\times10^{-19}}=6.02\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}=602\,\mathrm{nm}λ=Ehc​=3.298×10−19(6.626×10−34)(2.998×108)​=6.02×10−7m=602nm.
  3. Visible? 602 nm602\,\mathrm{nm}602nm lies within 400400400–700 nm700\,\mathrm{nm}700nm (orange), so yes, it is in the visible range; the complex absorbs orange and appears blue-ish.
  4. Takeaway. λ=NAhc/Δ\lambda=N_Ahc/\Deltaλ=NA​hc/Δ; Δ≈200 kJ/mol\Delta\approx200\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}Δ≈200kJ/mol corresponds to roughly 600 nm600\,\mathrm{nm}600nm.
