Four regions of a weak-acid titration

Hints
  1. Use weak-acid equilibrium initially, Henderson–Hasselbalch at half-equivalence, acetate hydrolysis at equivalence, and excess OH−\mathrm{OH^-}OH− after equivalence.

All acid becomes acetate: [CH3COO−]=0.002500 mol/0.05000 L=0.0500 M[\mathrm{CH_3COO^-}]=0.002500\,\mathrm{mol}/0.05000\,\mathrm{L}=0.0500\,\mathrm{M}[CH3​COO−]=0.002500mol/0.05000L=0.0500M.
