Coffee-cup neutralization calorimetry

커피컵 중화 열량계

In a coffee-cup calorimeter, 50.0 mL50.0\,\mathrm{mL}50.0mL of 1.00 M HCl1.00\,\mathrm{M\ HCl}1.00M HCl is mixed with 50.0 mL50.0\,\mathrm{mL}50.0mL of 1.00 M NaOH1.00\,\mathrm{M\ NaOH}1.00M NaOH. The temperature rises from 21.0∘C21.0^\circ\mathrm{C}21.0∘C to 27.5∘C27.5^\circ\mathrm{C}27.5∘C. Assume a total solution mass of 100.0 g100.0\,\mathrm{g}100.0g, Cs=4.18 J g−1 K−1C_s=4.18\,\mathrm{J\,g^{-1}\,K^{-1}}Cs​=4.18Jg−1K−1, and no heat loss. Find ΔH\Delta HΔH in kJ/mol\mathrm{kJ/mol}kJ/mol of HCl.

Hints

  2. Energy conservation gives qrxn=−qsolnq_{\mathrm{rxn}}=-q_{\mathrm{soln}}qrxn​=−qsoln​.
한글 힌트
  1. 먼저 qsoln=mCsΔTq_{\mathrm{soln}}=mC_s\Delta Tqsoln​=mCs​ΔT를 구한다.
  2. 에너지 보존에 따라 qrxn=−qsolnq_{\mathrm{rxn}}=-q_{\mathrm{soln}}qrxn​=−qsoln​이다.
Worked solution
Answer
ΔH=−54 kJ/mol HCl\Delta H=-54\,\mathrm{kJ/mol\ HCl}ΔH=−54kJ/mol HCl
  1. Start with the measured temperature change. ΔT=27.5−21.0=6.5 K\Delta T=27.5-21.0=6.5\,\mathrm{K}ΔT=27.5−21.0=6.5K. The positive change shows that the solution absorbed heat.
  2. Calculate heat absorbed by the solution. qsoln=(100.0 g)(4.18 J g−1 K−1)(6.5 K)=2.72×103 J=2.72 kJq_{\mathrm{soln}}=(100.0\,\mathrm{g})(4.18\,\mathrm{J\,g^{-1}\,K^{-1}})(6.5\,\mathrm{K})=2.72\times10^3\,\mathrm{J}=2.72\,\mathrm{kJ}qsoln​=(100.0g)(4.18Jg−1K−1)(6.5K)=2.72×103J=2.72kJ.
  3. Assign the reaction sign. With no heat lost, qrxn=−qsoln=−2.72 kJq_{\mathrm{rxn}}=-q_{\mathrm{soln}}=-2.72\,\mathrm{kJ}qrxn​=−qsoln​=−2.72kJ, so neutralization is exothermic.
  4. Find moles reacted. nHCl=(0.0500 L)(1.00 mol/L)=0.0500 moln_{\mathrm{HCl}}=(0.0500\,\mathrm{L})(1.00\,\mathrm{mol/L})=0.0500\,\mathrm{mol}nHCl​=(0.0500L)(1.00mol/L)=0.0500mol. Equal moles of NaOH are present, so all HCl reacts.
  5. Calculate molar enthalpy. ΔH=(−2.72 kJ)/(0.0500 mol)=−54.3 kJ/mol\Delta H=(-2.72\,\mathrm{kJ})/(0.0500\,\mathrm{mol})=-54.3\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}ΔH=(−2.72kJ)/(0.0500mol)=−54.3kJ/mol.
  6. Takeaway. In coffee-cup calorimetry, the solution and reaction have equal-magnitude, opposite-sign heats; always report the reaction sign.

한글 해설
  1. 측정된 온도 변화부터 구한다. ΔT=27.5−21.0=6.5 K\Delta T=27.5-21.0=6.5\,\mathrm{K}ΔT=27.5−21.0=6.5K. 온도가 올랐으므로 용액이 열을 흡수했다.
  2. 용액이 흡수한 열. qsoln=(100.0)(4.18)(6.5)=2.72×103 J=2.72 kJq_{\mathrm{soln}}=(100.0)(4.18)(6.5)=2.72\times10^3\,\mathrm{J}=2.72\,\mathrm{kJ}qsoln​=(100.0)(4.18)(6.5)=2.72×103J=2.72kJ.
  3. 반응열의 부호를 정한다. 열 손실이 없으므로 qrxn=−qsoln=−2.72 kJq_{\mathrm{rxn}}=-q_{\mathrm{soln}}=-2.72\,\mathrm{kJ}qrxn​=−qsoln​=−2.72kJ이고, 중화 반응은 발열이다.
  4. 반응한 mol을 구한다. nHCl=(0.0500 L)(1.00 mol/L)=0.0500 moln_{\mathrm{HCl}}=(0.0500\,\mathrm{L})(1.00\,\mathrm{mol/L})=0.0500\,\mathrm{mol}nHCl​=(0.0500L)(1.00mol/L)=0.0500mol. NaOH도 같은 mol이므로 HCl이 모두 반응한다.
  5. 몰 엔탈피를 계산한다. ΔH=(−2.72 kJ)/(0.0500 mol)=−54.3 kJ/mol\Delta H=(-2.72\,\mathrm{kJ})/(0.0500\,\mathrm{mol})=-54.3\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}ΔH=(−2.72kJ)/(0.0500mol)=−54.3kJ/mol.
  6. 정리. 커피컵 열량계에서는 용액과 반응의 열 크기가 같고 부호가 반대이다.
