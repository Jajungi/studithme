ΔE from heat and work, endo- or exothermic

열과 일로부터 ΔE와 흡열·발열 판정

For the following processes, calculate the change in internal energy of the system and determine whether the process is endothermic or exothermic.

(a) A balloon is cooled by removing 0.655 kJ0.655\,\mathrm{kJ}0.655kJ of heat. It shrinks on cooling, and the atmosphere does 382 J382\,\mathrm{J}382J of work on the balloon.

(b) A 100.0 g100.0\,\mathrm{g}100.0g bar of gold is heated from 25 ∘C25\,^\circ\mathrm{C}25∘C to 50 ∘C50\,^\circ\mathrm{C}50∘C during which it absorbs 322 J322\,\mathrm{J}322J of heat. Assume the volume of the gold bar remains constant.

(a) 풍선에서 0.655 kJ0.655\,\mathrm{kJ}0.655kJ의 열을 빼앗아 냉각한다. 냉각되면서 풍선이 수축하고 대기가 풍선에 382 J382\,\mathrm{J}382J의 일을 한다.

(b) 100.0 g100.0\,\mathrm{g}100.0g의 금 막대를 25 ∘C25\,^\circ\mathrm{C}25∘C에서 50 ∘C50\,^\circ\mathrm{C}50∘C로 가열하는 동안 322 J322\,\mathrm{J}322J의 열을 흡수한다. 금 막대의 부피는 일정하다고 가정한다.

Hints
  1. Heat removed from the system is negative; work done on the system is positive.
  2. Constant volume means w=−PΔV=0w=-P\Delta V=0w=−PΔV=0.
한글 힌트
  1. 계에서 빠져나간 열은 −, 계에 가해진 일은 +이다.
  2. 부피가 일정하면 w=−PΔV=0w=-P\Delta V=0w=−PΔV=0이다.
Worked solution
Answer
(a) ΔE=−273 J\Delta E=-273\,\mathrm{J}ΔE=−273J, exothermic; (b) ΔE=+322 J\Delta E=+322\,\mathrm{J}ΔE=+322J, endothermic
  1. Fix the sign convention. Heat absorbed by the system and work done on the system are positive; heat released and work done by the system are negative.
  2. Part (a): assign qqq and www. Heat is removed, so q=−0.655 kJ=−655 Jq=-0.655\,\mathrm{kJ}=-655\,\mathrm{J}q=−0.655kJ=−655J. The atmosphere does work on the balloon, so w=+382 Jw=+382\,\mathrm{J}w=+382J.
  3. Part (a): apply the first law. ΔE=q+w=−655+382=−273 J\Delta E=q+w=-655+382=-273\,\mathrm{J}ΔE=q+w=−655+382=−273J. Because q<0q<0q<0 the process is exothermic.
  4. Part (b): assign qqq and www. The bar absorbs heat: q=+322 Jq=+322\,\mathrm{J}q=+322J. At constant volume no PΔVP\Delta VPΔV work is done, so w=0w=0w=0.
  5. Part (b): apply the first law. ΔE=q+w=+322 J\Delta E=q+w=+322\,\mathrm{J}ΔE=q+w=+322J. Because q>0q>0q>0 the process is endothermic.
  6. Takeaway. Read qqq and www separately from the wording; the endo/exo label follows the sign of qqq only.
