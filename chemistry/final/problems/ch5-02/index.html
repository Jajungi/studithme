Work for a compression against constant pressure

일정 압력에 맞선 압축에서의 일

How much work (in J) is involved in a chemical reaction if the volume decreases from 33.6 L33.6\,\mathrm{L}33.6L to 11.2 L11.2\,\mathrm{L}11.2L against a constant pressure of 90.5 kPa90.5\,\mathrm{kPa}90.5kPa? State whether the system or the surroundings does the work.
한글
어떤 화학 반응에서 부피가 일정 압력 90.5 kPa90.5\,\mathrm{kPa}90.5kPa에 맞서 33.6 L33.6\,\mathrm{L}33.6L에서 11.2 L11.2\,\mathrm{L}11.2L로 줄었다. 관여한 일(J)을 구하고, 계가 일을 한 것인지 주위가 일을 한 것인지 쓰시오.

한글 힌트

  2. 1 kPa L=1 J1\,\mathrm{kPa\,L}=1\,\mathrm{J}1kPaL=1J이므로 추가 환산이 필요 없다.
Worked solution
Answer
w=+2.03×103 Jw=+2.03\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{J}w=+2.03×103J; the surroundings do work on the system
  1. Start with the volume change, signs included. ΔV=Vf−Vi=11.2−33.6=−22.4 L\Delta V=V_f-V_i=11.2-33.6=-22.4\,\mathrm{L}ΔV=Vf​−Vi​=11.2−33.6=−22.4L. The negative value flags a compression.
  2. Apply the pressure–volume work formula. w=−PextΔV=−(90.5 kPa)(−22.4 L)=+2.03×103 kPa Lw=-P_{\mathrm{ext}}\Delta V=-(90.5\,\mathrm{kPa})(-22.4\,\mathrm{L})=+2.03\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{kPa\,L}w=−Pext​ΔV=−(90.5kPa)(−22.4L)=+2.03×103kPaL.
  3. Convert the unit. Since 1 kPa L=1 J1\,\mathrm{kPa\,L}=1\,\mathrm{J}1kPaL=1J, the work is w=+2.03×103 Jw=+2.03\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{J}w=+2.03×103J. Had the pressure been in atm, the factor 101.325 J/(L atm)101.325\,\mathrm{J/(L\,atm)}101.325J/(Latm) would be required.
  4. Interpret the sign. A positive www means energy entered the system: the surroundings compressed the gas and did work on it.
  5. Takeaway. Get the sign from ΔV\Delta VΔV rather than from the word “compression”, and remember that kPa·L is already joules.
