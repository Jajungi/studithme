Tetrahedral vs square planar, colorless ions and charge-transfer color

정사면체 대 평면 사각형·무색 이온·전하 이동 색

(a) Four-coordinate Ni(II) complexes can be square planar or tetrahedral. [NiCl4]2−[\mathrm{NiCl_4}]^{2-}[NiCl4​]2− is paramagnetic; [Ni(CN)4]2−[\mathrm{Ni(CN)_4}]^{2-}[Ni(CN)4​]2− is diamagnetic. Which geometry does each have?

(b) How many unpaired electrons do you predict for tetrahedral [MnCl4]2−[\mathrm{MnCl_4}]^{2-}[MnCl4​]2−?

(c) For a given metal ion and ligands, is Δ\DeltaΔ larger for tetrahedral or octahedral geometry? Give the approximate ratio.

(d) For which of Ti4+\mathrm{Ti^{4+}}Ti4+, Cr3+\mathrm{Cr^{3+}}Cr3+, Fe2+\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}Fe2+, Co3+\mathrm{Co^{3+}}Co3+, Cu+\mathrm{Cu^+}Cu+, Cu2+\mathrm{Cu^{2+}}Cu2+, Zn2+\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}Zn2+, Ag+\mathrm{Ag^+}Ag+ would you expect colorless octahedral compounds?

(e) MnO4−\mathrm{MnO_4^-}MnO4−​ (Mn(VII), d0d^0d0) is deep purple, but ClO4−\mathrm{ClO_4^-}ClO4−​ is colorless. Explain.

(f) In a square-planar crystal field, for which ddd orbital do the lobes point directly at the ligands?
한글
(a) 4배위 Ni(II) 착물은 평면 사각형이거나 정사면체일 수 있다. [NiCl4]2−[\mathrm{NiCl_4}]^{2-}[NiCl4​]2−는 상자성이고 [Ni(CN)4]2−[\mathrm{Ni(CN)_4}]^{2-}[Ni(CN)4​]2−는 반자성이다. 각각 어떤 구조인가?

(b) 정사면체 [MnCl4]2−[\mathrm{MnCl_4}]^{2-}[MnCl4​]2−의 홀전자 수는 몇 개로 예측되는가?

(c) 같은 금속 이온과 리간드에서 Δ\DeltaΔ는 정사면체와 팔면체 중 어느 쪽이 더 큰가? 대략적인 비를 쓰시오.

(d) Ti4+\mathrm{Ti^{4+}}Ti4+, Cr3+\mathrm{Cr^{3+}}Cr3+, Fe2+\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}Fe2+, Co3+\mathrm{Co^{3+}}Co3+, Cu+\mathrm{Cu^+}Cu+, Cu2+\mathrm{Cu^{2+}}Cu2+, Zn2+\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}Zn2+, Ag+\mathrm{Ag^+}Ag+ 중 무색의 팔면체 화합물을 만들 것으로 예상되는 이온은?

(e) MnO4−\mathrm{MnO_4^-}MnO4−​(Mn(VII), d0d^0d0)는 짙은 보라색이지만 ClO4−\mathrm{ClO_4^-}ClO4−​는 무색이다. 이유를 설명하시오.

(f) 평면 사각형 결정장에서 로브가 리간드를 정면으로 향하는 ddd 오비탈은 무엇인가?

Hints
  1. Ni2+\mathrm{Ni^{2+}}Ni2+ is d8d^8d8: tetrahedral is high spin (paramagnetic); square planar pairs all eight electrons.
  2. No ddd–ddd transition is possible if the ddd subshell is empty (d0d^0d0) or full (d10d^{10}d10).
한글 힌트
  1. Ni2+\mathrm{Ni^{2+}}Ni2+는 d8d^8d8이다. 정사면체는 고스핀(상자성)이고 평면 사각형에서는 여덟 전자가 모두 쌍을 이룬다.
  2. ddd 부껍질이 비어 있거나(d0d^0d0) 가득 차면(d10d^{10}d10) ddd–ddd 전이가 불가능하다.
Worked solution
Answer
(a) [NiCl4]2−[\mathrm{NiCl_4}]^{2-}[NiCl4​]2− tetrahedral, [Ni(CN)4]2−[\mathrm{Ni(CN)_4}]^{2-}[Ni(CN)4​]2− square planar; (b) 5; (c) octahedral, Δtet≈49Δoct\Delta_{\mathrm{tet}}\approx\tfrac49\Delta_{\mathrm{oct}}Δtet​≈94​Δoct​; (d) Ti4+,Cu+,Zn2+,Ag+\mathrm{Ti^{4+},Cu^+,Zn^{2+},Ag^+}Ti4+,Cu+,Zn2+,Ag+; (e) LMCT in MnO4−\mathrm{MnO_4^-}MnO4−​, none in the visible for ClO4−\mathrm{ClO_4^-}ClO4−​; (f) dx2−y2d_{x^2-y^2}dx2−y2​
  1. (a) Geometry from magnetism. Ni2+\mathrm{Ni^{2+}}Ni2+ is [Ar]3d8[\mathrm{Ar}]3d^8[Ar]3d8. Tetrahedral: e4t24e^4t_2^4e4t24​, 2 unpaired → paramagnetic → [NiCl4]2−[\mathrm{NiCl_4}]^{2-}[NiCl4​]2−. Square planar: all eight paired, dx2−y2d_{x^2-y^2}dx2−y2​ empty → diamagnetic → [Ni(CN)4]2−[\mathrm{Ni(CN)_4}]^{2-}[Ni(CN)4​]2−.
  2. (b) [MnCl4]2−[\mathrm{MnCl_4}]^{2-}[MnCl4​]2−. Mn2+\mathrm{Mn^{2+}}Mn2+ is d5d^5d5; tetrahedral complexes are essentially always high spin: e2t23e^2t_2^3e2t23​ → 5 unpaired electrons.
  3. (c) Δ\DeltaΔ comparison. Octahedral is larger: Δtet≈49Δoct\Delta_{\mathrm{tet}}\approx\tfrac49\Delta_{\mathrm{oct}}Δtet​≈94​Δoct​ (fewer ligands and no orbital points directly at them).
  4. (d) Colorless ions. d0d^0d0 or d10d^{10}d10: Ti4+\mathrm{Ti^{4+}}Ti4+ (d0d^0d0), Cu+\mathrm{Cu^+}Cu+, Zn2+\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}Zn2+, Ag+\mathrm{Ag^+}Ag+ (d10d^{10}d10). The others are colored: Cr3+\mathrm{Cr^{3+}}Cr3+ d3d^3d3, Fe2+\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}Fe2+ d6d^6d6, Co3+\mathrm{Co^{3+}}Co3+ d6d^6d6, Cu2+\mathrm{Cu^{2+}}Cu2+ d9d^9d9.
  5. (e) Charge-transfer color. MnO4−\mathrm{MnO_4^-}MnO4−​ has no ddd electrons, so no ddd–ddd band; its color comes from LMCT (an O electron excited into an empty Mn 3d orbital), absorbing about 565 nm (yellow) → violet. In ClO4−\mathrm{ClO_4^-}ClO4−​, Cl(VII) has no low-lying empty orbitals, so absorption is in the UV and the salt is colorless/white. LMCT bands are much more intense than ddd–ddd bands.
  6. (f) Square-planar orbitals. With ligands along ±x\pm x±x and ±y\pm y±y, the lobes of dx2−y2d_{x^2-y^2}dx2−y2​ point directly at them (highest energy); dz2d_{z^2}dz2​ is much lower.
  7. Takeaway. Use magnetism to pick geometry for d8d^8d8, remember Δtet≈49Δoct\Delta_{\mathrm{tet}}\approx\tfrac49\Delta_{\mathrm{oct}}Δtet​≈94​Δoct​, and use LMCT when a d0d^0d0 ion is still colored.
