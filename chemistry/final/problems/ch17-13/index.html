Will a precipitate form? Compare Q with Ksp

침전이 생기는가? Q와 Ksp 비교

(a) Will Ca(OH)2\mathrm{Ca(OH)_2}Ca(OH)2​ precipitate from solution if the pH of a 0.050 M0.050\,\mathrm{M}0.050M solution of CaCl2\mathrm{CaCl_2}CaCl2​ is adjusted to 8.08.08.0?

(b) Will Ag2SO4\mathrm{Ag_2SO_4}Ag2​SO4​ precipitate when 100 mL100\,\mathrm{mL}100mL of 0.050 M0.050\,\mathrm{M}0.050M AgNO3\mathrm{AgNO_3}AgNO3​ is mixed with 10 mL10\,\mathrm{mL}10mL of 5.0×10−2 M5.0\times10^{-2}\,\mathrm{M}5.0×10−2M Na2SO4\mathrm{Na_2SO_4}Na2​SO4​ solution?

(Ksp(Ca(OH)2)=6.5×10−6K_{sp}(\mathrm{Ca(OH)_2})=6.5\times10^{-6}Ksp​(Ca(OH)2​)=6.5×10−6, Ksp(Ag2SO4)=1.5×10−5K_{sp}(\mathrm{Ag_2SO_4})=1.5\times10^{-5}Ksp​(Ag2​SO4​)=1.5×10−5)

Hints
  1. Compute QQQ with the actual (post-mixing) concentrations and compare with KspK_{sp}Ksp​.
  2. In (b) the total volume is 110 mL110\,\mathrm{mL}110mL — dilute both ions.
한글 힌트
  1. 혼합 후의 실제 농도로 QQQ를 구하고 KspK_{sp}Ksp​와 비교한다.
  2. (b)에서는 전체 부피가 110 mL110\,\mathrm{mL}110mL이므로 두 이온 모두 묽어진다.
Worked solution
Answer
(a) No (Q=5.0×10−14<KspQ=5.0\times10^{-14}<K_{sp}Q=5.0×10−14<Ksp​); (b) No (Q=9.4×10−6<1.5×10−5Q=9.4\times10^{-6}<1.5\times10^{-5}Q=9.4×10−6<1.5×10−5)
  1. Part (a): hydroxide concentration. pH=8.0⇒pOH=6.0\mathrm{pH}=8.0\Rightarrow\mathrm{pOH}=6.0pH=8.0⇒pOH=6.0, so [OH−]=1.0×10−6 M[\mathrm{OH^-}]=1.0\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{M}[OH−]=1.0×10−6M.
  2. Part (a): QQQ. Q=[Ca2+][OH−]2=(0.050)(1.0×10−6)2=5.0×10−14Q=[\mathrm{Ca^{2+}}][\mathrm{OH^-}]^2=(0.050)(1.0\times10^{-6})^2=5.0\times10^{-14}Q=[Ca2+][OH−]2=(0.050)(1.0×10−6)2=5.0×10−14.
  3. Part (a): compare. Q=5.0×10−14≪Ksp=6.5×10−6Q=5.0\times10^{-14}\ll K_{sp}=6.5\times10^{-6}Q=5.0×10−14≪Ksp​=6.5×10−6, so no precipitate.
  4. Part (b): concentrations after mixing. Total volume 110 mL110\,\mathrm{mL}110mL. [Ag+]=(100)(0.050)/110=0.0455 M[\mathrm{Ag^+}]=(100)(0.050)/110=0.0455\,\mathrm{M}[Ag+]=(100)(0.050)/110=0.0455M; [SO42−]=(10)(0.050)/110=4.55×10−3 M[\mathrm{SO_4^{2-}}]=(10)(0.050)/110=4.55\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{M}[SO42−​]=(10)(0.050)/110=4.55×10−3M.
  5. Part (b): QQQ. Q=[Ag+]2[SO42−]=(0.0455)2(4.55×10−3)=9.4×10−6Q=[\mathrm{Ag^+}]^2[\mathrm{SO_4^{2-}}]=(0.0455)^2(4.55\times10^{-3})=9.4\times10^{-6}Q=[Ag+]2[SO42−​]=(0.0455)2(4.55×10−3)=9.4×10−6.
  6. Part (b): compare. Q=9.4×10−6<Ksp=1.5×10−5Q=9.4\times10^{-6}<K_{sp}=1.5\times10^{-5}Q=9.4×10−6<Ksp​=1.5×10−5, so no precipitate (the solution is unsaturated).
  7. Takeaway. Always dilute upon mixing, raise each concentration to its stoichiometric power, then compare QQQ with KspK_{sp}Ksp​.
