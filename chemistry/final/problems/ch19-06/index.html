Higher entropy per mole in each pair

각 쌍에서 몰당 엔트로피가 큰 물질

For each pair, predict which substance has the higher entropy per mole at a given temperature:

(a) I2(s)\mathrm{I_2(s)}I2​(s) or I2(g)\mathrm{I_2(g)}I2​(g);

(b) O2(g)\mathrm{O_2(g)}O2​(g) at 50.7 kPa50.7\,\mathrm{kPa}50.7kPa or O2\mathrm{O_2}O2​ at 101.3 kPa101.3\,\mathrm{kPa}101.3kPa;

(c) 1 mol1\,\mathrm{mol}1mol of N2\mathrm{N_2}N2​ in 22.4 L22.4\,\mathrm{L}22.4L or 1 mol1\,\mathrm{mol}1mol of N2\mathrm{N_2}N2​ in 44.8 L44.8\,\mathrm{L}44.8L;

(d) CH3OH(l)\mathrm{CH_3OH(l)}CH3​OH(l) or CH3OH(s)\mathrm{CH_3OH(s)}CH3​OH(s).

Hints
  1. More accessible volume and more freedom of motion mean more microstates.
  2. Gas ≫ liquid > solid; lower pressure means larger volume.
한글 힌트
  1. 접근 가능한 부피와 운동의 자유도가 클수록 미시 상태가 많다.
  2. 기체 ≫ 액체 > 고체이고, 압력이 낮을수록 부피가 크다.
Worked solution
Answer
(a) I2(g)\mathrm{I_2(g)}I2​(g); (b) O2\mathrm{O_2}O2​ at 50.7 kPa50.7\,\mathrm{kPa}50.7kPa; (c) N2\mathrm{N_2}N2​ in 44.8 L44.8\,\mathrm{L}44.8L; (d) CH3OH(l)\mathrm{CH_3OH(l)}CH3​OH(l)
  1. (a) Gas has far more freedom than solid: I2(g)\mathrm{I_2(g)}I2​(g) has the higher entropy.
  2. (b) At the same TTT, lower pressure means larger molar volume: O2\mathrm{O_2}O2​ at 50.7 kPa50.7\,\mathrm{kPa}50.7kPa has the higher entropy.
  3. (c) The larger volume (44.8 L44.8\,\mathrm{L}44.8L) gives more microstates: N2\mathrm{N_2}N2​ in 44.8 L44.8\,\mathrm{L}44.8L has the higher entropy.
  4. (d) Liquid is more disordered than solid: CH3OH(l)\mathrm{CH_3OH(l)}CH3​OH(l) has the higher entropy.
  5. Takeaway. SSS rises with phase freedom (s < l < g) and with available volume (lower pressure).

  2. (b) 같은 TTT에서 압력이 낮으면 몰 부피가 크다. 50.7 kPa50.7\,\mathrm{kPa}50.7kPa의 O2\mathrm{O_2}O2​가 더 크다.
  3. (c) 더 큰 부피(44.8 L44.8\,\mathrm{L}44.8L)가 더 많은 미시 상태를 준다. 44.8 L44.8\,\mathrm{L}44.8L 속 N2\mathrm{N_2}N2​가 더 크다.
