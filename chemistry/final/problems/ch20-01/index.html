Fe/Ag cell: standard emf and effect of concentration changes

Fe/Ag 전지: 표준 기전력과 농도 변화의 효과

Consider the voltaic cell made of a 1 M1\,\mathrm{M}1M Fe2+/Fe\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}/\mathrm{Fe}Fe2+/Fe half-cell and a 1 M1\,\mathrm{M}1M Ag+/Ag\mathrm{Ag^+}/\mathrm{Ag}Ag+/Ag half-cell.

(a) Which electrode is the cathode?

(b) What is the standard emf?

(c) What is the change in voltage when the ion concentrations in the cathode half-cell are increased by a factor of 101010?

(d) What is the change in voltage when the ion concentrations in the anode half-cell are increased by a factor of 101010?

(Ered∘E^\circ_{\mathrm{red}}Ered∘​: Ag+/Ag=+0.80 V\mathrm{Ag^+/Ag}=+0.80\,\mathrm{V}Ag+/Ag=+0.80V, Fe2+/Fe=−0.44 V\mathrm{Fe^{2+}/Fe}=-0.44\,\mathrm{V}Fe2+/Fe=−0.44V; use 0.0592 V0.0592\,\mathrm{V}0.0592V at 298 K298\,\mathrm{K}298K.)

(d) 산화 전극 반쪽 전지의 이온 농도를 101010배로 늘리면 전압은 얼마나 변하는가?

Hints
  1. The half-cell with the larger Ered∘E^\circ_{\mathrm{red}}Ered∘​ is the cathode.
  2. The overall reaction is Fe+2Ag+→Fe2++2Ag\mathrm{Fe+2Ag^+\rightarrow Fe^{2+}+2Ag}Fe+2Ag+→Fe2++2Ag, so Q=[Fe2+]/[Ag+]2Q=[\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}]/[\mathrm{Ag^+}]^2Q=[Fe2+]/[Ag+]2 and n=2n=2n=2.

Worked solution
Answer
(a) Ag; (b) E∘=+1.24 VE^\circ=+1.24\,\mathrm{V}E∘=+1.24V; (c) +0.059 V+0.059\,\mathrm{V}+0.059V; (d) −0.030 V-0.030\,\mathrm{V}−0.030V
  1. Part (a): cathode. Ag+/Ag\mathrm{Ag^+/Ag}Ag+/Ag (+0.80 V+0.80\,\mathrm{V}+0.80V) is more positive than Fe2+/Fe\mathrm{Fe^{2+}/Fe}Fe2+/Fe (−0.44 V-0.44\,\mathrm{V}−0.44V), so the Ag electrode is the cathode and Fe is the anode.
  2. Part (b): standard emf. Ecell∘=Ecath∘−Ean∘=0.80−(−0.44)=+1.24 VE^\circ_{\mathrm{cell}}=E^\circ_{\mathrm{cath}}-E^\circ_{\mathrm{an}}=0.80-(-0.44)=+1.24\,\mathrm{V}Ecell∘​=Ecath∘​−Ean∘​=0.80−(−0.44)=+1.24V.
  3. Set up the Nernst equation. Fe+2Ag+→Fe2++2Ag\mathrm{Fe+2Ag^+\rightarrow Fe^{2+}+2Ag}Fe+2Ag+→Fe2++2Ag gives E=E∘−0.05922log⁡[Fe2+][Ag+]2E=E^\circ-\dfrac{0.0592}{2}\log\dfrac{[\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}]}{[\mathrm{Ag^+}]^2}E=E∘−20.0592​log[Ag+]2[Fe2+]​.
  4. Part (c): cathode [Ag+][\mathrm{Ag^+}][Ag+] ×10. QQQ falls by a factor of 10210^{2}102, so log⁡Q\log QlogQ decreases by 2: ΔE=−0.05922(−2)=+0.059 V\Delta E=-\dfrac{0.0592}{2}(-2)=+0.059\,\mathrm{V}ΔE=−20.0592​(−2)=+0.059V. The voltage increases by about 0.059 V0.059\,\mathrm{V}0.059V.
  5. Part (d): anode [Fe2+][\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}][Fe2+] ×10. QQQ rises by a factor of 101010, so log⁡Q\log QlogQ increases by 1: ΔE=−0.05922(+1)=−0.030 V\Delta E=-\dfrac{0.0592}{2}(+1)=-0.030\,\mathrm{V}ΔE=−20.0592​(+1)=−0.030V. The voltage decreases by about 0.030 V0.030\,\mathrm{V}0.030V.
  6. Takeaway. Raising reactant-side (cathode) concentration raises EEE; raising product-side (anode ion) concentration lowers it, each by (0.0592/n)log⁡(factor)(0.0592/n)\log(\text{factor})(0.0592/n)log(factor) scaled by the stoichiometric power.
