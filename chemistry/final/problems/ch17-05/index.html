AgCl solubility through complex-ion formation

착이온 형성에 의한 AgCl 용해도

Calculate the molar solubility of AgCl\mathrm{AgCl}AgCl in 1.0 M1.0\,\mathrm{M}1.0M NH3\mathrm{NH_3}NH3​. Use Ksp(AgCl)=1.8×10−10K_{sp}(\mathrm{AgCl})=1.8\times10^{-10}Ksp​(AgCl)=1.8×10−10 and Kf[Ag(NH3)2+]=1.7×107K_f[\mathrm{Ag(NH_3)_2^+}]=1.7\times10^7Kf​[Ag(NH3​)2+​]=1.7×107. Compare with its molar solubility in pure water.
한글
1.0 M1.0\,\mathrm{M}1.0M NH3\mathrm{NH_3}NH3​에서 AgCl\mathrm{AgCl}AgCl의 몰 용해도를 구하고 순수한 물에서의 값과 비교하시오. Ksp(AgCl)=1.8×10−10K_{sp}(\mathrm{AgCl})=1.8\times10^{-10}Ksp​(AgCl)=1.8×10−10, Kf[Ag(NH3)2+]=1.7×107K_f[\mathrm{Ag(NH_3)_2^+}]=1.7\times10^7Kf​[Ag(NH3​)2+​]=1.7×107.

Hints
  1. Add the dissolution and complex-formation equilibria; multiply their constants.
  2. Overall: AgCl(s)+2NH3(aq)⇌Ag(NH3)2+(aq)+Cl−(aq)\mathrm{AgCl(s)+2NH_3(aq)\rightleftharpoons Ag(NH_3)_2^+(aq)+Cl^-(aq)}AgCl(s)+2NH3​(aq)⇌Ag(NH3​)2+​(aq)+Cl−(aq).
한글 힌트
  1. 용해 평형과 착이온 형성 평형을 더하고 두 평형 상수를 곱한다.
  2. 전체 반응은 AgCl(s)+2NH3(aq)⇌Ag(NH3)2+(aq)+Cl−(aq)\mathrm{AgCl(s)+2NH_3(aq)\rightleftharpoons Ag(NH_3)_2^+(aq)+Cl^-(aq)}AgCl(s)+2NH3​(aq)⇌Ag(NH3​)2+​(aq)+Cl−(aq)이다.
Worked solution
Answer
s≈0.050 Ms\approx0.050\,\mathrm{M}s≈0.050M in 1.0 M NH31.0\,\mathrm{M\ NH_3}1.0M NH3​ vs 1.3×10−5 M1.3\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm{M}1.3×10−5M in water
  1. Start by coupling the equilibria. Adding AgCl⇌Ag++Cl−\mathrm{AgCl\rightleftharpoons Ag^++Cl^-}AgCl⇌Ag++Cl− and Ag++2NH3⇌Ag(NH3)2+\mathrm{Ag^++2NH_3\rightleftharpoons Ag(NH_3)_2^+}Ag++2NH3​⇌Ag(NH3​)2+​ cancels free Ag+\mathrm{Ag^+}Ag+.
  2. Find the overall equilibrium constant. K=KspKf=(1.8×10−10)(1.7×107)=3.06×10−3K=K_{sp}K_f=(1.8\times10^{-10})(1.7\times10^7)=3.06\times10^{-3}K=Ksp​Kf​=(1.8×10−10)(1.7×107)=3.06×10−3.
  3. Set up the overall equilibrium. If xxx mol/L dissolves, [Ag(NH3)2+]=[Cl−]=x[\mathrm{Ag(NH_3)_2^+}]=[\mathrm{Cl^-}]=x[Ag(NH3​)2+​]=[Cl−]=x and [NH3]=1.0−2x[\mathrm{NH_3}]=1.0-2x[NH3​]=1.0−2x. Thus K=x2/(1.0−2x)2K=x^2/(1.0-2x)^2K=x2/(1.0−2x)2.
  4. Solve without discarding ligand consumption. x/(1.0−2x)=3.06×10−3=0.0553x/(1.0-2x)=\sqrt{3.06\times10^{-3}}=0.0553x/(1.0−2x)=3.06×10−3​=0.0553, giving x=0.0498 M≈0.050 Mx=0.0498\,\mathrm{M}\approx0.050\,\mathrm{M}x=0.0498M≈0.050M.
  5. Compare with pure water. There, s=Ksp=1.34×10−5 Ms=\sqrt{K_{sp}}=1.34\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm{M}s=Ksp​​=1.34×10−5M; ammonia raises solubility by about 3.7×1033.7\times10^33.7×103.
  6. Takeaway. A ligand increases solubility by removing free metal ion; when equilibria are added, their equilibrium constants multiply.
