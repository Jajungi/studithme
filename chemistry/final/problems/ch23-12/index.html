Formula and structure of a platinum complex from composition

조성으로부터 백금 착물의 화학식과 구조 결정

A platinum complex of Cl−\mathrm{Cl^-}Cl− and NH3\mathrm{NH_3}NH3​ contains 23.63%23.63\%23.63% Cl, 9.34%9.34\%9.34% N and 2.02%2.02\%2.02% H by mass. Its solutions do not conduct electricity and it has a dipole moment. Determine the formula and structure.

(MMM: Pt 195.08, Cl 35.45, N 14.01, H 1.008 g/mol)
한글
Cl−\mathrm{Cl^-}Cl−와 NH3\mathrm{NH_3}NH3​를 가진 백금 착물이 질량 비율로 Cl 23.63%23.63\%23.63%, N 9.34%9.34\%9.34%, H 2.02%2.02\%2.02%를 포함한다. 이 용액은 전기를 통하지 않고 쌍극자 모멘트가 있다. 화학식과 구조를 결정하시오.

(MMM: Pt 195.08, Cl 35.45, N 14.01, H 1.008 g/mol)

Hints
  1. Platinum is the remainder: 100−23.63−9.34−2.02100-23.63-9.34-2.02100−23.63−9.34−2.02.
  2. Non-conducting means no free ions (all Cl−^-− are inside the coordination sphere); a dipole moment rules out the symmetric trans isomer.
한글 힌트
  1. 백금은 나머지이다: 100−23.63−9.34−2.02100-23.63-9.34-2.02100−23.63−9.34−2.02.
  2. 전도성이 없으면 자유 이온이 없다는 뜻이다(모든 Cl−^-−가 배위권 안). 쌍극자 모멘트가 있으면 대칭인 trans 이성질체는 제외된다.
Worked solution
Answer
[Pt(NH3)2Cl2]\mathrm{[Pt(NH_3)_2Cl_2]}[Pt(NH3​)2​Cl2​], square planar, cis (cisplatin)
  1. Mass percent of Pt. 100−23.63−9.34−2.02=65.01%100-23.63-9.34-2.02=65.01\%100−23.63−9.34−2.02=65.01% Pt.
  2. Moles per 100 g. Pt: 65.01/195.08=0.333365.01/195.08=0.333365.01/195.08=0.3333; Cl: 23.63/35.45=0.666623.63/35.45=0.666623.63/35.45=0.6666; N: 9.34/14.01=0.66679.34/14.01=0.66679.34/14.01=0.6667; H: 2.02/1.008=2.0042.02/1.008=2.0042.02/1.008=2.004.
  3. Simplest ratio. Dividing by 0.33330.33330.3333: Pt : Cl : N : H =1:2:2:6=1:2:2:6=1:2:2:6, i.e. Pt(NH3)2Cl2\mathrm{Pt(NH_3)_2Cl_2}Pt(NH3​)2​Cl2​ (the 6 H are 2×32\times32×3 H of NH3\mathrm{NH_3}NH3​).
  4. Conductivity. Solutions do not conduct, so the compound is neutral and no Cl−^-− is outside the complex: both Cl−\mathrm{Cl^-}Cl− and both NH3\mathrm{NH_3}NH3​ are bound to Pt, giving [Pt(NH3)2Cl2]\mathrm{[Pt(NH_3)_2Cl_2]}[Pt(NH3​)2​Cl2​].
  5. Structure. Pt(II) (d8d^8d8) is square planar. A dipole moment requires the cis isomer; trans would be nonpolar.
  6. Takeaway. Mass% → mole ratio → formula; conductivity says all ligands are inside the sphere; polarity picks cis over trans.

  2. 100 g당 mol 수. Pt: 65.01/195.08=0.333365.01/195.08=0.333365.01/195.08=0.3333, Cl: 23.63/35.45=0.666623.63/35.45=0.666623.63/35.45=0.6666, N: 9.34/14.01=0.66679.34/14.01=0.66679.34/14.01=0.6667, H: 2.02/1.008=2.0042.02/1.008=2.0042.02/1.008=2.004.
