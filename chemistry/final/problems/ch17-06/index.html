Hints
  1. Calculate the ion concentrations after mixing and before any precipitation.
  2. For AgCl\mathrm{AgCl}AgCl, compare Q=[Ag+][Cl−]Q=[\mathrm{Ag^+}][\mathrm{Cl^-}]Q=[Ag+][Cl−] with KspK_{sp}Ksp​.
한글 힌트
  1. 침전이 생기기 전, 혼합으로 희석된 이온 농도를 계산한다.
  2. AgCl\mathrm{AgCl}AgCl에 대해 Q=[Ag+][Cl−]Q=[\mathrm{Ag^+}][\mathrm{Cl^-}]Q=[Ag+][Cl−]를 KspK_{sp}Ksp​와 비교한다.
Worked solution
Answer
Q=5.00×10−5>KspQ=5.00\times10^{-5}>K_{sp}Q=5.00×10−5>Ksp​; AgCl\mathrm{AgCl}AgCl precipitates
  1. Start with dilution on mixing. The total volume is 50.0+50.0=100.0 mL50.0+50.0=100.0\,\mathrm{mL}50.0+50.0=100.0mL, so each original solution is diluted by a factor of two before precipitation is considered.
  2. Find the trial ion concentrations. [Ag+]0=(0.0200)(50.0/100.0)=0.0100 M[\mathrm{Ag^+}]_0=(0.0200)(50.0/100.0)=0.0100\,\mathrm{M}[Ag+]0​=(0.0200)(50.0/100.0)=0.0100M and [Cl−]0=(0.0100)(50.0/100.0)=0.00500 M[\mathrm{Cl^-}]_0=(0.0100)(50.0/100.0)=0.00500\,\mathrm{M}[Cl−]0​=(0.0100)(50.0/100.0)=0.00500M.
  3. Calculate the reaction quotient. Q=[Ag+]0[Cl−]0=(0.0100)(0.00500)=5.00×10−5Q=[\mathrm{Ag^+}]_0[\mathrm{Cl^-}]_0=(0.0100)(0.00500)=5.00\times10^{-5}Q=[Ag+]0​[Cl−]0​=(0.0100)(0.00500)=5.00×10−5.
  4. Compare with the solubility product. Q=5.00×10−5≫Ksp=1.8×10−10Q=5.00\times10^{-5}\gg K_{sp}=1.8\times10^{-10}Q=5.00×10−5≫Ksp​=1.8×10−10, so the mixed solution is supersaturated.
  5. Predict the change. AgCl(s)\mathrm{AgCl(s)}AgCl(s) precipitates, consuming Ag+\mathrm{Ag^+}Ag+ and Cl−\mathrm{Cl^-}Cl− until the remaining dissolved ions satisfy Q=KspQ=K_{sp}Q=Ksp​.
  6. Takeaway. Use concentrations immediately after mixing for the trial QQQ: Q>KspQ>K_{sp}Q>Ksp​ means precipitation, Q=KspQ=K_{sp}Q=Ksp​ means saturation, and Q<KspQ<K_{sp}Q<Ksp​ means no precipitation.

한글 해설
  1. 혼합에 따른 희석부터 계산한다. 총부피는 50.0+50.0=100.0 mL50.0+50.0=100.0\,\mathrm{mL}50.0+50.0=100.0mL이므로 침전을 고려하기 전 각 용액은 2배 희석된다.
  2. 시험 이온 농도를 구한다. [Ag+]0=(0.0200)(50.0/100.0)=0.0100 M[\mathrm{Ag^+}]_0=(0.0200)(50.0/100.0)=0.0100\,\mathrm{M}[Ag+]0​=(0.0200)(50.0/100.0)=0.0100M, [Cl−]0=(0.0100)(50.0/100.0)=0.00500 M[\mathrm{Cl^-}]_0=(0.0100)(50.0/100.0)=0.00500\,\mathrm{M}[Cl−]0​=(0.0100)(50.0/100.0)=0.00500M.
  3. 반응 지수를 계산한다. Q=[Ag+]0[Cl−]0=(0.0100)(0.00500)=5.00×10−5Q=[\mathrm{Ag^+}]_0[\mathrm{Cl^-}]_0=(0.0100)(0.00500)=5.00\times10^{-5}Q=[Ag+]0​[Cl−]0​=(0.0100)(0.00500)=5.00×10−5.
  4. 용해도곱과 비교한다. Q=5.00×10−5≫Ksp=1.8×10−10Q=5.00\times10^{-5}\gg K_{sp}=1.8\times10^{-10}Q=5.00×10−5≫Ksp​=1.8×10−10이므로 혼합 용액은 과포화 상태이다.
  5. 변화를 예측한다. 남은 용액의 이온들이 Q=KspQ=K_{sp}Q=Ksp​를 만족할 때까지 Ag+\mathrm{Ag^+}Ag+와 Cl−\mathrm{Cl^-}Cl−가 소비되며 AgCl(s)\mathrm{AgCl(s)}AgCl(s)가 침전한다.
