Estimating reaction enthalpy from bonds

Estimate ΔH\Delta HΔH for the gas-phase substitution
CH4(g)+Cl2(g)→CH3Cl(g)+HCl(g)\mathrm{CH_4(g)+Cl_2(g)\rightarrow CH_3Cl(g)+HCl(g)}CH4​(g)+Cl2​(g)→CH3​Cl(g)+HCl(g)
using average bond enthalpies (kJ/mol): D(C−H)=413D(\mathrm{C-H})=413D(C−H)=413, D(Cl−Cl)=242D(\mathrm{Cl-Cl})=242D(Cl−Cl)=242, D(C−Cl)=328D(\mathrm{C-Cl})=328D(C−Cl)=328, and D(H−Cl)=431D(\mathrm{H-Cl})=431D(H−Cl)=431.
Hints
  1. ΔH≈∑D(bonds broken)−∑D(bonds formed)\Delta H\approx\sum D(\text{bonds broken})-\sum D(\text{bonds formed})ΔH≈∑D(bonds broken)−∑D(bonds formed).
  2. Only one C–H bond is replaced; unchanged bonds cancel.
Worked solution
  1. Start by identifying changed bonds. Break one C−H\mathrm{C-H}C−H bond and one Cl−Cl\mathrm{Cl-Cl}Cl−Cl bond; form one C−Cl\mathrm{C-Cl}C−Cl bond and one H−Cl\mathrm{H-Cl}H−Cl bond.
  2. Energy required to break bonds. 413+242=655 kJ/mol413+242=655\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}413+242=655kJ/mol. Bond breaking is assigned a positive energy cost.
  3. Energy associated with forming bonds. 328+431=759 kJ/mol328+431=759\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}328+431=759kJ/mol is released when the product bonds form.
  4. Calculate the estimate. ΔH≈655−759=−104 kJ/mol\Delta H\approx655-759=-104\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}ΔH≈655−759=−104kJ/mol.
  5. Interpret. The newly formed bonds are stronger in total than those broken, so the reaction is exothermic.
  6. Takeaway. Average bond enthalpies give a gas-phase estimate; use broken minus formed and count only bonds that change.
