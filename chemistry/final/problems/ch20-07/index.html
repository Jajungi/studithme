ΔG° and K for the Al–Pb cell

Al–Pb 전지의 ΔG°와 K

Calculate Ecell∘E^\circ_{\mathrm{cell}}Ecell∘​, ΔG∘\Delta G^\circΔG∘, and KKK at 298 K298\,\mathrm{K}298K for the cell

Al(s) ∣ Al3+(aq) ∥ Pb2+(aq) ∣ Pb(s)\mathrm{Al(s)\,|\,Al^{3+}(aq)\,\|\,Pb^{2+}(aq)\,|\,Pb(s)}Al(s)∣Al3+(aq)∥Pb2+(aq)∣Pb(s)

given Ered∘(Al3+/Al)=−1.66 VE^\circ_{\mathrm{red}}(\mathrm{Al^{3+}/Al})=-1.66\,\mathrm{V}Ered∘​(Al3+/Al)=−1.66V and Ered∘(Pb2+/Pb)=−0.13 VE^\circ_{\mathrm{red}}(\mathrm{Pb^{2+}/Pb})=-0.13\,\mathrm{V}Ered∘​(Pb2+/Pb)=−0.13V. Use F=96,485 C/molF=96{,}485\,\mathrm{C/mol}F=96,485C/mol.

Al(s) ∣ Al3+(aq) ∥ Pb2+(aq) ∣ Pb(s)\mathrm{Al(s)\,|\,Al^{3+}(aq)\,\|\,Pb^{2+}(aq)\,|\,Pb(s)}Al(s)∣Al3+(aq)∥Pb2+(aq)∣Pb(s)

Hints
  1. Balance the electrons first to find nnn; the smallest common multiple of 3 and 2 is 6.
  2. ΔG∘=−nFE∘\Delta G^\circ=-nFE^\circΔG∘=−nFE∘ gives J/mol; then use log⁡K=nE∘/0.0592\log K=nE^\circ/0.0592logK=nE∘/0.0592 at 298 K298\,\mathrm{K}298K.
한글 힌트
  1. 먼저 전자를 맞춰 nnn을 구한다. 3과 2의 최소공배수는 6이다.
  2. ΔG∘=−nFE∘\Delta G^\circ=-nFE^\circΔG∘=−nFE∘로 J/mol을 얻고, 298 K298\,\mathrm{K}298K에서 log⁡K=nE∘/0.0592\log K=nE^\circ/0.0592logK=nE∘/0.0592를 쓴다.
Worked solution
Answer
Ecell∘=+1.53 VE^\circ_{\mathrm{cell}}=+1.53\,\mathrm{V}Ecell∘​=+1.53V; ΔG∘≈−886 kJ/mol\Delta G^\circ\approx-886\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}ΔG∘≈−886kJ/mol; K≈1×10155K\approx1\times10^{155}K≈1×10155
  1. Write the half-reactions. Anode: 2Al→2Al3++6e−\mathrm{2Al\rightarrow2Al^{3+}+6e^-}2Al→2Al3++6e−. Cathode: 3Pb2++6e−→3Pb\mathrm{3Pb^{2+}+6e^-\rightarrow3Pb}3Pb2++6e−→3Pb. Overall: 2Al+3Pb2+→2Al3++3Pb\mathrm{2Al+3Pb^{2+}\rightarrow2Al^{3+}+3Pb}2Al+3Pb2+→2Al3++3Pb, so n=6n=6n=6.
  2. Standard cell potential. Ecell∘=Ecathode∘−Eanode∘=(−0.13)−(−1.66)=+1.53 VE^\circ_{\mathrm{cell}}=E^\circ_{\mathrm{cathode}}-E^\circ_{\mathrm{anode}}=(-0.13)-(-1.66)=+1.53\,\mathrm{V}Ecell∘​=Ecathode∘​−Eanode∘​=(−0.13)−(−1.66)=+1.53V.
  3. Free energy. ΔG∘=−nFE∘=−(6)(96,485 C/mol)(1.53 V)=−8.86×105 J/mol=−886 kJ/mol\Delta G^\circ=-nFE^\circ=-(6)(96{,}485\,\mathrm{C/mol})(1.53\,\mathrm{V})=-8.86\times10^{5}\,\mathrm{J/mol}=-886\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}ΔG∘=−nFE∘=−(6)(96,485C/mol)(1.53V)=−8.86×105J/mol=−886kJ/mol (using 1 C⋅V=1 J1\,\mathrm{C\cdot V}=1\,\mathrm{J}1C⋅V=1J).
  4. Equilibrium constant. log⁡K=nE∘/0.0592=(6)(1.53)/0.0592=155\log K=nE^\circ/0.0592=(6)(1.53)/0.0592=155logK=nE∘/0.0592=(6)(1.53)/0.0592=155, so K≈1×10155K\approx1\times10^{155}K≈1×10155.
  5. Cross-check. ln⁡K=−ΔG∘/RT=8.86×105/[(8.314)(298)]=357.6\ln K=-\Delta G^\circ/RT=8.86\times10^{5}/[(8.314)(298)]=357.6lnK=−ΔG∘/RT=8.86×105/[(8.314)(298)]=357.6, and 357.6/2.303=155.3357.6/2.303=155.3357.6/2.303=155.3 — consistent with the log result.
  6. Interpret. E∘>0E^\circ>0E∘>0, ΔG∘<0\Delta G^\circ<0ΔG∘<0, K≫1K\gg1K≫1: reactants are converted almost completely to products at equilibrium.
  7. Takeaway. Find nnn from the balanced equation, then chain E∘→ΔG∘=−nFE∘→KE^\circ\rightarrow\Delta G^\circ=-nFE^\circ\rightarrow KE∘→ΔG∘=−nFE∘→K; the three signs (E∘>0E^\circ>0E∘>0, ΔG∘<0\Delta G^\circ<0ΔG∘<0, K>1K>1K>1) always agree.
