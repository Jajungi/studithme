Naming coordination compounds and writing formulas

배위 화합물의 명명과 화학식 쓰기

(a) Name [Cr(OH2)4Cl2]Cl[\mathrm{Cr(OH_2)_4Cl_2}]\mathrm{Cl}[Cr(OH2​)4​Cl2​]Cl, K4[Ni(CN)4]\mathrm{K_4[Ni(CN)_4]}K4​[Ni(CN)4​] and [Rh(NH3)4Cl2]Cl[\mathrm{Rh(NH_3)_4Cl_2}]\mathrm{Cl}[Rh(NH3​)4​Cl2​]Cl.

(b) Write formulas for: (i) triamminetriaquachromium(III) nitrate (ii) dichloridobis(ethylenediamine)platinum(IV) bromide (iii) pentacarbonyliron(0) (iv) ammonium diaquabis(oxalato)cobaltate(II) (v) tris(bipyridyl)cobalt(III) sulfate

(c) A compound PtCl4⋅2KCl\mathrm{PtCl_4\cdot2KCl}PtCl4​⋅2KCl gives three ions per formula unit in water, and AgNO3\mathrm{AgNO_3}AgNO3​ gives no precipitate. What is its formula, and what is its name?

(a) [Cr(OH2)4Cl2]Cl[\mathrm{Cr(OH_2)_4Cl_2}]\mathrm{Cl}[Cr(OH2​)4​Cl2​]Cl, K4[Ni(CN)4]\mathrm{K_4[Ni(CN)_4]}K4​[Ni(CN)4​], [Rh(NH3)4Cl2]Cl[\mathrm{Rh(NH_3)_4Cl_2}]\mathrm{Cl}[Rh(NH3​)4​Cl2​]Cl의 이름을 쓰시오.

(c) 화합물 PtCl4⋅2KCl\mathrm{PtCl_4\cdot2KCl}PtCl4​⋅2KCl는 물에서 화학식 단위당 이온 3개를 내고 AgNO3\mathrm{AgNO_3}AgNO3​로 침전이 생기지 않는다. 화학식과 이름을 쓰시오.

Hints
  1. Name the cation first; inside the complex, list ligands alphabetically (ignore di-, tri-, bis-, tris-), then the metal with its Roman-numeral oxidation number.
  2. If the complex is an anion, the metal name ends in -ate.
한글 힌트
  1. 양이온을 먼저 읽고, 착물 안에서는 리간드를 알파벳 순으로(di-, tri-, bis-, tris-는 무시) 쓴 뒤 금속 이름과 로마 숫자 산화수를 쓴다.
  2. 착이온이 음이온이면 금속 이름이 -ate로 끝난다.

  2. (a) K4[Ni(CN)4]\mathrm{K_4[Ni(CN)_4]}K4​[Ni(CN)4​]. Complex 4−4-4−; x+4(−1)=−4x+4(-1)=-4x+4(−1)=−4 → x=0x=0x=0. Anionic complex → nickelate: potassium tetracyanidonickelate(0).
  3. (a) [Rh(NH3)4Cl2]Cl[\mathrm{Rh(NH_3)_4Cl_2}]\mathrm{Cl}[Rh(NH3​)4​Cl2​]Cl. Complex 1+1+1+, x−2=+1x-2=+1x−2=+1 → +3+3+3. Alphabetical: ammine, chlorido → tetraamminedichloridorhodium(III) chloride.
  4. (b) Build each formula. (i) [Cr(NH3)3(H2O)3](NO3)3[\mathrm{Cr(NH_3)_3(H_2O)_3}](\mathrm{NO_3})_3[Cr(NH3​)3​(H2​O)3​](NO3​)3​ (Cr3+\mathrm{Cr^{3+}}Cr3+, neutral ligands → 3+3+3+).   5. (c) PtCl4⋅2KCl\mathrm{PtCl_4\cdot2KCl}PtCl4​⋅2KCl. Three ions (2 K+2\,\mathrm{K^+}2K+ + one complex) and no AgCl\mathrm{AgCl}AgCl means all six Cl−\mathrm{Cl^-}Cl− are bonded to Pt\mathrm{Pt}Pt: K2[PtCl6]\mathrm{K_2[PtCl_6]}K2​[PtCl6​].   7. Takeaway. Order: cation → ligands (alphabetical) → metal (-ate if anionic) → oxidation number.
