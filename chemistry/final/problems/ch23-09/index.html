Unpaired electrons in octahedral complexes and the d⁴/d⁷ rule

팔면체 착물의 홀전자 수와 d⁴/d⁷ 규칙

(a) Find the metal ion’s dnd^ndn configuration, the spin state (high or low), and the number of unpaired electrons in each octahedral complex: (i) [Cr(OH2)6]3+[\mathrm{Cr(OH_2)_6}]^{3+}[Cr(OH2​)6​]3+ (ii) [V(H2O)6]3+[\mathrm{V(H_2O)_6}]^{3+}[V(H2​O)6​]3+ (iii) [FeF6]3−[\mathrm{FeF_6}]^{3-}[FeF6​]3− (iv) [RhCl6]3−[\mathrm{RhCl_6}]^{3-}[RhCl6​]3− (v) [Ni(NH3)6]2+[\mathrm{Ni(NH_3)_6}]^{2+}[Ni(NH3​)6​]2+. Which has the fewest unpaired electrons?

(b) Classify as diamagnetic or paramagnetic: [Co(NH3)5Br]2+[\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_5Br}]^{2+}[Co(NH3​)5​Br]2+, [W(CN)6]3−[\mathrm{W(CN)_6}]^{3-}[W(CN)6​]3−, [VF6]3−[\mathrm{VF_6}]^{3-}[VF6​]3−, [Rh(o-phen)3]3+[\mathrm{Rh(o\text{-}phen)_3}]^{3+}[Rh(o-phen)3​]3+.

(c) For which dnd^ndn ions does the high-spin octahedral complex have exactly two more unpaired electrons than the low-spin one? Give examples.
한글
(a) 각 팔면체 착물에서 금속 이온의 dnd^ndn 배치, 스핀 상태(고스핀/저스핀), 홀전자 수를 구하시오: (i) [Cr(OH2)6]3+[\mathrm{Cr(OH_2)_6}]^{3+}[Cr(OH2​)6​]3+ (ii) [V(H2O)6]3+[\mathrm{V(H_2O)_6}]^{3+}[V(H2​O)6​]3+ (iii) [FeF6]3−[\mathrm{FeF_6}]^{3-}[FeF6​]3− (iv) [RhCl6]3−[\mathrm{RhCl_6}]^{3-}[RhCl6​]3− (v) [Ni(NH3)6]2+[\mathrm{Ni(NH_3)_6}]^{2+}[Ni(NH3​)6​]2+. 홀전자가 가장 적은 것은?

(b) 반자성인지 상자성인지 분류하시오: [Co(NH3)5Br]2+[\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_5Br}]^{2+}[Co(NH3​)5​Br]2+, [W(CN)6]3−[\mathrm{W(CN)_6}]^{3-}[W(CN)6​]3−, [VF6]3−[\mathrm{VF_6}]^{3-}[VF6​]3−, [Rh(o-phen)3]3+[\mathrm{Rh(o\text{-}phen)_3}]^{3+}[Rh(o-phen)3​]3+.

(c) 팔면체 착물에서 고스핀이 저스핀보다 홀전자가 정확히 2개 더 많은 dnd^ndn 이온은 무엇인가? 예를 드시오.

Hints
  1. Charges: dn=d^n=dn= group number −-− oxidation state. High/low spin only matters for d4d^4d4–d7d^7d7.
  2. Period 5 and 6 ions have large Δ\DeltaΔ and usually form low-spin octahedral complexes.
한글 힌트
  1. dn=d^n=dn= 족 번호 −-− 산화수이다. 고스핀/저스핀 구분은 d4d^4d4–d7d^7d7에서만 의미가 있다.
  2. 5·6주기 이온은 Δ\DeltaΔ가 커서 보통 저스핀 팔면체 착물을 만든다.
Worked solution
Answer
(a) 3, 2, 5 (high spin), 0 (low spin), 2; fewest = [RhCl6]3−[\mathrm{RhCl_6}]^{3-}[RhCl6​]3−; (b) diamagnetic, paramagnetic, paramagnetic, diamagnetic; (c) d4d^4d4 (Cr2+,Mn3+\mathrm{Cr^{2+},Mn^{3+}}Cr2+,Mn3+) and d7d^7d7 (Co2+\mathrm{Co^{2+}}Co2+)
  1. (a) Count ddd electrons. (i) Cr3+\mathrm{Cr^{3+}}Cr3+ d3d^3d3 (ii) V3+\mathrm{V^{3+}}V3+ d2d^2d2 (iii) Fe3+\mathrm{Fe^{3+}}Fe3+ d5d^5d5 (iv) Rh3+\mathrm{Rh^{3+}}Rh3+ d6d^6d6 (v) Ni2+\mathrm{Ni^{2+}}Ni2+ d8d^8d8.
  2. (a) Decide spin and fill. (i) t23t_2^3t23​, 3 unpaired (no choice for d3d^3d3). (ii) t22t_2^2t22​, 2. (iii) F−\mathrm{F^-}F− weak-field → high spin t23e2t_2^3e^2t23​e2, 5. (iv) Rh3+\mathrm{Rh^{3+}}Rh3+ is 4d4d4d → large Δ\DeltaΔ → low spin t26t_2^6t26​, 0. (v) d8d^8d8: t26e2t_2^6e^2t26​e2, 2.
  3. (a) Fewest. [RhCl6]3−[\mathrm{RhCl_6}]^{3-}[RhCl6​]3− with 0 unpaired electrons.
  4. (b) Magnetism. [Co(NH3)5Br]2+[\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_5Br}]^{2+}[Co(NH3​)5​Br]2+: Co(III) d6d^6d6 low spin → diamagnetic. [W(CN)6]3−[\mathrm{W(CN)_6}]^{3-}[W(CN)6​]3−: W(III) d3d^3d3 → paramagnetic. [VF6]3−[\mathrm{VF_6}]^{3-}[VF6​]3−: V(III) d2d^2d2 → paramagnetic. [Rh(o-phen)3]3+[\mathrm{Rh(o\text{-}phen)_3}]^{3+}[Rh(o-phen)3​]3+: Rh(III) d6d^6d6, strong-field o-phen, 4d → low spin → diamagnetic.
  5. (c) Tabulate the ambiguous cases. d4d^4d4: 4 (HS) vs 2 (LS) → difference 2. d5d^5d5: 5 vs 1 → 4. d6d^6d6: 4 vs 0 → 4. d7d^7d7: 3 vs 1 → 2.
  6. (c) Examples. d4d^4d4 (e.g. Cr2+\mathrm{Cr^{2+}}Cr2+, Mn3+\mathrm{Mn^{3+}}Mn3+) and d7d^7d7 (e.g. Co2+\mathrm{Co^{2+}}Co2+).
  7. Takeaway. Count dnd^ndn, ask whether the spin state is even a choice (d4d^4d4–d7d^7d7), then fill t2t_2t2​ before eee and pair only when Δ>P\Delta>PΔ>P.
