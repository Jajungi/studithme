Haber ΔG at elevated temperature

고온에서 Haber의 ΔG

For the Haber process N2(g)+3H2(g)→2NH3(g)\mathrm{N_2(g)+3H_2(g)\rightarrow 2NH_3(g)}N2​(g)+3H2​(g)→2NH3​(g), ΔH∘=−92.38 kJ\Delta H^\circ=-92.38\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔH∘=−92.38kJ and ΔS∘=−198.3 J K−1\Delta S^\circ=-198.3\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}ΔS∘=−198.3JK−1. Estimate ΔG\Delta GΔG at 500 ∘C500\,^\circ\mathrm{C}500∘C, assuming ΔH∘\Delta H^\circΔH∘ and ΔS∘\Delta S^\circΔS∘ do not change with TTT. Compare with ΔG∘=−33.3 kJ\Delta G^\circ=-33.3\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔG∘=−33.3kJ at 298 K298\,\mathrm{K}298K. Below what temperature is the reaction spontaneous under standard conditions?

Haber 공정 N2(g)+3H2(g)→2NH3(g)\mathrm{N_2(g)+3H_2(g)\rightarrow 2NH_3(g)}N2​(g)+3H2​(g)→2NH3​(g)의 ΔH∘=−92.38 kJ\Delta H^\circ=-92.38\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔH∘=−92.38kJ, ΔS∘=−198.3 J K−1\Delta S^\circ=-198.3\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}ΔS∘=−198.3JK−1이다. ΔH∘\Delta H^\circΔH∘와 ΔS∘\Delta S^\circΔS∘가 온도에 무관하다고 가정하고 500 ∘C500\,^\circ\mathrm{C}500∘C에서 ΔG\Delta GΔG를 추정하시오. 298 K298\,\mathrm{K}298K의 ΔG∘=−33.3 kJ\Delta G^\circ=-33.3\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔG∘=−33.3kJ과 비교하고, 표준 조건에서 어느 온도 아래에서 자발적인지 쓰시오.

Hints
  1. Convert 500 ∘C500\,^\circ\mathrm{C}500∘C to kelvin, then use ΔG=ΔH−TΔS\Delta G=\Delta H-T\Delta SΔG=ΔH−TΔS with matching units.
  2. For ΔH<0\Delta H<0ΔH<0 and ΔS<0\Delta S<0ΔS<0, the process is favored only below T=ΔH/ΔST=\Delta H/\Delta ST=ΔH/ΔS.
한글 힌트
  1. 500 ∘C500\,^\circ\mathrm{C}500∘C를 켈빈으로 바꾼 뒤 단위를 맞춰 ΔG=ΔH−TΔS\Delta G=\Delta H-T\Delta SΔG=ΔH−TΔS를 쓴다.
  2. ΔH<0\Delta H<0ΔH<0, ΔS<0\Delta S<0ΔS<0이면 T=ΔH/ΔST=\Delta H/\Delta ST=ΔH/ΔS보다 아래에서만 유리하다.
Worked solution
Answer
ΔG(+773 K)=+60.9 kJ\Delta G(+773\,\mathrm{K})=+60.9\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔG(+773K)=+60.9kJ (vs −33.3 kJ-33.3\,\mathrm{kJ}−33.3kJ at 298 K); spontaneous below ≈466 K\approx466\,\mathrm{K}≈466K
  1. Convert temperature. T=500+273=773 KT=500+273=773\,\mathrm{K}T=500+273=773K; ΔS∘=−0.1983 kJ K−1\Delta S^\circ=-0.1983\,\mathrm{kJ\,K^{-1}}ΔS∘=−0.1983kJK−1.
  2. Recalculate Gibbs energy. ΔG=−92.38−(773)(−0.1983)=−92.38+153.3=+60.9 kJ\Delta G=-92.38-(773)(-0.1983)=-92.38+153.3=+60.9\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔG=−92.38−(773)(−0.1983)=−92.38+153.3=+60.9kJ.
  3. Compare with 298 K. At 298 K298\,\mathrm{K}298K, ΔG∘=−33.3 kJ\Delta G^\circ=-33.3\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔG∘=−33.3kJ (spontaneous); at 773 K773\,\mathrm{K}773K, ΔG=+60.9 kJ\Delta G=+60.9\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔG=+60.9kJ (nonspontaneous under standard conditions).
  4. Find the crossover. T=ΔH/ΔS=92.38/0.1983≈466 KT=\Delta H/\Delta S=92.38/0.1983\approx466\,\mathrm{K}T=ΔH/ΔS=92.38/0.1983≈466K; spontaneous only below about 466 K466\,\mathrm{K}466K.
  5. Industrial note. Plants still run near 500 ∘C500\,^\circ\mathrm{C}500∘C for kinetics (rate), not because high TTT improves thermodynamics.
  6. Takeaway. Recompute ΔG\Delta GΔG at the new TTT when ΔH\Delta HΔH and ΔS\Delta SΔS share a sign; high industrial TTT can be a kinetics compromise.
