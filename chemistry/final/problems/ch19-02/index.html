Standard entropy change for ammonia synthesis

암모니아 합성의 표준 엔트로피 변화

Calculate ΔS∘\Delta S^\circΔS∘ at 298 K298\,\mathrm{K}298K for
N2(g)+3H2(g)→2NH3(g)\mathrm{N_2(g)+3H_2(g)\rightarrow 2NH_3(g)}N2​(g)+3H2​(g)→2NH3​(g).
Standard molar entropies in J mol−1 K−1\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}}Jmol−1K−1 are S∘(N2)=191.5S^\circ(\mathrm{N_2})=191.5S∘(N2​)=191.5, S∘(H2)=130.6S^\circ(\mathrm{H_2})=130.6S∘(H2​)=130.6, and S∘(NH3)=192.5S^\circ(\mathrm{NH_3})=192.5S∘(NH3​)=192.5.
Hints
  1. ΔS∘=∑nS∘(products)−∑mS∘(reactants)\Delta S^\circ=\sum nS^\circ(\mathrm{products})-\sum mS^\circ(\mathrm{reactants})ΔS∘=∑nS∘(products)−∑mS∘(reactants).
  2. Unlike ΔHf∘\Delta H_f^\circΔHf∘​, elemental standard molar entropies are not zero at 298 K.
Worked solution
  1. Start with products minus reactants. ΔS∘=∑nSproducts∘−∑mSreactants∘\Delta S^\circ=\sum nS^\circ_{\mathrm{products}}-\sum mS^\circ_{\mathrm{reactants}}ΔS∘=∑nSproducts∘​−∑mSreactants∘​.
  2. Sum product entropy. 2S∘(NH3)=2(192.5)=385.0 J K−12S^\circ(\mathrm{NH_3})=2(192.5)=385.0\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}2S∘(NH3​)=2(192.5)=385.0JK−1.
  3. Sum reactant entropy. S∘(N2)+3S∘(H2)=191.5+3(130.6)=583.3 J K−1S^\circ(\mathrm{N_2})+3S^\circ(\mathrm{H_2})=191.5+3(130.6)=583.3\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}S∘(N2​)+3S∘(H2​)=191.5+3(130.6)=583.3JK−1.
  4. Subtract. ΔS∘=385.0−583.3=−198.3 J K−1\Delta S^\circ=385.0-583.3=-198.3\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}ΔS∘=385.0−583.3=−198.3JK−1 per mole of reaction as written.
  5. Check the sign. Four moles of gas become two, so a substantial entropy decrease is expected.
  6. Takeaway. Standard entropies of elements are not zero; include every species and its stoichiometric coefficient.
