Entropy of freezing mercury

수은 동결의 엔트로피 변화

Mercury’s normal freezing point is −38.9 ∘C-38.9\,^\circ\mathrm{C}−38.9∘C and its molar enthalpy of fusion is ΔHfus=2.29 kJ/mol\Delta H_{\mathrm{fus}}=2.29\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}ΔHfus​=2.29kJ/mol. What is the entropy change of the system when 50.0 g50.0\,\mathrm{g}50.0g of Hg(l)\mathrm{Hg(l)}Hg(l) freezes at the normal freezing point? (MHg=200.59 g/molM_{\mathrm{Hg}}=200.59\,\mathrm{g/mol}MHg​=200.59g/mol)

Hints
  1. At the normal freezing point the phase change is reversible: ΔS=qrev/T\Delta S=q_{\mathrm{rev}}/TΔS=qrev​/T.
  2. Freezing releases heat, so qrevq_{\mathrm{rev}}qrev​ for the system is negative.
한글 힌트
  1. 정상 어는점에서 상전이는 가역이므로 ΔS=qrev/T\Delta S=q_{\mathrm{rev}}/TΔS=qrev​/T이다.
  2. 동결은 열을 방출하므로 계의 qrevq_{\mathrm{rev}}qrev​는 음수이다.
Worked solution
Answer
ΔSsys=−2.44 J/K\Delta S_{\mathrm{sys}}=-2.44\,\mathrm{J/K}ΔSsys​=−2.44J/K
  1. Convert mass to moles. n=(50.0 g)/(200.59 g/mol)=0.249 moln=(50.0\,\mathrm{g})/(200.59\,\mathrm{g/mol})=0.249\,\mathrm{mol}n=(50.0g)/(200.59g/mol)=0.249mol.
  2. Assign the reversible heat. Freezing is the reverse of fusion, so qrev=−(0.249 mol)(2.29×103 J/mol)=−571 Jq_{\mathrm{rev}}=-(0.249\,\mathrm{mol})(2.29\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{J/mol})=-571\,\mathrm{J}qrev​=−(0.249mol)(2.29×103J/mol)=−571J.
  3. Convert the freezing point to kelvin. T=−38.9+273.15=234.3 KT=-38.9+273.15=234.3\,\mathrm{K}T=−38.9+273.15=234.3K.
  4. Apply ΔS=qrev/T\Delta S=q_{\mathrm{rev}}/TΔS=qrev​/T. ΔSsys=(−571 J)/(234.3 K)=−2.44 J/K\Delta S_{\mathrm{sys}}=(-571\,\mathrm{J})/(234.3\,\mathrm{K})=-2.44\,\mathrm{J/K}ΔSsys​=(−571J)/(234.3K)=−2.44J/K.
  5. Check the sign. Liquid → ordered solid decreases the system’s entropy, matching the negative result.
  6. Takeaway. For a phase change at the transition temperature, use ΔS=ΔHtrans/T\Delta S=\Delta H_{\mathrm{trans}}/TΔS=ΔHtrans​/T with the correct sign and TTT in kelvin.
