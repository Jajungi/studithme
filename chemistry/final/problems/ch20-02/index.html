Ch.20 · Calculation · #2 · Ch.20 lecture §20.2 Example 1 (MnO₄⁻ + C₂O₄²⁻); cf. Exercises 20.67–20.68 · 산화수·반쪽반응

Balance permanganate–oxalate in acid

산성에서 과망가니즈산–옥살산 균형 맞추기

Complete and balance the following equation by the method of half-reactions in acidic solution:

MnO4−(aq)+C2O42−(aq)→Mn2+(aq)+CO2(g)\mathrm{MnO_4^-(aq)+C_2O_4^{2-}(aq)\rightarrow Mn^{2+}(aq)+CO_2(g)}MnO4−​(aq)+C2​O42−​(aq)→Mn2+(aq)+CO2​(g)

Identify the species reduced and the reducing agent.
한글
산성 용액에서 반쪽 반응법으로 다음 식을 완성하고 균형을 맞추시오.

MnO4−(aq)+C2O42−(aq)→Mn2+(aq)+CO2(g)\mathrm{MnO_4^-(aq)+C_2O_4^{2-}(aq)\rightarrow Mn^{2+}(aq)+CO_2(g)}MnO4−​(aq)+C2​O42−​(aq)→Mn2+(aq)+CO2​(g)

Hints
  1. Write the two half-reactions, then balance in the order: other atoms, O with H2O\mathrm{H_2O}H2​O, H with H+\mathrm{H^+}H+, charge with e−e^-e−.
  2. Multiply so the electrons cancel (the least common multiple of 5 and 2 is 10).
한글 힌트
  1. 두 반쪽 반응으로 나눈 뒤 다른 원자 → O는 H2O\mathrm{H_2O}H2​O → H는 H+\mathrm{H^+}H+ → 전하는 e−e^-e− 순으로 맞춘다.
  2. 전자가 같아지도록 곱한다 (5와 2의 최소공배수 10).
Worked solution
Answer
2MnO4−+5C2O42−+16H+→2Mn2++10CO2+8H2O\mathrm{2MnO_4^-+5C_2O_4^{2-}+16H^+\rightarrow2Mn^{2+}+10CO_2+8H_2O}2MnO4−​+5C2​O42−​+16H+→2Mn2++10CO2​+8H2​O; MnO4−\mathrm{MnO_4^-}MnO4−​ is reduced; C2O42−\mathrm{C_2O_4^{2-}}C2​O42−​ is the reducing agent
  1. Assign oxidation numbers. Mn: +7→+2+7\rightarrow+2+7→+2 (reduced). C in C2O42−\mathrm{C_2O_4^{2-}}C2​O42−​: +3+3+3; in CO2\mathrm{CO_2}CO2​: +4+4+4 (oxidized). So MnO4−\mathrm{MnO_4^-}MnO4−​ is the oxidizing agent and C2O42−\mathrm{C_2O_4^{2-}}C2​O42−​ is the reducing agent.
  2. Reduction half-reaction. MnO4−→Mn2+\mathrm{MnO_4^-\rightarrow Mn^{2+}}MnO4−​→Mn2+. Add 4 H2O4\,\mathrm{H_2O}4H2​O to the right for O, then 8 H+8\,\mathrm{H^+}8H+ to the left for H, then 5e−5e^-5e− to the left for charge: MnO4−+8H++5e−→Mn2++4H2O\mathrm{MnO_4^-+8H^++5e^-\rightarrow Mn^{2+}+4H_2O}MnO4−​+8H++5e−→Mn2++4H2​O.
  3. Oxidation half-reaction. C2O42−→2CO2\mathrm{C_2O_4^{2-}\rightarrow 2CO_2}C2​O42−​→2CO2​ balances C and O. Charge: −2-2−2 on the left, 000 on the right, so add 2e−2e^-2e− to the right: C2O42−→2CO2+2e−\mathrm{C_2O_4^{2-}\rightarrow 2CO_2+2e^-}C2​O42−​→2CO2​+2e−.
  4. Equalize electrons. Multiply the reduction by 2 and the oxidation by 5, giving 10e−10e^-10e− in each: 2MnO4−+16H++10e−→2Mn2++8H2O\mathrm{2MnO_4^-+16H^++10e^-\rightarrow 2Mn^{2+}+8H_2O}2MnO4−​+16H++10e−→2Mn2++8H2​O and 5C2O42−→10CO2+10e−\mathrm{5C_2O_4^{2-}\rightarrow10CO_2+10e^-}5C2​O42−​→10CO2​+10e−.
  5. Add and cancel. 2MnO4−+5C2O42−+16H+→2Mn2++10CO2+8H2O\mathrm{2MnO_4^-+5C_2O_4^{2-}+16H^+\rightarrow 2Mn^{2+}+10CO_2+8H_2O}2MnO4−​+5C2​O42−​+16H+→2Mn2++10CO2​+8H2​O; the 10e−10e^-10e− cancel.
  6. Check atoms and charge. O: 8+20=288+20=288+20=28 on the left, 20+8=2820+8=2820+8=28 on the right. Charge: −2−10+16=+4-2-10+16=+4−2−10+16=+4 on the left and +4+4+4 on the right.
  7. Takeaway. In acid use H2O\mathrm{H_2O}H2​O for O, H+\mathrm{H^+}H+ for H, e−e^-e− for charge; the H+\mathrm{H^+}H+ appears on the reactant side because the permanganate reduction consumes acid.
한글 답

한글 해설
  1. 산화수를 부여한다. Mn: +7→+2+7\rightarrow+2+7→+2 (환원). C2O42−\mathrm{C_2O_4^{2-}}C2​O42−​의 C는 +3+3+3, CO2\mathrm{CO_2}CO2​의 C는 +4+4+4 (산화). 따라서 MnO4−\mathrm{MnO_4^-}MnO4−​가 산화제, C2O42−\mathrm{C_2O_4^{2-}}C2​O42−​가 환원제이다.
  2. 환원 반쪽 반응. MnO4−→Mn2+\mathrm{MnO_4^-\rightarrow Mn^{2+}}MnO4−​→Mn2+. O를 위해 오른쪽에 4 H2O4\,\mathrm{H_2O}4H2​O, H를 위해 왼쪽에 8 H+8\,\mathrm{H^+}8H+, 전하를 위해 왼쪽에 5e−5e^-5e−: MnO4−+8H++5e−→Mn2++4H2O\mathrm{MnO_4^-+8H^++5e^-\rightarrow Mn^{2+}+4H_2O}MnO4−​+8H++5e−→Mn2++4H2​O.
  3. 산화 반쪽 반응. C2O42−→2CO2\mathrm{C_2O_4^{2-}\rightarrow 2CO_2}C2​O42−​→2CO2​로 C와 O가 맞는다. 전하는 왼쪽 −2-2−2, 오른쪽 000이므로 오른쪽에 2e−2e^-2e−: C2O42−→2CO2+2e−\mathrm{C_2O_4^{2-}\rightarrow 2CO_2+2e^-}C2​O42−​→2CO2​+2e−.
  4. 전자 수를 맞춘다. 환원에 2, 산화에 5를 곱해 각각 10e−10e^-10e−: 2MnO4−+16H++10e−→2Mn2++8H2O\mathrm{2MnO_4^-+16H^++10e^-\rightarrow 2Mn^{2+}+8H_2O}2MnO4−​+16H++10e−→2Mn2++8H2​O, 5C2O42−→10CO2+10e−\mathrm{5C_2O_4^{2-}\rightarrow10CO_2+10e^-}5C2​O42−​→10CO2​+10e−.
  5. 더하고 소거한다. 2MnO4−+5C2O42−+16H+→2Mn2++10CO2+8H2O\mathrm{2MnO_4^-+5C_2O_4^{2-}+16H^+\rightarrow 2Mn^{2+}+10CO_2+8H_2O}2MnO4−​+5C2​O42−​+16H+→2Mn2++10CO2​+8H2​O; 10e−10e^-10e−가 소거된다.
  6. 원자와 전하를 점검한다. O: 왼쪽 8+20=288+20=288+20=28, 오른쪽 20+8=2820+8=2820+8=28. 전하: 왼쪽 −2−10+16=+4-2-10+16=+4−2−10+16=+4, 오른쪽 +4+4+4.
  7. 정리. 산성에서는 O는 H2O\mathrm{H_2O}H2​O, H는 H+\mathrm{H^+}H+, 전하는 e−e^-e−로 맞춘다. 과망가니즈산 환원이 산을 소비하므로 H+\mathrm{H^+}H+가 반응물 쪽에 나타난다.
