ΔG° from ΔH° and ΔS° at 298 K

298 K에서 ΔH°와 ΔS°로부터 ΔG° 구하기

For a certain chemical reaction, ΔH∘=−40.0 kJ\Delta H^\circ=-40.0\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔH∘=−40.0kJ and ΔS∘=−150.0 J/K\Delta S^\circ=-150.0\,\mathrm{J/K}ΔS∘=−150.0J/K.

(a) Does the reaction lead to an increase or decrease in the randomness of the system?

(b) Of the surroundings?

(c) Calculate ΔG∘\Delta G^\circΔG∘ for the reaction at 298 K298\,\mathrm{K}298K.

(d) Is the reaction spontaneous at 298 K298\,\mathrm{K}298K under standard conditions?

(a) 이 반응은 계의 무질서도를 증가시키는가, 감소시키는가?

(b) 주위의 무질서도는?

(c) 298 K298\,\mathrm{K}298K에서 반응의 ΔG∘\Delta G^\circΔG∘를 구하시오.

(d) 298 K298\,\mathrm{K}298K 표준 조건에서 이 반응은 자발적인가?

  1. ΔSsurr=−ΔHsys/T\Delta S_{\mathrm{surr}}=-\Delta H_{\mathrm{sys}}/TΔSsurr​=−ΔHsys​/T.
  2. ΔH∘\Delta H^\circΔH∘와 합치기 전에 ΔS∘\Delta S^\circΔS∘를 kJ/K로 바꾼다.
Worked solution
Answer
(a) decrease; (b) increase (+134 J/K+134\,\mathrm{J/K}+134J/K); (c) ΔG∘=+4.7 kJ\Delta G^\circ=+4.7\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔG∘=+4.7kJ; (d) not spontaneous
  1. Part (a): system. ΔS∘<0\Delta S^\circ<0ΔS∘<0, so randomness of the system decreases.
  2. Part (b): surroundings. ΔSsurr=−ΔH/T=+40000/298=+134 J/K\Delta S_{\mathrm{surr}}=-\Delta H/T=+40000/298=+134\,\mathrm{J/K}ΔSsurr​=−ΔH/T=+40000/298=+134J/K, so the surroundings' randomness increases (the reaction is exothermic).
  3. Part (c): ΔG°. ΔG∘=ΔH∘−TΔS∘=−40.0−(298)(−0.1500)=−40.0+44.7=+4.7 kJ\Delta G^\circ=\Delta H^\circ-T\Delta S^\circ=-40.0-(298)(-0.1500)=-40.0+44.7=+4.7\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔG∘=ΔH∘−TΔS∘=−40.0−(298)(−0.1500)=−40.0+44.7=+4.7kJ.
  4. Part (d): spontaneity. ΔG∘>0\Delta G^\circ>0ΔG∘>0, so not spontaneous at 298 K298\,\mathrm{K}298K under standard conditions. Consistently, ΔSuniv=−150+134=−16 J/K<0\Delta S_{\mathrm{univ}}=-150+134=-16\,\mathrm{J/K}<0ΔSuniv​=−150+134=−16J/K<0.
  5. Takeaway. The surroundings' gain (134 J/K) fails to offset the system's loss (150 J/K), so ΔG>0\Delta G>0ΔG>0 — same verdict from either criterion.
