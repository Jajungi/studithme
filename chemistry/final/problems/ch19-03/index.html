Ch.19 · Calculation · #3

Free energy from enthalpy and entropy

엔탈피와 엔트로피로 자유 에너지 계산

For N2(g)+O2(g)→2NO(g)\mathrm{N_2(g)+O_2(g)\rightarrow 2NO(g)}N2​(g)+O2​(g)→2NO(g) at 298 K298\,\mathrm{K}298K, ΔH∘=180.7 kJ\Delta H^\circ=180.7\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔH∘=180.7kJ and ΔS∘=24.7 J K−1\Delta S^\circ=24.7\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}ΔS∘=24.7JK−1. Calculate ΔG∘\Delta G^\circΔG∘ and determine whether the forward reaction is spontaneous under standard conditions.
Hints
  1. ΔG∘=ΔH∘−TΔS∘\Delta G^\circ=\Delta H^\circ-T\Delta S^\circΔG∘=ΔH∘−TΔS∘.
  2. Convert ΔS∘\Delta S^\circΔS∘ from J/K to kJ/K before subtracting.
Worked solution
  1. Start with consistent units. ΔS∘=24.7 J K−1=0.0247 kJ K−1\Delta S^\circ=24.7\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}=0.0247\,\mathrm{kJ\,K^{-1}}ΔS∘=24.7JK−1=0.0247kJK−1.
  2. Calculate the entropy term. TΔS∘=(298 K)(0.0247 kJ K−1)=7.36 kJT\Delta S^\circ=(298\,\mathrm{K})(0.0247\,\mathrm{kJ\,K^{-1}})=7.36\,\mathrm{kJ}TΔS∘=(298K)(0.0247kJK−1)=7.36kJ.
  3. Apply the Gibbs equation. ΔG∘=180.7−7.36=173.3 kJ\Delta G^\circ=180.7-7.36=173.3\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔG∘=180.7−7.36=173.3kJ.
  4. Interpret the sign. Since ΔG∘>0\Delta G^\circ>0ΔG∘>0, the forward reaction is nonspontaneous under standard conditions at 298 K298\,\mathrm{K}298K; the reverse is spontaneous.
  5. Takeaway. Match energy units before combining ΔH\Delta HΔH and TΔST\Delta STΔS, then use the sign of ΔG\Delta GΔG as the spontaneity criterion.
