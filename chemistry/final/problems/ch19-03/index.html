Universe entropy for ice melting in the hand

손바닥에서 얼음이 녹을 때의 우주 엔트로피

One mole of ice (ΔHfus=6.01 kJ/mol\Delta H_{\mathrm{fus}}=6.01\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}ΔHfus​=6.01kJ/mol) melts at 0 ∘C0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}0∘C (273 K273\,\mathrm{K}273K) in the palm of your hand, which is at body temperature (37 ∘C=310 K37\,^\circ\mathrm{C}=310\,\mathrm{K}37∘C=310K). Calculate ΔSsys\Delta S_{\mathrm{sys}}ΔSsys​, ΔSsurr\Delta S_{\mathrm{surr}}ΔSsurr​, and ΔSuniv\Delta S_{\mathrm{univ}}ΔSuniv​. Is the process spontaneous?
한글

ΔSsys\Delta S_{\mathrm{sys}}ΔSsys​, ΔSsurr\Delta S_{\mathrm{surr}}ΔSsurr​, ΔSuniv\Delta S_{\mathrm{univ}}ΔSuniv​를 구하고 자발적인지 판정하시오.

Hints
  1. The system melts reversibly at 273 K273\,\mathrm{K}273K: ΔSsys=ΔHfus/Tsys\Delta S_{\mathrm{sys}}=\Delta H_{\mathrm{fus}}/T_{\mathrm{sys}}ΔSsys​=ΔHfus​/Tsys​.
  2. The palm loses the same heat at 310 K310\,\mathrm{K}310K, so ΔSsurr=−qsys/Tsurr\Delta S_{\mathrm{surr}}=-q_{\mathrm{sys}}/T_{\mathrm{surr}}ΔSsurr​=−qsys​/Tsurr​.
한글 힌트
  1. 계는 273 K273\,\mathrm{K}273K에서 가역적으로 녹는다: ΔSsys=ΔHfus/Tsys\Delta S_{\mathrm{sys}}=\Delta H_{\mathrm{fus}}/T_{\mathrm{sys}}ΔSsys​=ΔHfus​/Tsys​.
  2. 손바닥은 같은 열을 310 K310\,\mathrm{K}310K에서 잃으므로 ΔSsurr=−qsys/Tsurr\Delta S_{\mathrm{surr}}=-q_{\mathrm{sys}}/T_{\mathrm{surr}}ΔSsurr​=−qsys​/Tsurr​.
Worked solution
Answer
ΔSsys=+22.0 J/K\Delta S_{\mathrm{sys}}=+22.0\,\mathrm{J/K}ΔSsys​=+22.0J/K; ΔSsurr=−19.4 J/K\Delta S_{\mathrm{surr}}=-19.4\,\mathrm{J/K}ΔSsurr​=−19.4J/K; ΔSuniv=+2.6 J/K\Delta S_{\mathrm{univ}}=+2.6\,\mathrm{J/K}ΔSuniv​=+2.6J/K; spontaneous
  1. Compute the system entropy at the melting point. ΔSsys=(1 mol)(6.01×103 J/mol)/(273 K)=+22.0 J/K\Delta S_{\mathrm{sys}}=(1\,\mathrm{mol})(6.01\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{J/mol})/(273\,\mathrm{K})=+22.0\,\mathrm{J/K}ΔSsys​=(1mol)(6.01×103J/mol)/(273K)=+22.0J/K.
  2. Compute the surroundings entropy at body temperature. The palm loses 6.01×103 J6.01\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{J}6.01×103J, so ΔSsurr=(−6.01×103 J)/(310 K)=−19.4 J/K\Delta S_{\mathrm{surr}}=(-6.01\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{J})/(310\,\mathrm{K})=-19.4\,\mathrm{J/K}ΔSsurr​=(−6.01×103J)/(310K)=−19.4J/K.
  3. Add for the universe. ΔSuniv=ΔSsys+ΔSsurr=22.0+(−19.4)=+2.6 J/K\Delta S_{\mathrm{univ}}=\Delta S_{\mathrm{sys}}+\Delta S_{\mathrm{surr}}=22.0+(-19.4)=+2.6\,\mathrm{J/K}ΔSuniv​=ΔSsys​+ΔSsurr​=22.0+(−19.4)=+2.6J/K.
  4. Apply the second law. ΔSuniv>0\Delta S_{\mathrm{univ}}>0ΔSuniv​>0, so the irreversible melting in a warm hand is spontaneous.
  5. Note the reversible limit. If ice and surroundings were both at 0 ∘C0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}0∘C, then ΔSuniv=0\Delta S_{\mathrm{univ}}=0ΔSuniv​=0 and melting would be reversible.
  6. Takeaway. Spontaneity is decided by ΔSuniv\Delta S_{\mathrm{univ}}ΔSuniv​, not by ΔSsys\Delta S_{\mathrm{sys}}ΔSsys​ alone; use each reservoir’s own temperature.
