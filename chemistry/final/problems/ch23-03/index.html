Ligands, denticity and the chelate effect

Hints
  1. Count donor atoms: each py has one N; bipy has two N.
  2. Replacing one chelating ligand by two monodentate ligands changes the number of free particles in solution.
한글 힌트
  1. donor 원자 수를 센다. py는 N이 하나, bipy는 N이 둘이다.
  2. 킬레이트 리간드 하나를 한자리 리간드 둘로 바꾸면 용액 속 자유 입자 수가 달라진다.
Worked solution
Answer
(a) monodentate; (b) K<1K<1K<1; (c) bidentate; (d) CO32−\mathrm{CO_3^{2-}}CO32−​, H2O\mathrm{H_2O}H2​O, PH3\mathrm{PH_3}PH3​; (e) 6, 6, 4, 6; (f) ΔG∘≈−17\Delta G^\circ\approx-17ΔG∘≈−17 to −18 kJ-18\ \mathrm{kJ}−18 kJ, entropy-driven
  1. (a) Pyridine. One ring N lone pair → monodentate.
  2. (b) Chelate effect. The reaction swaps one bidentate bipy for two monodentate py: the reactant side has 3 particles (complex + 2 py), the product side only 2 (complex + bipy), so ΔS\Delta SΔS is negative and the chelate is lost → K<1K<1K<1.
  3. (c) Carbonate. Co(III) needs CN 6: 4 NH34\,\mathrm{NH_3}4NH3​ supply 4, so carbonate must supply 2 → bidentate.
  4. (d) Ligand ability. A ligand needs an available lone pair and is usually anionic or polar. (i) CO32−\mathrm{CO_3^{2-}}CO32−​ (anion with lone pairs). (ii) H2O\mathrm{H_2O}H2​O (H3O+\mathrm{H_3O^+}H3​O+ is positive and its lone pair is already used). (iii) PH3\mathrm{PH_3}PH3​ (P has a lone pair; H3PO4\mathrm{H_3PO_4}H3​PO4​ is a neutral acid with no P lone pair).
  5. (e) CN. (i) 3 bipy × 2 = 6. (ii) 2 o-phen × 2 + oxalate 2 = 6. (iii) 3 PPh₃ + Cl⁻ = 4. (iv) EDTA is hexadentate = 6.
  6. (f) Entropy term. T=300 KT=300\ \mathrm{K}T=300 K: −TΔS∘=−(300)(0.031)=−9.3 kJ-T\Delta S^\circ=-(300)(0.031)=-9.3\ \mathrm{kJ}−TΔS∘=−(300)(0.031)=−9.3 kJ. ΔG∘=−8−9.3≈−17 kJ\Delta G^\circ=-8-9.3\approx-17\ \mathrm{kJ}ΔG∘=−8−9.3≈−17 kJ (the table quotes −18-18−18 kJ because of rounding). The entropy contribution (−9-9−9 kJ) is slightly larger than ΔH∘\Delta H^\circΔH∘ (−8-8−8 kJ): the chelate effect is entropy-driven.
  7. Takeaway. Count donor atoms, not ligand names; chelates win mainly through ΔS∘>0\Delta S^\circ>0ΔS∘>0 (more free particles).
