Hints
  1. ΔHrxn∘=∑nΔHf∘(products)−∑mΔHf∘(reactants)\Delta H^\circ_{\mathrm{rxn}}=\sum n\Delta H_f^\circ(\mathrm{products})-\sum m\Delta H_f^\circ(\mathrm{reactants})ΔHrxn∘​=∑nΔHf∘​(products)−∑mΔHf∘​(reactants).
  2. Include stoichiometric coefficients and the physical state of water.
한글 힌트
  1. ΔHrxn∘=∑nΔHf∘(생성물)−∑mΔHf∘(반응물)\Delta H^\circ_{\mathrm{rxn}}=\sum n\Delta H_f^\circ(\mathrm{생성물})-\sum m\Delta H_f^\circ(\mathrm{반응물})ΔHrxn∘​=∑nΔHf∘​(생성물)−∑mΔHf∘​(반응물).
  2. 계수와 물의 물리적 상태를 반영한다.
Worked solution
Answer
ΔHrxn∘=−2.220×103 kJ\Delta H^\circ_{\mathrm{rxn}}=-2.220\times10^3\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔHrxn∘​=−2.220×103kJ
  1. Start with products minus reactants. Use ΔHrxn∘=∑nΔHf∘(products)−∑mΔHf∘(reactants)\Delta H^\circ_{\mathrm{rxn}}=\sum n\Delta H_f^\circ(\mathrm{products})-\sum m\Delta H_f^\circ(\mathrm{reactants})ΔHrxn∘​=∑nΔHf∘​(products)−∑mΔHf∘​(reactants).
  2. Sum the products. 3(−393.5)+4(−285.8)=−2323.7 kJ3(-393.5)+4(-285.8)=-2323.7\,\mathrm{kJ}3(−393.5)+4(−285.8)=−2323.7kJ.
  3. Sum the reactants. 1(−103.85)+5(0)=−103.85 kJ1(-103.85)+5(0)=-103.85\,\mathrm{kJ}1(−103.85)+5(0)=−103.85kJ; elemental O2(g)\mathrm{O_2(g)}O2​(g) in its standard state has ΔHf∘=0\Delta H_f^\circ=0ΔHf∘​=0.
  4. Subtract. ΔHrxn∘=−2323.7−(−103.85)=−2219.85 kJ\Delta H^\circ_{\mathrm{rxn}}=-2323.7-(-103.85)=-2219.85\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔHrxn∘​=−2323.7−(−103.85)=−2219.85kJ, or −2.220×103 kJ-2.220\times10^3\,\mathrm{kJ}−2.220×103kJ per reaction as written.
  5. Interpret. The large negative value is consistent with strongly exothermic hydrocarbon combustion.
  6. Takeaway. Formation-enthalpy calculations are always products minus reactants; coefficients multiply every tabulated value.
