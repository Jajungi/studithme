Scaling ΔH for methanol decomposition

메탄올 분해 반응의 ΔH 환산

Consider 2CH3OH(g)→2CH4(g)+O2(g)\mathrm{2CH_3OH(g)\rightarrow 2CH_4(g)+O_2(g)}2CH3​OH(g)→2CH4​(g)+O2​(g), ΔH=+252.8 kJ\Delta H=+252.8\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔH=+252.8kJ.

(a) Is this reaction exothermic or endothermic?

(b) Calculate the heat transferred when 24.0 g24.0\,\mathrm{g}24.0g of CH3OH(g)\mathrm{CH_3OH(g)}CH3​OH(g) is decomposed at constant pressure.

(c) For a given sample of CH3OH\mathrm{CH_3OH}CH3​OH the enthalpy change is 82.1 kJ82.1\,\mathrm{kJ}82.1kJ. How many grams of methane gas are produced?

(d) How many kilojoules of heat are released when 38.5 g38.5\,\mathrm{g}38.5g of CH4(g)\mathrm{CH_4(g)}CH4​(g) reacts completely with O2(g)\mathrm{O_2(g)}O2​(g) to form CH3OH(g)\mathrm{CH_3OH(g)}CH3​OH(g) at constant pressure?

(MCH3OH=32.04M_{\mathrm{CH_3OH}}=32.04MCH3​OH​=32.04, MCH4=16.04 g/molM_{\mathrm{CH_4}}=16.04\,\mathrm{g/mol}MCH4​​=16.04g/mol)

Hints
  1. The coefficient of CH3OH\mathrm{CH_3OH}CH3​OH is 2, so +252.8 kJ+252.8\,\mathrm{kJ}+252.8kJ corresponds to two moles.
  2. Part (d) runs the equation backwards, which reverses the sign of ΔH\Delta HΔH.
한글 힌트
  1. CH3OH\mathrm{CH_3OH}CH3​OH의 계수가 2이므로 +252.8 kJ+252.8\,\mathrm{kJ}+252.8kJ은 2 mol에 대한 값이다.
  2. (d)는 반응식을 거꾸로 쓰는 것이므로 ΔH\Delta HΔH의 부호가 바뀐다.
Worked solution
Answer
(a) endothermic; (b) +94.7 kJ+94.7\,\mathrm{kJ}+94.7kJ; (c) 10.4 g CH410.4\,\mathrm{g}\ \mathrm{CH_4}10.4g CH4​; (d) 303 kJ303\,\mathrm{kJ}303kJ released
  1. Part (a): read the sign. ΔH>0\Delta H>0ΔH>0 means heat flows into the system, so the decomposition is endothermic.
  2. Convert the equation to a per-mole factor. The balanced equation consumes 2 mol of CH3OH\mathrm{CH_3OH}CH3​OH and makes 2 mol of CH4\mathrm{CH_4}CH4​ for +252.8 kJ+252.8\,\mathrm{kJ}+252.8kJ, so the factor is +252.8/2=+126.4 kJ+252.8/2=+126.4\,\mathrm{kJ}+252.8/2=+126.4kJ per mole of either species.
  3. Part (b): mass to moles to heat. n=(24.0 g)/(32.04 g/mol)=0.749 moln=(24.0\,\mathrm{g})/(32.04\,\mathrm{g/mol})=0.749\,\mathrm{mol}n=(24.0g)/(32.04g/mol)=0.749mol, giving qP=(0.749)(+126.4)=+94.7 kJq_P=(0.749)(+126.4)=+94.7\,\mathrm{kJ}qP​=(0.749)(+126.4)=+94.7kJ absorbed.
  4. Part (c): run the conversion backwards. nCH4=(82.1 kJ)/(126.4 kJ/mol)=0.649 moln_{\mathrm{CH_4}}=(82.1\,\mathrm{kJ})/(126.4\,\mathrm{kJ/mol})=0.649\,\mathrm{mol}nCH4​​=(82.1kJ)/(126.4kJ/mol)=0.649mol, so the mass is (0.649)(16.04)=10.4 g(0.649)(16.04)=10.4\,\mathrm{g}(0.649)(16.04)=10.4g of CH4\mathrm{CH_4}CH4​.
  5. Part (d): reverse the reaction. Forming CH3OH\mathrm{CH_3OH}CH3​OH from CH4\mathrm{CH_4}CH4​ is the reverse direction, so the factor becomes −126.4 kJ-126.4\,\mathrm{kJ}−126.4kJ per mole of CH4\mathrm{CH_4}CH4​. With n=(38.5)/(16.04)=2.40 moln=(38.5)/(16.04)=2.40\,\mathrm{mol}n=(38.5)/(16.04)=2.40mol, qP=−303 kJq_P=-303\,\mathrm{kJ}qP​=−303kJ, i.e. 303 kJ303\,\mathrm{kJ}303kJ released.
  6. Takeaway. Before touching the numbers, divide ΔH\Delta HΔH by the coefficient to get a per-mole factor; then direction only controls the sign.
