pH-dependent and amphoteric solubility

pH·양쪽성에 따른 용해도

Decide whether each solid becomes more soluble in strongly acidic solution, strongly basic solution, both, or neither (relative to pure water). Briefly justify.

(a) CaCO3\mathrm{CaCO_3}CaCO3​

(b) AgCl\mathrm{AgCl}AgCl

(c) AgI\mathrm{AgI}AgI

(d) Al(OH)3\mathrm{Al(OH)_3}Al(OH)3​

(e) CaF2\mathrm{CaF_2}CaF2​
한글
각 고체가 순수 물에 비해 강산에서, 강염기에서, 둘 다에서, 아니면 어느 쪽에서도 더 잘 녹는지 고르고 짧게 근거를 쓰시오.

(a) CaCO3\mathrm{CaCO_3}CaCO3​

(b) AgCl\mathrm{AgCl}AgCl

(c) AgI\mathrm{AgI}AgI

(d) Al(OH)3\mathrm{Al(OH)_3}Al(OH)3​

(e) CaF2\mathrm{CaF_2}CaF2​

Hints
  1. Anions that are conjugate bases of weak acids are removed by H+\mathrm{H^+}H+.
  2. Amphoteric hydroxides dissolve in both strong acid and strong base.
한글 힌트
  1. 약산의 공액 염기인 음이온은 H+\mathrm{H^+}H+에 의해 제거된다.
  2. 양쪽성 수산화물은 강산과 강염기 모두에 녹는다.
Worked solution
Answer
(a) acid; (b) neither; (c) neither; (d) both; (e) acid
  1. Concept. Acid increases solubility when the anion is a weak-base (consumes H+\mathrm{H^+}H+); amphoteric hydroxides also dissolve in excess OH−\mathrm{OH^-}OH−.
  2. (a) CaCO₃. CO32−\mathrm{CO_3^{2-}}CO32−​ is basic → more soluble in acid (forms HCO3−/H2CO3/CO2\mathrm{HCO_3^-/H_2CO_3/CO_2}HCO3−​/H2​CO3​/CO2​). Not amphoteric as a hydroxide.
  3. (b) AgCl. Cl−\mathrm{Cl^-}Cl− is the conjugate base of strong HCl → acid does not help; neither strong base (unless complexation is considered separately). Answer: neither for simple pH effect.
  4. (c) AgI. Same as AgCl for pH — iodide is a very weak base → neither.
  5. (d) Al(OH)₃. Amphoteric: dissolves in acid (Al3+\mathrm{Al^{3+}}Al3+) and in strong base ([Al(OH)4]−[\mathrm{Al(OH)_4}]^-[Al(OH)4​]−).
  6. (e) CaF₂. F−\mathrm{F^-}F− is basic (HF weak) → more soluble in acid.
  7. Takeaway. Ask “is the anion basic?” and “is the hydroxide amphoteric?” before guessing pH effects.
한글 답
(a) 산; (b) 해당 없음; (c) 해당 없음; (d) 둘 다; (e) 산
한글 해설
  1. 개념. 음이온이 약염기이면 산에서 용해도↑; 양쪽성 수산화물은 과량 OH−\mathrm{OH^-}OH−에도 녹는다.
  2. (a) 산 (탄산염).
  3. (b) 해당 없음(단순 pH 효과).
  4. (c) 해당 없음.
  5. (d) 둘 다(양쪽성).
  6. (e) 산 (플루오린화물).
  7. 정리. “음이온이 염기성인가?”, “수산화물이 양쪽성인가?”를 먼저 묻는다.
