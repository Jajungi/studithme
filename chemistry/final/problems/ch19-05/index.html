Equilibrium constant from standard free energy

표준 자유 에너지에서 평형 상수 계산

For the Haber equilibrium N2(g)+3H2(g)⇌2NH3(g)\mathrm{N_2(g)+3H_2(g)\rightleftharpoons 2NH_3(g)}N2​(g)+3H2​(g)⇌2NH3​(g) at 298 K298\,\mathrm{K}298K, ΔG∘=−33.3 kJ\Delta G^\circ=-33.3\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔG∘=−33.3kJ per reaction as written. Calculate KKK and state which side equilibrium favors. Use R=8.314 J mol−1 K−1R=8.314\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}}R=8.314Jmol−1K−1.
Hints
  1. ΔG∘=−RTln⁡K\Delta G^\circ=-RT\ln KΔG∘=−RTlnK.
  2. Convert ΔG∘\Delta G^\circΔG∘ to joules to match the units of RRR.
Worked solution
  1. Start with the free-energy/equilibrium relation. ΔG∘=−RTln⁡K\Delta G^\circ=-RT\ln KΔG∘=−RTlnK, so ln⁡K=−ΔG∘/(RT)\ln K=-\Delta G^\circ/(RT)lnK=−ΔG∘/(RT).
  2. Make units consistent. ΔG∘=−33.3 kJ=−3.33×104 J\Delta G^\circ=-33.3\,\mathrm{kJ}=-3.33\times10^4\,\mathrm{J}ΔG∘=−33.3kJ=−3.33×104J.
  3. Calculate the exponent. ln⁡K=−(−33300)/[(8.314)(298)]=13.44\ln K=-(-33300)/[(8.314)(298)]=13.44lnK=−(−33300)/[(8.314)(298)]=13.44.
  4. Exponentiate. K=e13.44=6.9×105K=e^{13.44}=6.9\times10^5K=e13.44=6.9×105.
  5. Interpret. Because K≫1K\gg1K≫1 and ΔG∘<0\Delta G^\circ<0ΔG∘<0, products are strongly favored under standard-state thermodynamic conditions at 298 K298\,\mathrm{K}298K.
  6. Takeaway. Negative ΔG∘\Delta G^\circΔG∘ corresponds to K>1K>1K>1; the more negative it is relative to RTRTRT, the larger the equilibrium constant.
