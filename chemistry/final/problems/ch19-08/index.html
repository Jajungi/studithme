Hints
  1. ΔG∘=∑nΔGf∘(products)−∑mΔGf∘(reactants)\Delta G^\circ=\sum n\Delta G_f^\circ(\mathrm{products})-\sum m\Delta G_f^\circ(\mathrm{reactants})ΔG∘=∑nΔGf∘​(products)−∑mΔGf∘​(reactants).
  2. An element in its standard state has ΔGf∘=0\Delta G_f^\circ=0ΔGf∘​=0; reversing flips the sign of ΔG∘\Delta G^\circΔG∘.
한글 힌트
  1. ΔG∘=∑nΔGf∘(생성물)−∑mΔGf∘(반응물)\Delta G^\circ=\sum n\Delta G_f^\circ(\mathrm{생성물})-\sum m\Delta G_f^\circ(\mathrm{반응물})ΔG∘=∑nΔGf∘​(생성물)−∑mΔGf∘​(반응물).
  2. 표준 상태 원소의 ΔGf∘=0\Delta G_f^\circ=0ΔGf∘​=0이고, 역반응은 부호만 바꾼다.
Worked solution
Answer
ΔG∘=−1102.8 kJ\Delta G^\circ=-1102.8\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔG∘=−1102.8kJ (spontaneous); reverse +1102.8 kJ+1102.8\,\mathrm{kJ}+1102.8kJ
  1. Sum product free energies. 4ΔGf∘(PCl3)=4(−269.6)=−1078.4 kJ4\Delta G_f^\circ(\mathrm{PCl_3})=4(-269.6)=-1078.4\,\mathrm{kJ}4ΔGf∘​(PCl3​)=4(−269.6)=−1078.4kJ.
  2. Sum reactant free energies. ΔGf∘(P4)+6ΔGf∘(Cl2)=24.4+6(0)=24.4 kJ\Delta G_f^\circ(\mathrm{P_4})+6\Delta G_f^\circ(\mathrm{Cl_2})=24.4+6(0)=24.4\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔGf∘​(P4​)+6ΔGf∘​(Cl2​)=24.4+6(0)=24.4kJ.
  3. Subtract. ΔG∘=−1078.4−24.4=−1102.8 kJ\Delta G^\circ=-1078.4-24.4=-1102.8\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔG∘=−1078.4−24.4=−1102.8kJ.
  4. Interpret. ΔG∘<0\Delta G^\circ<0ΔG∘<0, so the forward reaction is spontaneous under standard conditions.
  5. Reverse the equation. For 4PCl3(g)→P4(g)+6Cl2(g)\mathrm{4PCl_3(g)\rightarrow P_4(g)+6Cl_2(g)}4PCl3​(g)→P4​(g)+6Cl2​(g), ΔG∘=+1102.8 kJ\Delta G^\circ=+1102.8\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔG∘=+1102.8kJ.
  6. Takeaway. Formation tables give reaction ΔG∘\Delta G^\circΔG∘ by products − reactants; standard-state elements contribute zero, and reversing flips the sign.
