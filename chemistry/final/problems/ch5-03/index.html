Hess’s law formation of carbon monoxide

헤스 법칙으로 일산화탄소 생성

Use Hess’s law to calculate ΔH\Delta HΔH for
C(s)+12O2(g)→CO(g)\mathrm{C(s)+\tfrac12O_2(g)\rightarrow CO(g)}C(s)+21​O2​(g)→CO(g)
from:

(1) C(s)+O2(g)→CO2(g)\mathrm{C(s)+O_2(g)\rightarrow CO_2(g)}C(s)+O2​(g)→CO2​(g) ΔH1=−393.5 kJ\Delta H_1=-393.5\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔH1​=−393.5kJ

(2) CO(g)+12O2(g)→CO2(g)\mathrm{CO(g)+\tfrac12O_2(g)\rightarrow CO_2(g)}CO(g)+21​O2​(g)→CO2​(g) ΔH2=−283.0 kJ\Delta H_2=-283.0\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔH2​=−283.0kJ.
Hints
  1. The target has CO\mathrm{CO}CO as a product, so reverse equation (2).
  2. Reversing an equation reverses the sign of ΔH\Delta HΔH.
Worked solution
  1. Start with the target species. Keep equation (1) as written because it places C(s)\mathrm{C(s)}C(s) on the reactant side.
  2. Reverse equation (2). CO2(g)→CO(g)+12O2(g)\mathrm{CO_2(g)\rightarrow CO(g)+\tfrac12O_2(g)}CO2​(g)→CO(g)+21​O2​(g) and therefore ΔH=+283.0 kJ\Delta H=+283.0\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔH=+283.0kJ.
  3. Add the manipulated equations. CO2\mathrm{CO_2}CO2​ cancels, and one half mole of O2\mathrm{O_2}O2​ cancels, leaving C(s)+12O2(g)→CO(g)\mathrm{C(s)+\tfrac12O_2(g)\rightarrow CO(g)}C(s)+21​O2​(g)→CO(g).
  4. Add the enthalpy changes. ΔH=−393.5+283.0=−110.5 kJ\Delta H=-393.5+283.0=-110.5\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔH=−393.5+283.0=−110.5kJ.
  5. Check the result. The summed chemical equation exactly matches the target, and the negative sign means formation of CO from its elements is exothermic.
  6. Takeaway. Manipulate equations and their ΔH\Delta HΔH values together, then verify every non-target species cancels.
