Common-ion effect on calcium fluoride

플루오린화 칼슘의 공통 이온 효과

For CaF2(s)⇌Ca2+(aq)+2F−(aq)\mathrm{CaF_2(s)\rightleftharpoons Ca^{2+}(aq)+2F^-(aq)}CaF2​(s)⇌Ca2+(aq)+2F−(aq), Ksp=3.9×10−11K_{sp}=3.9\times10^{-11}Ksp​=3.9×10−11 at 25∘C25^\circ\mathrm{C}25∘C. Calculate the molar solubility of CaF2\mathrm{CaF_2}CaF2​ in (a) 0.010 M0.010\,\mathrm{M}0.010M Ca(NO3)2\mathrm{Ca(NO_3)_2}Ca(NO3​)2​ and (b) 0.010 M0.010\,\mathrm{M}0.010M NaF\mathrm{NaF}NaF.

  2. Use the dominant common-ion concentration when the added concentration is much larger than the solubility.

  2. 첨가된 공통 이온 농도가 용해도보다 매우 크면 그 농도를 지배값으로 사용한다.
Worked solution
Answer
(a) 3.1×10−5 M3.1\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm{M}3.1×10−5M (b) 3.9×10−7 M3.9\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{M}3.9×10−7M
  1. Start with dissolution stoichiometry. If the molar solubility is sss, dissolution adds sss M Ca2+\mathrm{Ca^{2+}}Ca2+ and 2s2s2s M F−\mathrm{F^-}F−.
  2. (a) Calcium common ion. Ksp=(0.010+s)(2s)2≈(0.010)(4s2)K_{sp}=(0.010+s)(2s)^2\approx(0.010)(4s^2)Ksp​=(0.010+s)(2s)2≈(0.010)(4s2). Thus s=(3.9×10−11)/[4(0.010)]=3.1×10−5 Ms=\sqrt{(3.9\times10^{-11})/[4(0.010)]}=3.1\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm{M}s=(3.9×10−11)/[4(0.010)]​=3.1×10−5M.
  3. Validate (a). s/0.010=0.0031s/0.010=0.0031s/0.010=0.0031, so neglecting sss beside 0.0100.0100.010 is well within the 5% guideline.
  4. (b) Fluoride common ion. Ksp=s(0.010+2s)2≈s(0.010)2K_{sp}=s(0.010+2s)^2\approx s(0.010)^2Ksp​=s(0.010+2s)2≈s(0.010)2, so s=(3.9×10−11)/(1.0×10−4)=3.9×10−7 Ms=(3.9\times10^{-11})/(1.0\times10^{-4})=3.9\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{M}s=(3.9×10−11)/(1.0×10−4)=3.9×10−7M.
  5. Compare the effects. Fluoride suppresses solubility more strongly here because it is squared in the KspK_{sp}Ksp​ expression.
  6. Takeaway. Write the full stoichiometric concentrations before approximating; the exponent on a common ion controls how strongly it suppresses solubility.
