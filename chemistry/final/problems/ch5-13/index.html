Formation enthalpy of diethyl ether from its combustion

연소열로부터 다이에틸 에터의 생성 엔탈피

The complete combustion of 1 mol of diethyl ether, C4H10O(l)\mathrm{C_4H_{10}O(l)}C4​H10​O(l), to CO2(g)\mathrm{CO_2(g)}CO2​(g) and H2O(l)\mathrm{H_2O(l)}H2​O(l) yields ΔH∘=−2723.7 kJ\Delta H^\circ=-2723.7\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔH∘=−2723.7kJ.

(a) Write a balanced equation for the combustion of 1 mol of C4H10O(l)\mathrm{C_4H_{10}O(l)}C4​H10​O(l).

(b) Using this information and ΔHf∘\Delta H_f^\circΔHf∘​ values of −393.5 kJ/mol-393.5\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}−393.5kJ/mol for CO2(g)\mathrm{CO_2(g)}CO2​(g) and −285.8 kJ/mol-285.8\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}−285.8kJ/mol for H2O(l)\mathrm{H_2O(l)}H2​O(l), calculate ΔHf∘\Delta H_f^\circΔHf∘​ for diethyl ether.
한글
다이에틸 에터 C4H10O(l)\mathrm{C_4H_{10}O(l)}C4​H10​O(l) 1 mol이 CO2(g)\mathrm{CO_2(g)}CO2​(g)와 H2O(l)\mathrm{H_2O(l)}H2​O(l)로 완전 연소할 때 ΔH∘=−2723.7 kJ\Delta H^\circ=-2723.7\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔH∘=−2723.7kJ이다.

(a) C4H10O(l)\mathrm{C_4H_{10}O(l)}C4​H10​O(l) 1 mol의 연소 반응식을 균형 맞춰 쓰시오.

(b) 이 값과 ΔHf∘\Delta H_f^\circΔHf∘​(CO2(g)=−393.5\mathrm{CO_2(g)}=-393.5CO2​(g)=−393.5, H2O(l)=−285.8 kJ/mol\mathrm{H_2O(l)}=-285.8\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}H2​O(l)=−285.8kJ/mol)를 이용해 다이에틸 에터의 ΔHf∘\Delta H_f^\circΔHf∘​를 구하시오.

Hints
  1. Balance the equation first — the oxygen coefficient must account for the O already in the ether.
  2. Write ΔHrxn∘=∑nΔHf∘(products)−∑mΔHf∘(reactants)\Delta H^\circ_{\mathrm{rxn}}=\sum n\Delta H_f^\circ(\mathrm{products})-\sum m\Delta H_f^\circ(\mathrm{reactants})ΔHrxn∘​=∑nΔHf∘​(products)−∑mΔHf∘​(reactants) and solve for the unknown.
한글 힌트
  1. 반응식부터 균형을 맞춘다. 에터 자체에 산소가 하나 들어 있다는 점을 반영해야 한다.
  2. ΔHrxn∘=∑nΔHf∘(생성물)−∑mΔHf∘(반응물)\Delta H^\circ_{\mathrm{rxn}}=\sum n\Delta H_f^\circ(\mathrm{생성물})-\sum m\Delta H_f^\circ(\mathrm{반응물})ΔHrxn∘​=∑nΔHf∘​(생성물)−∑mΔHf∘​(반응물)를 세우고 미지수를 푼다.
Worked solution
Answer
(a) C4H10O(l)+6O2(g)→4CO2(g)+5H2O(l)\mathrm{C_4H_{10}O(l)+6O_2(g)\rightarrow 4CO_2(g)+5H_2O(l)}C4​H10​O(l)+6O2​(g)→4CO2​(g)+5H2​O(l); (b) ΔHf∘=−279.3 kJ/mol\Delta H_f^\circ=-279.3\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}ΔHf∘​=−279.3kJ/mol
  1. Part (a): balance carbon and hydrogen first. Four carbons give 4CO24\mathrm{CO_2}4CO2​ and ten hydrogens give 5H2O5\mathrm{H_2O}5H2​O, which requires 13 oxygen atoms on the right. The ether already supplies one, so 6O26\mathrm{O_2}6O2​ supplies the other twelve: C4H10O(l)+6O2(g)→4CO2(g)+5H2O(l)\mathrm{C_4H_{10}O(l)+6O_2(g)\rightarrow 4CO_2(g)+5H_2O(l)}C4​H10​O(l)+6O2​(g)→4CO2​(g)+5H2​O(l).
  2. Part (b): set up the formation equation. ΔHrxn∘=[4ΔHf∘(CO2)+5ΔHf∘(H2O)]−[ΔHf∘(C4H10O)+6(0)]\Delta H^\circ_{\mathrm{rxn}}=[4\Delta H_f^\circ(\mathrm{CO_2})+5\Delta H_f^\circ(\mathrm{H_2O})]-[\Delta H_f^\circ(\mathrm{C_4H_{10}O})+6(0)]ΔHrxn∘​=[4ΔHf∘​(CO2​)+5ΔHf∘​(H2​O)]−[ΔHf∘​(C4​H10​O)+6(0)], with O2(g)\mathrm{O_2(g)}O2​(g) zero as the standard-state element.
  3. Sum the products. 4(−393.5)+5(−285.8)=−1574.0−1429.0=−3003.0 kJ4(-393.5)+5(-285.8)=-1574.0-1429.0=-3003.0\,\mathrm{kJ}4(−393.5)+5(−285.8)=−1574.0−1429.0=−3003.0kJ.
  4. Substitute the measured enthalpy. −2723.7=−3003.0−ΔHf∘(C4H10O)-2723.7=-3003.0-\Delta H_f^\circ(\mathrm{C_4H_{10}O})−2723.7=−3003.0−ΔHf∘​(C4​H10​O).
  5. Solve for the unknown. ΔHf∘(C4H10O)=−3003.0+2723.7=−279.3 kJ/mol\Delta H_f^\circ(\mathrm{C_4H_{10}O})=-3003.0+2723.7=-279.3\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}ΔHf∘​(C4​H10​O)=−3003.0+2723.7=−279.3kJ/mol.
  6. Takeaway. When one formation enthalpy is unknown, keep it as a symbol in the products-minus-reactants equation and solve algebraically; a wrong balance in part (a) ruins part (b).

  4. 측정값을 대입한다. −2723.7=−3003.0−ΔHf∘(C4H10O)-2723.7=-3003.0-\Delta H_f^\circ(\mathrm{C_4H_{10}O})−2723.7=−3003.0−ΔHf∘​(C4​H10​O).
