Ag–O₂ reaction: scaling E°, ΔG°, and K

Ag–O₂ 반응: E°, ΔG°, K의 스케일링

4Ag(s)+O2(g)+4H+(aq)→4Ag+(aq)+2H2O(l)\mathrm{4Ag(s)+O_2(g)+4H^+(aq)\rightarrow4Ag^+(aq)+2H_2O(l)}4Ag(s)+O2​(g)+4H+(aq)→4Ag+(aq)+2H2​O(l)

(b) The same reaction is written 2Ag(s)+12O2(g)+2H+(aq)→2Ag+(aq)+H2O(l)\mathrm{2Ag(s)+\tfrac12O_2(g)+2H^+(aq)\rightarrow2Ag^+(aq)+H_2O(l)}2Ag(s)+21​O2​(g)+2H+(aq)→2Ag+(aq)+H2​O(l). What are E∘E^\circE∘, ΔG∘\Delta G^\circΔG∘, and KKK now? Explain what changes and what does not.

Hints
  1. Count nnn from the balanced equation as written: four Ag atoms each lose one electron.
  2. E∘E^\circE∘ is intensive; ΔG∘\Delta G^\circΔG∘ is per mole of reaction as written, and KKK follows from ΔG∘\Delta G^\circΔG∘.
한글 힌트
  1. 쓰여진 균형식에서 nnn을 센다: Ag 원자 4개가 각각 전자 1개를 잃는다.
  2. E∘E^\circE∘는 세기 성질, ΔG∘\Delta G^\circΔG∘는 쓰여진 반응 1 mol당 값이며 KKK는 ΔG∘\Delta G^\circΔG∘에서 구한다.
Worked solution
Answer
(a) E∘=+0.43 VE^\circ=+0.43\,\mathrm{V}E∘=+0.43V; ΔG∘≈−166 kJ\Delta G^\circ\approx-166\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔG∘≈−166kJ; K≈1×1029K\approx1\times10^{29}K≈1×1029; (b) E∘=+0.43 VE^\circ=+0.43\,\mathrm{V}E∘=+0.43V (same); ΔG∘≈−83 kJ\Delta G^\circ\approx-83\,\mathrm{kJ}ΔG∘≈−83kJ; K≈3×1014K\approx3\times10^{14}K≈3×1014
  1. (a) Cell potential. Reduction: O2+4H++4e−→2H2O\mathrm{O_2+4H^++4e^-\rightarrow2H_2O}O2​+4H++4e−→2H2​O (+1.23+1.23+1.23). Oxidation: 4Ag→4Ag++4e−\mathrm{4Ag\rightarrow4Ag^++4e^-}4Ag→4Ag++4e− (Ered∘=+0.80E^\circ_{\mathrm{red}}=+0.80Ered∘​=+0.80). E∘=1.23−0.80=+0.43 VE^\circ=1.23-0.80=+0.43\,\mathrm{V}E∘=1.23−0.80=+0.43V, n=4n=4n=4.
  2. (a) Free energy. ΔG∘=−nFE∘=−(4)(96,485)(0.43)=−1.66×105 J/mol≈−166 kJ/mol\Delta G^\circ=-nFE^\circ=-(4)(96{,}485)(0.43)=-1.66\times10^{5}\,\mathrm{J/mol}\approx-166\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}ΔG∘=−nFE∘=−(4)(96,485)(0.43)=−1.66×105J/mol≈−166kJ/mol (the lecture rounds to about −170 kJ-170\,\mathrm{kJ}−170kJ).
  3. (a) Equilibrium constant. ln⁡K=−ΔG∘/RT=1.66×105/[(8.314)(298)]=67.0\ln K=-\Delta G^\circ/RT=1.66\times10^{5}/[(8.314)(298)]=67.0lnK=−ΔG∘/RT=1.66×105/[(8.314)(298)]=67.0, so K=e67.0≈1×1029K=e^{67.0}\approx1\times10^{29}K=e67.0≈1×1029.
  4. (b) What is the same. The half-reaction potentials have not changed, so E∘=+0.43 VE^\circ=+0.43\,\mathrm{V}E∘=+0.43V (intensive property).
  5. (b) What changes. Now n=2n=2n=2: ΔG∘=−(2)(96,485)(0.43)=−8.3×104 J/mol=−83 kJ/mol\Delta G^\circ=-(2)(96{,}485)(0.43)=-8.3\times10^{4}\,\mathrm{J/mol}=-83\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}ΔG∘=−(2)(96,485)(0.43)=−8.3×104J/mol=−83kJ/mol, half of the previous value because only half as much reaction is written.
  6. (b) New KKK. ln⁡K=8.3×104/(8.314×298)=33.5\ln K=8.3\times10^{4}/(8.314\times298)=33.5lnK=8.3×104/(8.314×298)=33.5, so K≈3×1014K\approx3\times10^{14}K≈3×1014 — the square root of the earlier KKK (K→K1/2K\rightarrow K^{1/2}K→K1/2).
  7. Takeaway. Scaling a reaction by a factor mmm leaves E∘E^\circE∘ unchanged but multiplies nnn and ΔG∘\Delta G^\circΔG∘ by mmm and raises KKK to the mmm-th power.
