Oxalate complexes of Co(II) and free Co²⁺ concentration

Co(II) 옥살산 착물과 자유 Co²⁺ 농도

Oxalate (C2O42−\mathrm{C_2O_4^{2-}}C2​O42−​, ox) forms [Co(C2O4)(H2O)4][\mathrm{Co(C_2O_4)(H_2O)_4}][Co(C2​O4​)(H2​O)4​] with Co(II).

(a) What is the most likely geometry of this complex?

(b) When three oxalate ligands coordinate to Co(II), what is the formula of the corresponding sodium salt?

(c) What types of isomerism are possible for the complex ion with three oxalate ligands?

(d) KfK_fKf​ for [Co(C2O4)3]4−[\mathrm{Co(C_2O_4)_3}]^{4-}[Co(C2​O4​)3​]4− is 5.0×1095.0\times10^{9}5.0×109; for [Co(o-phen)3]2+[\mathrm{Co(o\text{-}phen)_3}]^{2+}[Co(o-phen)3​]2+ it is 9×10199\times10^{19}9×1019. What can you conclude?

(e) Calculate the equilibrium concentration of free Co2+\mathrm{Co^{2+}}Co2+ in a solution that initially contains 0.040 M0.040\ \mathrm{M}0.040 M oxalate and 0.0010 M0.0010\ \mathrm{M}0.0010 M Co2+\mathrm{Co^{2+}}Co2+, assuming formation of [Co(C2O4)3]4−[\mathrm{Co(C_2O_4)_3}]^{4-}[Co(C2​O4​)3​]4−.
한글
옥살산 이온(C2O42−\mathrm{C_2O_4^{2-}}C2​O42−​, ox)은 Co(II)와 [Co(C2O4)(H2O)4][\mathrm{Co(C_2O_4)(H_2O)_4}][Co(C2​O4​)(H2​O)4​]를 만든다.

(a) 이 착물의 가장 가능성 높은 기하 구조는?

(b) 옥살산 이온 3개가 Co(II)에 배위할 때 해당 나트륨염의 화학식은?

(c) 옥살산 이온 3개를 가진 착이온에서 가능한 이성질체의 종류는?

(d) [Co(C2O4)3]4−[\mathrm{Co(C_2O_4)_3}]^{4-}[Co(C2​O4​)3​]4−의 KfK_fKf​는 5.0×1095.0\times10^{9}5.0×109, [Co(o-phen)3]2+[\mathrm{Co(o\text{-}phen)_3}]^{2+}[Co(o-phen)3​]2+의 KfK_fKf​는 9×10199\times10^{19}9×1019이다. 어떤 결론을 낼 수 있는가?

(e) 처음에 옥살산 이온 0.040 M0.040\ \mathrm{M}0.040 M과 Co2+\mathrm{Co^{2+}}Co2+ 0.0010 M0.0010\ \mathrm{M}0.0010 M을 포함한 용액에서 [Co(C2O4)3]4−[\mathrm{Co(C_2O_4)_3}]^{4-}[Co(C2​O4​)3​]4−가 생성된다고 가정할 때 평형의 자유 Co2+\mathrm{Co^{2+}}Co2+ 농도를 구하시오.

Hints
  1. Oxalate is bidentate; count donor atoms for the coordination number.
  2. Because KfK_fKf​ is huge and oxalate is in excess, assume essentially all Co2+\mathrm{Co^{2+}}Co2+ is complexed, then back-calculate the free [Co2+][\mathrm{Co^{2+}}][Co2+].
한글 힌트
  1. 옥살산 이온은 두자리 리간드이다. donor 원자 수로 배위수를 센다.
  2. KfK_fKf​가 매우 크고 옥살산 이온이 과량이므로 Co2+\mathrm{Co^{2+}}Co2+가 거의 모두 착물이 되었다고 가정한 뒤 자유 [Co2+][\mathrm{Co^{2+}}][Co2+]를 역으로 구한다.
Worked solution
Answer
(a) octahedral; (b) Na4[Co(C2O4)3]\mathrm{Na_4[Co(C_2O_4)_3]}Na4​[Co(C2​O4​)3​]; (c) optical isomers (enantiomers) only; (d) o-phen binds more strongly than oxalate; (e) [Co2+]≈4.0×10−9 M[\mathrm{Co^{2+}}]\approx4.0\times10^{-9}\ \mathrm{M}[Co2+]≈4.0×10−9 M
  1. (a) Geometry. 1×2+4×1=61\times2+4\times1=61×2+4×1=6 donor atoms → CN 6 → octahedral.
  2. (b) Salt. Three C2O42−\mathrm{C_2O_4^{2-}}C2​O42−​ and Co2+\mathrm{Co^{2+}}Co2+: +2+3(−2)=−4+2+3(-2)=-4+2+3(−2)=−4 → complex 4−4-4− → Na4[Co(C2O4)3]\mathrm{Na_4[Co(C_2O_4)_3]}Na4​[Co(C2​O4​)3​].
  3. (c) Isomerism. All three bidentate ligands are identical, so there are no geometric isomers; the propeller-shaped complex and its mirror image cannot be superimposed → optical isomers (a pair of enantiomers).
  4. (d) Compare KfK_fKf​. o-phen is also bidentate, but its KfK_fKf​ is about 101010^{10}1010 times larger → the neutral o-phen binds Co(II) much more strongly than oxalate (a stronger-field, more stable chelate).
  5. (e) Stoichiometry. Each Co2+\mathrm{Co^{2+}}Co2+ takes 3 ox: 3(0.0010)=0.0030 M3(0.0010)=0.0030\ \mathrm{M}3(0.0010)=0.0030 M used, so [ox]=0.040−0.0030=0.037 M[\mathrm{ox}]=0.040-0.0030=0.037\ \mathrm{M}[ox]=0.040−0.0030=0.037 M and [Co(ox)34−]≈0.0010 M[\mathrm{Co(ox)_3^{4-}}]\approx0.0010\ \mathrm{M}[Co(ox)34−​]≈0.0010 M.
  6. (e) Solve for free metal. [Co2+]=[Co(ox)34−]Kf[ox]3=1.0×10−3(5.0×109)(0.037)3=1.0×10−32.5×105≈4.0×10−9 M[\mathrm{Co^{2+}}]=\dfrac{[\mathrm{Co(ox)_3^{4-}}]}{K_f[\mathrm{ox}]^3}=\dfrac{1.0\times10^{-3}}{(5.0\times10^9)(0.037)^3}=\dfrac{1.0\times10^{-3}}{2.5\times10^{5}}\approx4.0\times10^{-9}\ \mathrm{M}[Co2+]=Kf​[ox]3[Co(ox)34−​]​=(5.0×109)(0.037)31.0×10−3​=2.5×1051.0×10−3​≈4.0×10−9 M.
  7. Takeaway. With large KfK_fKf​ assume complete reaction first; the free metal ion is whatever the equilibrium expression then gives.
