Worked solution
Answer
(a) 236 g236\,\mathrm{g}236g Cr; (b) 2.51 A2.51\,\mathrm{A}2.51A
  1. Part (a): charge. t=2.00 d=172 800 st=2.00\,\mathrm{d}=172\,800\,\mathrm{s}t=2.00d=172800s, so Q=It=(7.60 A)(172 800 s)=1.313×106 CQ=It=(7.60\,\mathrm{A})(172\,800\,\mathrm{s})=1.313\times10^{6}\,\mathrm{C}Q=It=(7.60A)(172800s)=1.313×106C.
  2. Part (a): moles of electrons and Cr. ne−=1.313×106/96 485=13.61 moln_{e^-}=1.313\times10^{6}/96\,485=13.61\,\mathrm{mol}ne−​=1.313×106/96485=13.61mol, so nCr=13.61/3=4.537 moln_{\mathrm{Cr}}=13.61/3=4.537\,\mathrm{mol}nCr​=13.61/3=4.537mol.
  3. Part (a): mass. m=(4.537)(52.00)=236 gm=(4.537)(52.00)=236\,\mathrm{g}m=(4.537)(52.00)=236g.
  4. Part (b): electrons needed. 0.250 mol Cr×3=0.750 mol e−0.250\,\mathrm{mol}\ \mathrm{Cr}\times3=0.750\,\mathrm{mol}\ e^-0.250mol Cr×3=0.750mol e−, so Q=(0.750)(96 485)=7.24×104 CQ=(0.750)(96\,485)=7.24\times10^{4}\,\mathrm{C}Q=(0.750)(96485)=7.24×104C.
  5. Part (b): current. t=8.00 h=28 800 st=8.00\,\mathrm{h}=28\,800\,\mathrm{s}t=8.00h=28800s, so I=Q/t=7.24×104/28 800=2.51 AI=Q/t=7.24\times10^{4}/28\,800=2.51\,\mathrm{A}I=Q/t=7.24×104/28800=2.51A.
  6. Takeaway. Faraday chain: amps × seconds → coulombs → mol e−e^-e− → mol metal → grams (and reverse for current).
