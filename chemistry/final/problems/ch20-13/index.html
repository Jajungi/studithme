Batteries and fuel cells: lead–acid, alkaline, Li-ion, H₂–O₂

Hints
  1. A discharging battery is a voltaic cell: anode = oxidation, cathode = reduction; series cells add voltages.
  2. Charging is electrolysis: the discharge reactions run in reverse.
한글 힌트
  1. 방전 중인 배터리는 볼타 전지이다: anode = 산화, cathode = 환원. 직렬 셀은 전압이 더해진다.
  2. 충전은 전기분해이며 방전 반응이 역으로 진행된다.

Six cells in series give 6×2.05=12.3 V6\times2.05=12.3\,\mathrm{V}6×2.05=12.3V (the “12 V” battery). The reactants and products are solids, so no separate half-cells are needed and the voltage stays nearly constant.
  2. (b) Alkaline battery. Zn is oxidized (0→+20\rightarrow+20→+2), so Zn is the reducing agent at the anode; Mn\mathrm{Mn}Mn goes +4→+3+4\rightarrow+3+4→+3 at the cathode.
  3. (b) Overall. Adding the half-reactions (the 2e−2e^-2e− cancel and 2 OH−2\,\mathrm{OH^-}2OH− cancel): Zn(s)+2MnO2(s)+2H2O(l)→Zn(OH)2(s)+2MnO(OH)(s)\mathrm{Zn(s)+2MnO_2(s)+2H_2O(l)\rightarrow Zn(OH)_2(s)+2MnO(OH)(s)}Zn(s)+2MnO2​(s)+2H2​O(l)→Zn(OH)2​(s)+2MnO(OH)(s). It is a primary cell (not rechargeable).
  4. (c) Fuel cell. 2H2+O2→2H2O\mathrm{2H_2+O_2\rightarrow2H_2O}2H2​+O2​→2H2​O is spontaneous (the reverse needs electrolysis). Anode: H2→2H++2e−\mathrm{H_2\rightarrow2H^++2e^-}H2​→2H++2e− (Ered∘=0E^\circ_{\mathrm{red}}=0Ered∘​=0); cathode: O2+4H++4e−→2H2O\mathrm{O_2+4H^++4e^-\rightarrow2H_2O}O2​+4H++4e−→2H2​O (+1.23+1.23+1.23). E∘=1.23−0=+1.23 VE^\circ=1.23-0=+1.23\,\mathrm{V}E∘=1.23−0=+1.23V. Unlike a battery, a fuel cell needs a continuous fuel supply.
  5. (d) Oxidation number of Co. In LiCoO2\mathrm{LiCoO_2}LiCoO2​: +1+x+2(−2)=0⇒x=+3+1+x+2(-2)=0\Rightarrow x=+3+1+x+2(−2)=0⇒x=+3.
  6. (d) Charge vs discharge. Charging removes Li+\mathrm{Li^+}Li+ from the cathode and returns it to graphite; Co is oxidized (oxidation number increases). Discharging reverses this and Co is reduced.
  7. (d) Separator. The porous membrane keeps the electrodes apart (preventing an internal short circuit) while letting ions pass; a short can cause overheating and thermal runaway.
  8. Takeaway. Discharge = voltaic (anode oxidizes, cathode reduces), charge = reverse; series cells add; fuel cells use continuously supplied fuel with E∘=1.23 VE^\circ=1.23\,\mathrm{V}E∘=1.23V for H2/O2\mathrm{H_2/O_2}H2​/O2​.
