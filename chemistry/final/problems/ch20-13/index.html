Lead–acid battery discharge stoichiometry

납축전지 방전의 화학량론

During discharge of a lead–acid battery, 300 g300\,\mathrm{g}300g of PbO2(s)\mathrm{PbO_2(s)}PbO2​(s) from the cathode is converted into PbSO4(s)\mathrm{PbSO_4(s)}PbSO4​(s).

(a) What mass of Pb(s)\mathrm{Pb(s)}Pb(s) is oxidized at the anode?

(b) How many coulombs are transferred from Pb to PbO2\mathrm{PbO_2}PbO2​?

(MPbO2=239.2 g/molM_{\mathrm{PbO_2}}=239.2\,\mathrm{g/mol}MPbO2​​=239.2g/mol, MPb=207.2 g/molM_{\mathrm{Pb}}=207.2\,\mathrm{g/mol}MPb​=207.2g/mol, F=96 485 C/molF=96\,485\,\mathrm{C/mol}F=96485C/mol)

Worked solution
Answer
(a) 260 g260\,\mathrm{g}260g Pb; (b) 2.42×105 C2.42\times10^{5}\,\mathrm{C}2.42×105C
  1. Write the half-reactions. Cathode: PbO2+4H++SO42−+2e−→PbSO4+2H2O\mathrm{PbO_2+4H^++SO_4^{2-}+2e^-\rightarrow PbSO_4+2H_2O}PbO2​+4H++SO42−​+2e−→PbSO4​+2H2​O. Anode: Pb+SO42−→PbSO4+2e−\mathrm{Pb+SO_4^{2-}\rightarrow PbSO_4+2e^-}Pb+SO42−​→PbSO4​+2e−.
  2. Moles of PbO2\mathrm{PbO_2}PbO2​. n=300/239.2=1.254 moln=300/239.2=1.254\,\mathrm{mol}n=300/239.2=1.254mol.
  3. Part (a): Pb oxidized. The ratio is 1:1, so nPb=1.254 moln_{\mathrm{Pb}}=1.254\,\mathrm{mol}nPb​=1.254mol and m=(1.254)(207.2)=260 gm=(1.254)(207.2)=260\,\mathrm{g}m=(1.254)(207.2)=260g.
  4. Part (b): electrons. Each mole of PbO2\mathrm{PbO_2}PbO2​ takes 2 mol of electrons, so ne−=2(1.254)=2.508 moln_{e^-}=2(1.254)=2.508\,\mathrm{mol}ne−​=2(1.254)=2.508mol.
  5. Part (b): charge. Q=(2.508 mol)(96 485 C/mol)=2.42×105 CQ=(2.508\,\mathrm{mol})(96\,485\,\mathrm{C/mol})=2.42\times10^{5}\,\mathrm{C}Q=(2.508mol)(96485C/mol)=2.42×105C.
  6. Takeaway. Use the half-reaction mole ratio, then Q=ne−FQ=n_{e^-}FQ=ne−​F.
