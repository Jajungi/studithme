Hints
  1. Chain: current × time →\rightarrow→ coulombs →\rightarrow→ mol e−e^-e− (÷F\div F÷F) →\rightarrow→ mol metal (÷\div÷ electrons per ion) →\rightarrow→ grams.
  2. Convert hours to seconds first; C=A⋅s\mathrm{C}=\mathrm{A\cdot s}C=A⋅s.
한글 힌트
  1. 사슬: 전류 × 시간 →\rightarrow→ 쿨롱 →\rightarrow→ mol e−e^-e− (÷F\div F÷F) →\rightarrow→ mol 금속 (÷\div÷ 이온당 전자 수) →\rightarrow→ g.
  2. 시간을 먼저 초로 바꾼다. C=A⋅s\mathrm{C}=\mathrm{A\cdot s}C=A⋅s.
Worked solution
Answer
(a) 3.36 g Al3.36\,\mathrm{g\ Al}3.36g Al; (b) t≈3.7×103 st\approx3.7\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{s}t≈3.7×103s (≈62 min\approx62\,\mathrm{min}≈62min); (c) M =52.0 g/mol=52.0\,\mathrm{g/mol}=52.0g/mol, Cr
  1. (a) Charge. Q=It=(10.0 C/s)(1.00 h)(3600 s/h)=3.60×104 CQ=It=(10.0\,\mathrm{C/s})(1.00\,\mathrm{h})(3600\,\mathrm{s/h})=3.60\times10^{4}\,\mathrm{C}Q=It=(10.0C/s)(1.00h)(3600s/h)=3.60×104C.
  2. (a) Moles and mass. ne−=3.60×104/96,485=0.373 moln_{e^-}=3.60\times10^{4}/96{,}485=0.373\,\mathrm{mol}ne−​=3.60×104/96,485=0.373mol. Al3++3e−→Al\mathrm{Al^{3+}+3e^-\rightarrow Al}Al3++3e−→Al gives 0.373/3=0.124 mol Al0.373/3=0.124\,\mathrm{mol\ Al}0.373/3=0.124mol Al, so m=0.124×26.98=3.36 gm=0.124\times26.98=3.36\,\mathrm{g}m=0.124×26.98=3.36g.
  3. (b) Reverse the chain. 1.0 g/52.00=0.0192 mol Cr1.0\,\mathrm{g}/52.00=0.0192\,\mathrm{mol\ Cr}1.0g/52.00=0.0192mol Cr. Cr3++3e−→Cr\mathrm{Cr^{3+}+3e^-\rightarrow Cr}Cr3++3e−→Cr needs 3×0.0192=0.0577 mol e−3\times0.0192=0.0577\,\mathrm{mol}\,e^-3×0.0192=0.0577mole−.
  4. (b) Charge and time. Q=(0.0577)(96,485)=5.57×103 CQ=(0.0577)(96{,}485)=5.57\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{C}Q=(0.0577)(96,485)=5.57×103C; t=Q/I=5.57×103/1.5=3.7×103 s≈62 mint=Q/I=5.57\times10^{3}/1.5=3.7\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{s}\approx62\,\mathrm{min}t=Q/I=5.57×103/1.5=3.7×103s≈62min.
  5. (c) Moles of electrons. ne−=3610/96,485=0.0374 moln_{e^-}=3610/96{,}485=0.0374\,\mathrm{mol}ne−​=3610/96,485=0.0374mol.
  6. (c) Moles and molar mass. M3++3e−→M\mathrm{M^{3+}+3e^-\rightarrow M}M3++3e−→M gives 0.0374/3=0.01247 mol M0.0374/3=0.01247\,\mathrm{mol\ M}0.0374/3=0.01247mol M; M=0.648/0.01247=52.0 g/molM=0.648/0.01247=52.0\,\mathrm{g/mol}M=0.648/0.01247=52.0g/mol, which is chromium.
  7. Takeaway. Treat electrons as a reagent: It/FIt/FIt/F gives mol e−e^-e−, the half-reaction gives the ratio, and molar mass converts to grams; use seconds and the number of electrons per ion.
