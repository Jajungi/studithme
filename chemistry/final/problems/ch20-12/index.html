Copper concentration cell

구리 농도차 전지

A voltaic cell is constructed with two Cu2+\mathrm{Cu^{2+}}Cu2+–Cu\mathrm{Cu}Cu half-cells. The concentration of Cu2+\mathrm{Cu^{2+}}Cu2+ is 0.100 M0.100\,\mathrm{M}0.100M in one and 1.00×10−4 M1.00\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm{M}1.00×10−4M in the other.

(a) Which half-cell is the cathode?

(b) What is the standard emf of the cell?

(c) What is the cell emf at 298 K298\,\mathrm{K}298K?

(d) For each half-cell, predict whether [Cu2+][\mathrm{Cu^{2+}}][Cu2+] will increase, decrease, or stay the same as the cell operates.
