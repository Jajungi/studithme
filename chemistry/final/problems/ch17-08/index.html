Equivalence-point pH of three titrations

세 적정의 당량점 pH 예측

Predict whether the equivalence point of each of the following titrations is below, above or at pH 777:

(a) NaHCO3\mathrm{NaHCO_3}NaHCO3​ titrated with NaOH

(b) NH3\mathrm{NH_3}NH3​ titrated with HCl

(c) KOH titrated with HBr
한글
다음 각 적정의 당량점이 pH 777보다 낮은지, 높은지, 같은지 예측하시오.

(a) NaHCO3\mathrm{NaHCO_3}NaHCO3​를 NaOH로 적정

(b) NH3\mathrm{NH_3}NH3​를 HCl로 적정

(c) KOH를 HBr로 적정

Hints
  1. Identify the species present at equivalence, then ask whether it is a weak acid, a weak base, or a neutral ion.
한글 힌트
  1. 당량점에 있는 화학종이 약산, 약염기, 중성 이온 중 무엇인지 따진다.
Worked solution
Answer
(a) above 777; (b) below 777; (c) at 777
  1. Part (a): species at equivalence. HCO3−+OH−→CO32−+H2O\mathrm{HCO_3^-+OH^-\rightarrow CO_3^{2-}+H_2O}HCO3−​+OH−→CO32−​+H2​O. The solution contains CO32−\mathrm{CO_3^{2-}}CO32−​, the conjugate base of the weak acid HCO3−\mathrm{HCO_3^-}HCO3−​.
  2. Part (a): decide. CO32−+H2O⇌HCO3−+OH−\mathrm{CO_3^{2-}+H_2O\rightleftharpoons HCO_3^-+OH^-}CO32−​+H2​O⇌HCO3−​+OH− produces OH−\mathrm{OH^-}OH−, so pH>7\mathrm{pH}>7pH>7 (above).
  3. Part (b): species at equivalence. NH3+H+→NH4+\mathrm{NH_3+H^+\rightarrow NH_4^+}NH3​+H+→NH4+​. The solution contains NH4+\mathrm{NH_4^+}NH4+​, a weak acid.
  4. Part (b): decide. NH4+\mathrm{NH_4^+}NH4+​ gives H3O+\mathrm{H_3O^+}H3​O+, so pH<7\mathrm{pH}<7pH<7 (below).
  5. Part (c): species at equivalence. KBr only; K+\mathrm{K^+}K+ and Br−\mathrm{Br^-}Br− come from a strong base and a strong acid and do not hydrolyze, so pH=7\mathrm{pH}=7pH=7 (at).
  6. Takeaway. Weak base titrated → acidic equivalence; weak acid titrated → basic equivalence; strong/strong → neutral.
한글 답
(a) 777보다 높음; (b) 777보다 낮음; (c) 777
한글 해설
  1. (a) 당량점의 화학종. HCO3−+OH−→CO32−+H2O\mathrm{HCO_3^-+OH^-\rightarrow CO_3^{2-}+H_2O}HCO3−​+OH−→CO32−​+H2​O. 용액에는 약산 HCO3−\mathrm{HCO_3^-}HCO3−​의 짝염기 CO32−\mathrm{CO_3^{2-}}CO32−​가 있다.
  2. (a) 판정. CO32−+H2O⇌HCO3−+OH−\mathrm{CO_3^{2-}+H_2O\rightleftharpoons HCO_3^-+OH^-}CO32−​+H2​O⇌HCO3−​+OH−로 OH−\mathrm{OH^-}OH−가 생기므로 pH>7\mathrm{pH}>7pH>7 (높다).
  3. (b) 당량점의 화학종. NH3+H+→NH4+\mathrm{NH_3+H^+\rightarrow NH_4^+}NH3​+H+→NH4+​. 용액에는 약산 NH4+\mathrm{NH_4^+}NH4+​가 있다.
  4. (b) 판정. NH4+\mathrm{NH_4^+}NH4+​는 H3O+\mathrm{H_3O^+}H3​O+를 내므로 pH<7\mathrm{pH}<7pH<7 (낮다).
  5. (c) 당량점의 화학종. KBr만 있다. K+\mathrm{K^+}K+와 Br−\mathrm{Br^-}Br−는 강염기와 강산에서 유래하여 가수분해하지 않으므로 pH=7\mathrm{pH}=7pH=7 (같다).
