Flux through a disk and an annulus around a solenoid axis

솔레노이드 축 둘레 원판과 환을 지나는 자기 선속

A solenoid of radius r=1.25 cmr=1.25\,\mathrm{cm}r=1.25cm and length ℓ=30.0 cm\ell=30.0\,\mathrm{cm}ℓ=30.0cm has 300300300 turns and carries 12.0 A12.0\,\mathrm{A}12.0A. (a) Calculate the flux through the surface of a disk-shaped area of radius R=5.00 cmR=5.00\,\mathrm{cm}R=5.00cm that is positioned perpendicular to and centered on the axis of the solenoid (the disk surrounds the solenoid at its middle). (b) The end view of the same solenoid shows an annulus with inner radius a=0.400 cma=0.400\,\mathrm{cm}a=0.400cm and outer radius b=0.800 cmb=0.800\,\mathrm{cm}b=0.800cm. Calculate the flux through this annulus. Treat the solenoid as ideal and use μ0=4π×10−7 T m/A\mu_0=4\pi\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}μ0​=4π×10−7Tm/A.
한글
반지름 r=1.25 cmr=1.25\,\mathrm{cm}r=1.25cm, 길이 ℓ=30.0 cm\ell=30.0\,\mathrm{cm}ℓ=30.0cm인 솔레노이드가 300300300번 감겨 있고 12.0 A12.0\,\mathrm{A}12.0A가 흐른다. (a) 솔레노이드 축에 수직이고 중심이 축에 놓인 반지름 R=5.00 cmR=5.00\,\mathrm{cm}R=5.00cm 원판(솔레노이드 중간을 둘러쌈)을 지나는 선속을 구하라. (b) 같은 솔레노이드의 끝 쪽 단면에서 안 반지름 a=0.400 cma=0.400\,\mathrm{cm}a=0.400cm, 바깥 반지름 b=0.800 cmb=0.800\,\mathrm{cm}b=0.800cm인 환(annulus)을 지나는 선속을 구하라. 이상적 솔레노이드로 보고 μ0=4π×10−7 T m/A\mu_0=4\pi\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}μ0​=4π×10−7Tm/A.

Hints
  1. First find the interior field B=μ0nIB=\mu_0 nIB=μ0​nI with n=N/ℓn=N/\elln=N/ℓ.
  2. For an ideal solenoid B=0B=0B=0 outside, so a larger disk only collects flux over the solenoid's own cross-sectional area.
  3. The annulus lies entirely inside the winding: its area is π(b2−a2)\pi(b^{2}-a^{2})π(b2−a2).
한글 힌트
  1. 먼저 안쪽 장 B=μ0nIB=\mu_0 nIB=μ0​nI, n=N/ℓn=N/\elln=N/ℓ.
  2. 이상적 솔레노이드 밖은 B=0B=0B=0이므로 더 큰 원판은 솔레노이드 자신의 단면적에서만 선속을 얻는다.
  3. 환은 전부 코일 안쪽에 있다: 넓이 π(b2−a2)\pi(b^{2}-a^{2})π(b2−a2).
Worked solution
Answer
(a) 7.40×10−6 Wb7.40\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{Wb}7.40×10−6Wb; (b) 2.27×10−6 Wb2.27\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{Wb}2.27×10−6Wb
  1. Concept. Ampère's law with a rectangular loop gives the ideal solenoid field B=μ0nIB=\mu_0 nIB=μ0​nI inside and 000 outside. Flux is Φ=∫B⃗⋅dA⃗\Phi=\int\vec B\cdot d\vec AΦ=∫B⋅dA over the region where B≠0B\ne0B=0.
  2. Interior field. n=Nℓ=3000.300 m=1.00×103 m−1n=\dfrac{N}{\ell}=\dfrac{300}{0.300\,\mathrm{m}}=1.00\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}n=ℓN​=0.300m300​=1.00×103m−1, so B=μ0nI=(4π×10−7)(1.00×103)(12.0)=1.51×10−2 TB=\mu_0 nI=(4\pi\times10^{-7})(1.00\times10^{3})(12.0)=1.51\times10^{-2}\,\mathrm{T}B=μ0​nI=(4π×10−7)(1.00×103)(12.0)=1.51×10−2T along the axis.
  3. (a) Disk of radius R=5.00 cmR=5.00\,\mathrm{cm}R=5.00cm. The disk is larger than the solenoid. Outside the winding B=0B=0B=0, so only the part of the disk within r=1.25 cmr=1.25\,\mathrm{cm}r=1.25cm contributes: Φ=B πr2=(1.508×10−2)π(0.0125)2=(1.508×10−2)(4.909×10−4)=7.40×10−6 Wb\Phi=B\,\pi r^{2}=(1.508\times10^{-2})\pi(0.0125)^{2}=(1.508\times10^{-2})(4.909\times10^{-4})=7.40\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{Wb}Φ=Bπr2=(1.508×10−2)π(0.0125)2=(1.508×10−2)(4.909×10−4)=7.40×10−6Wb.
  4. (b) Annulus a<ρ<ba<\rho<ba<ρ<b. Entirely inside the solenoid, area π(b2−a2)=π[(0.800×10−2)2−(0.400×10−2)2]=π(4.80×10−5)=1.508×10−4 m2\pi(b^{2}-a^{2})=\pi\left[(0.800\times10^{-2})^{2}-(0.400\times10^{-2})^{2}\right]=\pi(4.80\times10^{-5})=1.508\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^{2}}π(b2−a2)=π[(0.800×10−2)2−(0.400×10−2)2]=π(4.80×10−5)=1.508×10−4m2. So Φ=B A=(1.508×10−2)(1.508×10−4)=2.27×10−6 Wb\Phi=B\,A=(1.508\times10^{-2})(1.508\times10^{-4})=2.27\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{Wb}Φ=BA=(1.508×10−2)(1.508×10−4)=2.27×10−6Wb.
  5. Check. The annulus flux is (b2−a2)/r2=4.8×10−5/1.5625×10−4≈0.307(b^{2}-a^{2})/r^{2}=4.8\times10^{-5}/1.5625\times10^{-4}\approx0.307(b2−a2)/r2=4.8×10−5/1.5625×10−4≈0.307 of the total flux through the solenoid cross-section, 2.27/7.40=0.3072.27/7.40=0.3072.27/7.40=0.307. ✓
  6. Pitfall check. Do not use πR2\pi R^{2}πR2 with R=5.00 cmR=5.00\,\mathrm{cm}R=5.00cm in part (a): the field is zero outside the winding, so that would overcount the flux by a factor of 161616.
  7. Final answer. (a) Φ≈7.40 μWb\Phi\approx7.40\,\mu\mathrm{Wb}Φ≈7.40μWb; (b) Φ≈2.27 μWb\Phi\approx2.27\,\mu\mathrm{Wb}Φ≈2.27μWb.
