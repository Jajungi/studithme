Current fractions in an iron water pipe

철 수도관: 철·물 전류 분율

An iron water pipe has outside diameter 2.5 cm2.5\,\mathrm{cm}2.5cm and inside diameter 2.0 cm2.0\,\mathrm{cm}2.0cm. It grounds an appliance: steady current I=20 AI = 20\,\mathrm{A}I=20A enters the pipe. Water inside has resistivity 0.01 Ω⋅m0.01\,\Omega\cdot\mathrm{m}0.01Ω⋅m; iron has ρiron=10×10−8 Ω⋅m\rho_\mathrm{iron} = 10\times 10^{-8}\,\Omega\cdot\mathrm{m}ρiron​=10×10−8Ω⋅m. Treat the iron shell and water core as two resistors in parallel along the same pipe length.

What fraction of the 20 A20\,\mathrm{A}20A flows in the iron? What fraction in the water?

Worked solution
Answer
Iron ≈99.998%\approx 99.998\%≈99.998% (Iiron≈20.0 AI_\mathrm{iron}\approx 20.0\,\mathrm{A}Iiron​≈20.0A); water ≈0.0018%\approx 0.0018\%≈0.0018% (Iw≈3.6×10−4 AI_\mathrm{w}\approx 3.6\times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{A}Iw​≈3.6×10−4A)
  1. Goal. Split I=20 AI=20\,\mathrm{A}I=20A between the conducting iron annulus and the water-filled core using parallel resistance.
  2. Radii. Outside diameter 2.5 cm⇒rout=1.25 cm=1.25×10−2 m2.5\,\mathrm{cm}\Rightarrow r_\mathrm{out}=1.25\,\mathrm{cm}=1.25\times 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m}2.5cm⇒rout​=1.25cm=1.25×10−2m. Inside diameter 2.0 cm⇒rin=1.00×10−2 m2.0\,\mathrm{cm}\Rightarrow r_\mathrm{in}=1.00\times 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m}2.0cm⇒rin​=1.00×10−2m.
  3. Areas. Airon=π(rout2−rin2)≈π(1.5625−1.00)×10−4 m2≈1.77×10−4 m2A_\mathrm{iron}=\pi(r_\mathrm{out}^2-r_\mathrm{in}^2)\approx \pi(1.5625-1.00)\times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}\approx 1.77\times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}Airon​=π(rout2​−rin2​)≈π(1.5625−1.00)×10−4m2≈1.77×10−4m2. Aw=πrin2≈3.14×10−4 m2A_\mathrm{w}=\pi r_\mathrm{in}^2\approx 3.14\times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}Aw​=πrin2​≈3.14×10−4m2.
  4. Resistance per unit length. R′=ρ/AR'=\rho/AR′=ρ/A, so for the same length the conductance ratio is Giron/Gw=(Airon/ρiron)/(Aw/ρw)G_\mathrm{iron}/G_\mathrm{w}=(A_\mathrm{iron}/\rho_\mathrm{iron})/(A_\mathrm{w}/\rho_\mathrm{w})Giron​/Gw​=(Airon​/ρiron​)/(Aw​/ρw​).
  5. Why iron carries almost everything. ρw/ρiron∼106\rho_\mathrm{w}/\rho_\mathrm{iron}\sim 10^6ρw​/ρiron​∼106 even though water’s area is larger; iron’s conductivity dominates.
  6. Current fractions. With parallel elements, Iiron/I=Giron/(Giron+Gw)≈0.99998I_\mathrm{iron}/I=G_\mathrm{iron}/(G_\mathrm{iron}+G_\mathrm{w})\approx 0.99998Iiron​/I=Giron​/(Giron​+Gw​)≈0.99998 and Iw/I≈1.8×10−5I_\mathrm{w}/I\approx 1.8\times 10^{-5}Iw​/I≈1.8×10−5.
  7. Pitfall check. Do not treat the pipe as a single solid iron cylinder—current can shunt through the water. Use parallel RRR, not series.
  8. Final answer. Iron: ≈99.998%\approx 99.998\%≈99.998% of 20 A20\,\mathrm{A}20A (≈20.0 A\approx 20.0\,\mathrm{A}≈20.0A); water: ≈0.0018%\approx 0.0018\%≈0.0018% (≈3.6×10−4 A\approx 3.6\times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{A}≈3.6×10−4A).
