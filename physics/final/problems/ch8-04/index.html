Readings of an ideal ammeter and voltmeter

이상 전류계·전압계의 읽음값

The circuit shown is a ladder between a left vertical wire LLL and a right vertical wire RtRtRt, with four rungs.

• Top rung: an ideal ammeter in series with 6.00 Ω6.00\,\Omega6.00Ω.
• Second rung: 10.0 Ω10.0\,\Omega10.0Ω from LLL to node mmm, then a 6.00 V6.00\,\mathrm{V}6.00V battery from mmm to RtRtRt with its +++ terminal toward mmm.
• Third rung: 5.00 Ω5.00\,\Omega5.00Ω from LLL to node nnn, then a 4.50 V4.50\,\mathrm{V}4.50V battery from nnn to RtRtRt with its +++ terminal toward nnn.
• Bottom rung: 6.00 Ω6.00\,\Omega6.00Ω alone.

An ideal voltmeter is connected in the vertical branch between mmm and nnn. What are the expected readings of (a) the ammeter and (b) the voltmeter?
한글

• 맨 위: 이상 전류계와 6.00 Ω6.00\,\Omega6.00Ω의 직렬.
• 두 번째: LLL에서 10.0 Ω10.0\,\Omega10.0Ω을 지나 노드 mmm, 이어서 +++단자가 mmm을 향하는 6.00 V6.00\,\mathrm{V}6.00V 전지를 지나 RtRtRt.
• 세 번째: LLL에서 5.00 Ω5.00\,\Omega5.00Ω을 지나 노드 nnn, 이어서 +++단자가 nnn을 향하는 4.50 V4.50\,\mathrm{V}4.50V 전지를 지나 RtRtRt.
• 맨 아래: 6.00 Ω6.00\,\Omega6.00Ω 하나.

이상 전압계가 mmm과 nnn을 잇는 수직 가지에 연결되어 있다. (a) 전류계와 (b) 전압계의 읽음값은 각각 얼마인가?

  1. Goal. Predict the two meter readings for a four-rung ladder driven by two batteries.
  2. Idealize the meters first. The ammeter has zero resistance, so the top rung is simply the 6.00 Ω6.00\,\Omega6.00Ω resistor, and the meter reads that rung’s current. The voltmeter has infinite resistance, so no current flows between mmm and nnn; the bridge branch does not disturb the rest of the circuit.
  3. Part (b) needs no algebra. Each battery is ideal, so it holds a fixed potential difference regardless of its current: Vm=VRt+6.00 VV_m=V_{Rt}+6.00\,\mathrm{V}Vm​=VRt​+6.00V and Vn=VRt+4.50 VV_n=V_{Rt}+4.50\,\mathrm{V}Vn​=VRt​+4.50V.
