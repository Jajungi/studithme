Charging an initially uncharged capacitor

처음 방전된 축전기의 충전

An initially uncharged capacitor C=3.0 μFC=3.0\,\mu\mathrm{F}C=3.0μF is connected in series with a resistor R=2.0 MΩR=2.0\,\mathrm{M}\OmegaR=2.0MΩ and an ideal battery ε=12 V\varepsilon=12\,\mathrm{V}ε=12V at t=0t=0t=0. Find (a) the time constant, (b) the charge on the capacitor, and (c) the current at t=12 st=12\,\mathrm{s}t=12s.
한글
처음 전하가 없는 축전기 C=3.0 μFC=3.0\,\mu\mathrm{F}C=3.0μF를 저항 R=2.0 MΩR=2.0\,\mathrm{M}\OmegaR=2.0MΩ, 이상적 전지 ε=12 V\varepsilon=12\,\mathrm{V}ε=12V와 직렬로 연결하고 t=0t=0t=0에 스위치를 닫는다. (a) 시정수, (b) t=12 st=12\,\mathrm{s}t=12s에서 축전기 전하, (c) 그때 전류를 구하라.

Hints
  1. For charging, q(t)=Cε(1−e−t/RC)q(t)=C\varepsilon(1-e^{-t/RC})q(t)=Cε(1−e−t/RC).
  2. The charging current is I(t)=(ε/R)e−t/RCI(t)=(\varepsilon/R)e^{-t/RC}I(t)=(ε/R)e−t/RC.
  3. MΩ⋅μF\mathrm{M}\Omega\cdot\mu\mathrm{F}MΩ⋅μF has units of seconds.
한글 힌트
  1. 충전할 때 q(t)=Cε(1−e−t/RC)q(t)=C\varepsilon(1-e^{-t/RC})q(t)=Cε(1−e−t/RC).
  2. 충전 전류는 I(t)=(ε/R)e−t/RCI(t)=(\varepsilon/R)e^{-t/RC}I(t)=(ε/R)e−t/RC.
  3. MΩ⋅μF\mathrm{M}\Omega\cdot\mu\mathrm{F}MΩ⋅μF의 단위는 초이다.
Worked solution
Answer
τ=6.0 s\tau=6.0\,\mathrm{s}τ=6.0s; q(12 s)≈31.1 μCq(12\,\mathrm{s})\approx31.1\,\mu\mathrm{C}q(12s)≈31.1μC; I(12 s)≈0.812 μAI(12\,\mathrm{s})\approx0.812\,\mu\mathrm{A}I(12s)≈0.812μA
  1. Goal. Evaluate the charging time scale, capacitor charge, and circuit current at the specified time.
  2. Knowns. R=2.0×106 ΩR=2.0\times10^6\,\OmegaR=2.0×106Ω, C=3.0×10−6 FC=3.0\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{F}C=3.0×10−6F, ε=12 V\varepsilon=12\,\mathrm{V}ε=12V, and t=12 st=12\,\mathrm{s}t=12s.
  3. Time constant. τ=RC=(2.0×106)(3.0×10−6) s=6.0 s\tau=RC=(2.0\times10^6)(3.0\times10^{-6})\,\mathrm{s}=6.0\,\mathrm{s}τ=RC=(2.0×106)(3.0×10−6)s=6.0s, so t/τ=2.0t/\tau=2.0t/τ=2.0.
  4. Final charge scale. As t→∞t\to\inftyt→∞, q∞=Cε=(3.0 μF)(12 V)=36 μCq_\infty=C\varepsilon=(3.0\,\mu\mathrm{F})(12\,\mathrm{V})=36\,\mu\mathrm{C}q∞​=Cε=(3.0μF)(12V)=36μC.
  5. Charge at 12 s12\,\mathrm{s}12s. q=q∞(1−e−2)=36(1−e−2) μC≈31.1 μCq=q_\infty(1-e^{-2})=36(1-e^{-2})\,\mu\mathrm{C}\approx31.1\,\mu\mathrm{C}q=q∞​(1−e−2)=36(1−e−2)μC≈31.1μC.
  6. Current at 12 s12\,\mathrm{s}12s. I=(ε/R)e−2=(12/(2.0×106))e−2 A≈0.812 μAI=(\varepsilon/R)e^{-2}=(12/(2.0\times10^6))e^{-2}\,\mathrm{A}\approx0.812\,\mu\mathrm{A}I=(ε/R)e−2=(12/(2.0×106))e−2A≈0.812μA.
  7. Pitfall check. During charging, qqq rises toward CεC\varepsilonCε while III falls toward zero. Do not use the discharging form q=q0e−t/RCq=q_0e^{-t/RC}q=q0​e−t/RC here.
  8. Final answer. τ=6.0 s\tau=6.0\,\mathrm{s}τ=6.0s, q(12 s)≈31.1 μCq(12\,\mathrm{s})\approx31.1\,\mu\mathrm{C}q(12s)≈31.1μC, and I(12 s)≈0.812 μAI(12\,\mathrm{s})\approx0.812\,\mu\mathrm{A}I(12s)≈0.812μA.
