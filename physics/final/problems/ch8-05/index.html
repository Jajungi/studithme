Discharging a charged capacitor

충전된 축전기의 방전

A 10 μF10\,\mu\mathrm{F}10μF capacitor initially holds charge q0=100 μCq_0=100\,\mu\mathrm{C}q0​=100μC. The battery is removed and the capacitor discharges through a 5.0 MΩ5.0\,\mathrm{M}\Omega5.0MΩ resistor. Find (a) the time constant, (b) the charge remaining after 75 s75\,\mathrm{s}75s, and (c) the magnitude of the discharge current then.

Answer

  1. Goal. Find the exponential decay time, remaining charge, and discharge-current magnitude.
  2. Knowns. C=10×10−6 FC=10\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{F}C=10×10−6F, R=5.0×106 ΩR=5.0\times10^6\,\OmegaR=5.0×106Ω, q0=100 μCq_0=100\,\mu\mathrm{C}q0​=100μC, and t=75 st=75\,\mathrm{s}t=75s.
  3. Time constant. τ=RC=(5.0×106)(10×10−6) s=50 s\tau=RC=(5.0\times10^6)(10\times10^{-6})\,\mathrm{s}=50\,\mathrm{s}τ=RC=(5.0×106)(10×10−6)s=50s, hence t/τ=1.5t/\tau=1.5t/τ=1.5.
  4. Remaining charge. q(75 s)=100e−1.5 μC≈22.3 μCq(75\,\mathrm{s})=100e^{-1.5}\,\mu\mathrm{C}\approx22.3\,\mu\mathrm{C}q(75s)=100e−1.5μC≈22.3μC.
  5. Initial-current scale. V0=q0/C=10 VV_0=q_0/C=10\,\mathrm{V}V0​=q0​/C=10V, so I0=V0/R=2.0 μAI_0=V_0/R=2.0\,\mu\mathrm{A}I0​=V0​/R=2.0μA.
  6. Current at 75 s75\,\mathrm{s}75s. I(t)=−I0e−t/τI(t)=-I_0e^{-t/\tau}I(t)=−I0​e−t/τ for the original charging-current sign convention. Thus ∣I∣=(2.0)e−1.5 μA≈0.446 μA|I|=(2.0)e^{-1.5}\,\mu\mathrm{A}\approx0.446\,\mu\mathrm{A}∣I∣=(2.0)e−1.5μA≈0.446μA.
  7. Pitfall check. The minus sign describes current direction; it does not make the current magnitude negative. Charge and current share the same exponential factor but have different units.
  8. Final answer. τ=50 s\tau=50\,\mathrm{s}τ=50s, q(75 s)≈22.3 μCq(75\,\mathrm{s})\approx22.3\,\mu\mathrm{C}q(75s)≈22.3μC, and ∣I(75 s)∣≈0.446 μA|I(75\,\mathrm{s})|\approx0.446\,\mu\mathrm{A}∣I(75s)∣≈0.446μA.
