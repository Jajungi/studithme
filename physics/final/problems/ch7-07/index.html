Drift time for one electron along a copper line

구리 송전선: 전자 드리프트로 200 km

A 200 km200\,\mathrm{km}200km copper transmission line of diameter 2.00 cm2.00\,\mathrm{cm}2.00cm carries steady current I=1000 AI = 1000\,\mathrm{A}I=1000A. The conduction-electron density is n=8.50×1028 m−3n = 8.50\times 10^{28}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}n=8.50×1028m−3. How many years does it take an electron to drift the full length of the cable?
한글

Hints
  1. Macroscopic link: I=neAvdI = n e A v_dI=neAvd​ for electrons (magnitude).
  2. Drift time t=L/vdt = L/v_dt=L/vd​; convert seconds to years (≈3.16×107 s/yr\approx 3.16\times 10^7\,\mathrm{s/yr}≈3.16×107s/yr).
  3. Diameter 2.00 cm2.00\,\mathrm{cm}2.00cm gives the wire cross-section.
한글 힌트
  1. I=neAvdI = n e A v_dI=neAvd​ (크기).
  2. t=L/vdt = L/v_dt=L/vd​, 초 →\to→ 년.
  3. 지름 2.00 cm2.00\,\mathrm{cm}2.00cm로 AAA 계산.
Worked solution
Answer
t≈27 yearst \approx 27\,\mathrm{years}t≈27years
  1. Goal. Estimate how long net electron drift takes to traverse L=200 kmL=200\,\mathrm{km}L=200km at the drift speed implied by I=1000 AI=1000\,\mathrm{A}I=1000A.
  2. Knowns. L=2.0×105 mL=2.0\times 10^5\,\mathrm{m}L=2.0×105m, diameter 2.00 cm⇒r=1.00 cm2.00\,\mathrm{cm}\Rightarrow r=1.00\,\mathrm{cm}2.00cm⇒r=1.00cm, n=8.50×1028 m−3n=8.50\times 10^{28}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}n=8.50×1028m−3, e=1.60×10−19 Ce=1.60\times 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}e=1.60×10−19C, I=1000 AI=1000\,\mathrm{A}I=1000A.
  3. Cross-section. A=πr2=π(1.00×10−2)2 m2≈3.14×10−4 m2A=\pi r^2=\pi(1.00\times 10^{-2})^2\,\mathrm{m}^2\approx 3.14\times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}A=πr2=π(1.00×10−2)2m2≈3.14×10−4m2.
  4. Drift speed from current. Steady uniform flow: I=neAvdI=n e A v_dI=neAvd​, so vd=I/(neA)v_d=I/(n e A)vd​=I/(neA). This uses only the net drift, not the random thermal speed.
  5. Numerical vdv_dvd​. vd≈1000/[(8.50×1028)(1.60×10−19)(3.14×10−4)] m/s≈2.3×10−4 m/sv_d\approx 1000/[(8.50\times 10^{28})(1.60\times 10^{-19})(3.14\times 10^{-4})]\,\mathrm{m/s}\approx 2.3\times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m/s}vd​≈1000/[(8.50×1028)(1.60×10−19)(3.14×10−4)]m/s≈2.3×10−4m/s.
  6. Drift time. t=L/vd≈(2.0×105)/(2.3×10−4) s≈8.6×108 st=L/v_d\approx (2.0\times 10^5)/(2.3\times 10^{-4})\,\mathrm{s}\approx 8.6\times 10^8\,\mathrm{s}t=L/vd​≈(2.0×105)/(2.3×10−4)s≈8.6×108s.
  7. Convert to years. tyr=t/(365.25×24×3600)≈27 yrt_\mathrm{yr}=t/(365.25\times 24\times 3600)\approx 27\,\mathrm{yr}tyr​=t/(365.25×24×3600)≈27yr.
  8. Pitfall check. AC household current does not mean electrons travel around the circuit each cycle—this estimate is for steady DC drift only. The answer is decades, not milliseconds.
  9. Final answer. About 27 years27\,\mathrm{years}27years of net drift along the full 200 km200\,\mathrm{km}200km.
