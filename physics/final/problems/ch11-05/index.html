AC generator: emf of a rotating coil

교류 발전기: 회전 코일의 기전력

A rectangular coil of N=100N=100N=100 turns and area A=0.020 m2A=0.020\,\mathrm{m}^{2}A=0.020m2 rotates at 60 Hz60\,\mathrm{Hz}60Hz in a uniform field B=0.30 TB=0.30\,\mathrm{T}B=0.30T. Let θ=ωt\theta=\omega tθ=ωt be the angle between the coil plane and B⃗\vec BB, so Φ=NABsin⁡ωt\Phi=NAB\sin\omega tΦ=NABsinωt. (a) Find ε(t)\varepsilon(t)ε(t) and the peak emf. (b) Find ε\varepsilonε at t=2.0 mst=2.0\,\mathrm{ms}t=2.0ms. (c) When is the flux maximum, and what is the emf at that moment? (d) The coil is connected to a total resistance of 50 Ω50\,\Omega50Ω; find the peak current. Describe Lenz's law for 0<ωt<π/20<\omega t<\pi/20<ωt<π/2.

(d) 전체 저항 50 Ω50\,\Omega50Ω에 연결했을 때 최대 전류는? 0<ωt<π/20<\omega t<\pi/20<ωt<π/2에서 렌츠 법칙을 설명하라.

Hints
  1. Convert fff to ω=2πf\omega=2\pi fω=2πf.
  2. ε=−dΦ/dt=−NABωcos⁡ωt\varepsilon=-d\Phi/dt=-NAB\omega\cos\omega tε=−dΦ/dt=−NABωcosωt.
  3. Maximum flux means dΦ/dt=0d\Phi/dt=0dΦ/dt=0.

  1. ω=2πf\omega=2\pi fω=2πf.
  2. ε=−dΦ/dt=−NABωcos⁡ωt\varepsilon=-d\Phi/dt=-NAB\omega\cos\omega tε=−dΦ/dt=−NABωcosωt.
  3. 선속 최대 ↔ dΦ/dt=0d\Phi/dt=0dΦ/dt=0.
Worked solution
Answer
(a) ε=−NABωcos⁡ωt\varepsilon=-NAB\omega\cos\omega tε=−NABωcosωt, εmax⁡=226 V\varepsilon_{\max}=226\,\mathrm{V}εmax​=226V; (b) ≈−165 V\approx-165\,\mathrm{V}≈−165V; (c) flux max at 4.2 ms4.2\,\mathrm{ms}4.2ms where ε=0\varepsilon=0ε=0; (d) Imax⁡=4.5 AI_{\max}=4.5\,\mathrm{A}Imax​=4.5A
  1. Concept. Faraday with a time-varying orientation: only θ\thetaθ changes, so Φ=NABsin⁡ωt\Phi=NAB\sin\omega tΦ=NABsinωt and ε=−N dΦ1/dt\varepsilon=-N\,d\Phi_1/dtε=−NdΦ1​/dt is a sinusoid.
  2. (a) Emf. ω=2π(60)=376.99 rad/s\omega=2\pi(60)=376.99\,\mathrm{rad/s}ω=2π(60)=376.99rad/s. ε(t)=−NABωcos⁡ωt\varepsilon(t)=-NAB\omega\cos\omega tε(t)=−NABωcosωt, and εmax⁡=NABω=(100)(0.020)(0.30)(376.99)=226 V\varepsilon_{\max}=NAB\omega=(100)(0.020)(0.30)(376.99)=226\,\mathrm{V}εmax​=NABω=(100)(0.020)(0.30)(376.99)=226V.
  3. (b) At t=2.0 mst=2.0\,\mathrm{ms}t=2.0ms. ωt=0.754 rad\omega t=0.754\,\mathrm{rad}ωt=0.754rad, cos⁡(0.754)=0.729\cos(0.754)=0.729cos(0.754)=0.729, so ε=−226(0.729)≈−165 V\varepsilon=-226(0.729)\approx-165\,\mathrm{V}ε=−226(0.729)≈−165V.
  4. (c) Flux maximum. sin⁡ωt=1⇒ωt=π/2\sin\omega t=1\Rightarrow\omega t=\pi/2sinωt=1⇒ωt=π/2, t=T/4=4.17 mst=T/4=4.17\,\mathrm{ms}t=T/4=4.17ms. At that moment cos⁡ωt=0\cos\omega t=0cosωt=0, so ε=0\varepsilon=0ε=0: the flux is momentarily constant, even though it is at its largest value (NAB=0.60 WbNAB=0.60\,\mathrm{Wb}NAB=0.60Wb).
  5. (d) Current and Lenz. Imax⁡=εmax⁡/R=226/50=4.5 AI_{\max}=\varepsilon_{\max}/R=226/50=4.5\,\mathrm{A}Imax​=εmax​/R=226/50=4.5A. For 0<ωt<π/20<\omega t<\pi/20<ωt<π/2 the flux is increasing, so the induced current sets up a field opposite to the applied B⃗\vec BB through the coil; after ωt=π/2\omega t=\pi/2ωt=π/2 the flux decreases and the induced field reinforces B⃗\vec BB (the current reverses).
  6. Pitfall check. Flux maximum is not emf maximum — they are 90∘90^{\circ}90∘ out of phase. Also, ω=2πf\omega=2\pi fω=2πf, not fff.
  7. Final answer. (a) ε=−NABωcos⁡ωt\varepsilon=-NAB\omega\cos\omega tε=−NABωcosωt, peak 226 V226\,\mathrm{V}226V; (b) −165 V-165\,\mathrm{V}−165V; (c) t=4.2 mst=4.2\,\mathrm{ms}t=4.2ms, ε=0\varepsilon=0ε=0; (d) 4.5 A4.5\,\mathrm{A}4.5A.
