Power surge on a 100-W lightbulb

100 W 전구: 서지 전력 증가율

A 100 W100\,\mathrm{W}100W bulb is connected to a 120 V120\,\mathrm{V}120V source. A surge briefly raises the voltage to 140 V140\,\mathrm{V}140V. Assuming the filament resistance does not change, by what percentage does the power output increase?
한글
100 W100\,\mathrm{W}100W 전구, 120 V120\,\mathrm{V}120V. 순간 140 V140\,\mathrm{V}140V 서지. 필라멘트 저항 불변일 때 전력 출력 몇 % 증가?

Hints
  1. At fixed RRR, power P=V2/RP=V^2/RP=V2/R scales with V2V^2V2.
  2. Percent increase: 100%×(Pnew/Pold−1)100\%\times (P_\mathrm{new}/P_\mathrm{old}-1)100%×(Pnew​/Pold​−1).
  3. You do not need to find RRR explicitly if you use the voltage ratio.
한글 힌트
  1. RRR 일정: P=V2/RP=V^2/RP=V2/R ∝V2\propto V^2∝V2.
  2. 증가율 100%×(Pnew/Pold−1)100\%\times(P_\mathrm{new}/P_\mathrm{old}-1)100%×(Pnew​/Pold​−1).
  3. RRR 없이 전압비만 써도 됨.
Worked solution
Answer
Power increases by ≈36.1%\approx 36.1\%≈36.1%
  1. Goal. Find the percent increase in dissipated power when voltage rises from 120 V120\,\mathrm{V}120V to 140 V140\,\mathrm{V}140V with constant resistance.
  2. Knowns. Nominal P0=100 WP_0=100\,\mathrm{W}P0​=100W at V0=120 VV_0=120\,\mathrm{V}V0​=120V; surge V1=140 VV_1=140\,\mathrm{V}V1​=140V; RRR constant (idealized hot filament).
  3. Power at fixed R. Ohmic resistor: P=IV=V2/RP=IV=V^2/RP=IV=V2/R. Therefore P∝V2P\propto V^2P∝V2 when RRR is unchanged.
  4. Ratio without finding R. P1/P0=(V1/V0)2=(140/120)2=(7/6)2=49/36≈1.361P_1/P_0=(V_1/V_0)^2=(140/120)^2=(7/6)^2=49/36\approx 1.361P1​/P0​=(V1​/V0​)2=(140/120)2=(7/6)2=49/36≈1.361.
  5. New power (optional check). R=V02/P0=144 ΩR=V_0^2/P_0=144\,\OmegaR=V02​/P0​=144Ω; P1=V12/R≈136 WP_1=V_1^2/R\approx 136\,\mathrm{W}P1​=V12​/R≈136W.
  6. Percent increase. ΔP%=100%×(P1/P0−1)=100%×(49/36−1)=100%×(13/36)≈36.1%\Delta P\% = 100\%\times (P_1/P_0-1)=100\%\times(49/36-1)=100\%\times(13/36)\approx 36.1\%ΔP%=100%×(P1​/P0​−1)=100%×(49/36−1)=100%×(13/36)≈36.1%.
  7. Pitfall check. Use percent increase, not the ratio 1.361.361.36 alone. Do not use P∝VP\propto VP∝V—that would underestimate heating.
  8. Final answer. Power increases by about 36%36\%36% (more precisely ≈36.1%\approx 36.1\%≈36.1%).
