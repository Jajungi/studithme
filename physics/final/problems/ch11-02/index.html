Rotating rod and the homopolar generator

회전하는 막대와 동극 발전기

A metal rod of total length 0.60 m0.60\,\mathrm{m}0.60m rotates counterclockwise about its midpoint at ω=40 rad/s\omega=40\,\mathrm{rad/s}ω=40rad/s in a uniform field B=0.25 TB=0.25\,\mathrm{T}B=0.25T pointing out of the page, perpendicular to the plane of rotation. (a) Find the emf between the midpoint and one end. (b) Find the emf between the two ends. (c) A sliding contact connects the midpoint and one end through a resistor so that the total circuit resistance is 0.15 Ω0.15\,\Omega0.15Ω. Find the current and the power dissipated. (d) What would the midpoint-to-end emf be if the rod were instead pivoted about one end at the same ω\omegaω?
한글
전체 길이 0.60 m0.60\,\mathrm{m}0.60m인 금속 막대가 중점을 축으로 반시계 방향으로 ω=40 rad/s\omega=40\,\mathrm{rad/s}ω=40rad/s로 회전한다. 균일 자기장 B=0.25 TB=0.25\,\mathrm{T}B=0.25T는 회전 평면에 수직이며 지면 밖을 향한다. (a) 중점과 한쪽 끝 사이 기전력은? (b) 양 끝 사이 기전력은? (c) 미끄럼 접점으로 중점과 한쪽 끝을 저항에 연결해 회로 전체 저항이 0.15 Ω0.15\,\Omega0.15Ω일 때 전류와 소비 전력은? (d) 같은 ω\omegaω로 막대를 한쪽 끝을 축으로 회전시키면 축–끝 기전력은?

Hints
  1. Each half has length l=0.30 ml=0.30\,\mathrm{m}l=0.30m and sweeps area πl2\pi l^{2}πl2 per revolution, i.e. 12l2ω\tfrac12 l^{2}\omega21​l2ω per second.
  2. The two ends have the same polarity relative to the midpoint, so they cancel.
  3. For part (d) the length from pivot to tip is 0.60 m0.60\,\mathrm{m}0.60m.
한글 힌트
  1. 절반의 길이는 l=0.30 ml=0.30\,\mathrm{m}l=0.30m이며 한 바퀴에 πl2\pi l^{2}πl2를 쓸어 초당 12l2ω\tfrac12 l^{2}\omega21​l2ω.
  2. 양 끝은 중점에 대해 같은 극성이라 상쇄된다.
  3. (d)에서는 축–끝 길이가 0.60 m0.60\,\mathrm{m}0.60m.
Worked solution
Answer
(a) ε=12Bωl2=0.45 V\varepsilon=\tfrac12B\omega l^{2}=0.45\,\mathrm{V}ε=21​Bωl2=0.45V; (b) 000; (c) I=3.0 AI=3.0\,\mathrm{A}I=3.0A, P=1.35 WP=1.35\,\mathrm{W}P=1.35W; (d) 1.8 V1.8\,\mathrm{V}1.8V
  1. Concept. Emf equals the rate at which the rod sweeps magnetic flux: ε=B dA/dt\varepsilon=B\,dA/dtε=BdA/dt. Half a rod of length lll sweeps πl2\pi l^{2}πl2 in time 2π/ω2\pi/\omega2π/ω, so dA/dt=12l2ωdA/dt=\tfrac12 l^{2}\omegadA/dt=21​l2ω.
  2. (a) Midpoint to end. ε=12Bl2ω=12(0.25)(0.30)2(40)=0.45 V\varepsilon=\tfrac12 Bl^{2}\omega=\tfrac12(0.25)(0.30)^{2}(40)=0.45\,\mathrm{V}ε=21​Bl2ω=21​(0.25)(0.30)2(40)=0.45V. Check by integrating: ε=∫0l(ωr)B dr=12Bωl2\varepsilon=\int_0^{l}(\omega r)B\,dr=\tfrac12B\omega l^{2}ε=∫0l​(ωr)Bdr=21​Bωl2.
  3. Polarity. At the +x+x+x end v⃗=+v y^\vec v=+v\,\hat yv=+vy^​, B⃗=+B z^\vec B=+B\,\hat zB=+Bz^, and y^×z^=+x^\hat y\times\hat z=+\hat xy^​×z^=+x^, so positive charge is pushed outward: both outer ends are +++ relative to the center.
  4. (b) Between the two ends. Both ends are at the same potential relative to the midpoint, so εends=0\varepsilon_{\text{ends}}=0εends​=0.
  5. (c) Circuit. I=ε/R=0.45/0.15=3.0 AI=\varepsilon/R=0.45/0.15=3.0\,\mathrm{A}I=ε/R=0.45/0.15=3.0A and P=I2R=(3.0)2(0.15)=1.35 WP=I^{2}R=(3.0)^{2}(0.15)=1.35\,\mathrm{W}P=I2R=(3.0)2(0.15)=1.35W. This is a homopolar generator; the power comes from the motor turning the rod, not from the field.
  6. (d) Pivot at one end. The pivot-to-tip length is l=0.60 ml=0.60\,\mathrm{m}l=0.60m, so ε=12(0.25)(0.60)2(40)=1.8 V\varepsilon=\tfrac12(0.25)(0.60)^{2}(40)=1.8\,\mathrm{V}ε=21​(0.25)(0.60)2(40)=1.8V — four times larger, because ε∝l2\varepsilon\propto l^{2}ε∝l2.
  7. Pitfall check. Using the full length 0.60 m0.60\,\mathrm{m}0.60m with a midpoint pivot, or forgetting the factor 12\tfrac1221​, gives wrong answers (a factor of 444 or 222).
  8. Final answer. (a) 0.45 V0.45\,\mathrm{V}0.45V; (b) 000; (c) 3.0 A3.0\,\mathrm{A}3.0A, 1.35 W1.35\,\mathrm{W}1.35W; (d) 1.8 V1.8\,\mathrm{V}1.8V.
