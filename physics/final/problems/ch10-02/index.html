Field of a long cylinder with current density J = br

전류 밀도 J=br인 긴 원통의 자기장

A long cylindrical conductor of radius RRR carries a current III along its axis. The current density JJJ is not uniform over the cross section but depends on the distance from the axis as J=brJ=brJ=br, where bbb is a constant. Find an expression for the magnetic field magnitude BBB (a) at a distance r1<Rr_1<Rr1​<R and (b) at a distance r2>Rr_2>Rr2​>R, measured from the center of the conductor.
한글
반지름 RRR인 긴 원통형 도체에 축 방향으로 전류 III가 흐른다. 전류 밀도 JJJ는 단면에서 균일하지 않고 축에서의 거리에 따라 J=brJ=brJ=br (bbb는 상수)이다. 중심에서 (a) r1<Rr_1<Rr1​<R인 곳, (b) r2>Rr_2>Rr2​>R인 곳의 자기장 크기 BBB를 구하라.

Hints
  1. Use a circular Amperian loop concentric with the cylinder: ∮B⃗⋅dℓ⃗=2πrB\oint\vec B\cdot d\vec\ell=2\pi rB∮B⋅dℓ=2πrB.
  2. Because JJJ changes with rrr, build IencI_\mathrm{enc}Ienc​ by integrating over thin rings of area 2πr dr2\pi r\,dr2πrdr.
  3. For r2>Rr_2>Rr2​>R the loop encloses the entire current I=∫0RJ 2πr drI=\int_0^R J\,2\pi r\,drI=∫0R​J2πrdr; relate bbb to III with it.
한글 힌트
  1. 원통과 동심인 원형 앙페르 경로: ∮B⃗⋅dℓ⃗=2πrB\oint\vec B\cdot d\vec\ell=2\pi rB∮B⋅dℓ=2πrB.
  2. JJJ가 rrr에 따라 변하므로 넓이 2πr dr2\pi r\,dr2πrdr인 얇은 고리를 적분해 IencI_\mathrm{enc}Ienc​를 만든다.
  3. r2>Rr_2>Rr2​>R이면 경로는 전체 전류 I=∫0RJ 2πr drI=\int_0^R J\,2\pi r\,drI=∫0R​J2πrdr를 감싼다. 이것으로 bbb와 III를 연결한다.
Worked solution
Answer
(a) B=μ0b r123B=\dfrac{\mu_0 b\,r_1^{2}}{3}B=3μ0​br12​​; (b) B=μ0b R33r2=μ0I2πr2B=\dfrac{\mu_0 b\,R^{3}}{3r_2}=\dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r_2}B=3r2​μ0​bR3​=2πr2​μ0​I​
  1. Concept. Cylindrical symmetry makes the field circulate around the axis with constant magnitude on a concentric circle, so ∮B⃗⋅dℓ⃗=2πrB=μ0Ienc\oint\vec B\cdot d\vec\ell=2\pi rB=\mu_0 I_\mathrm{enc}∮B⋅dℓ=2πrB=μ0​Ienc​. The work is in IencI_\mathrm{enc}Ienc​.
  2. Ring decomposition. A thin ring of radius r′r'r′ and thickness dr′dr'dr′ has area 2πr′ dr′2\pi r'\,dr'2πr′dr′ and carries dI=J dA=(br′) 2πr′ dr′=2πb r′2 dr′dI=J\,dA=(br')\,2\pi r'\,dr'=2\pi b\,r'^{2}\,dr'dI=JdA=(br′)2πr′dr′=2πbr′2dr′.
  3. (a) Inside, r1<Rr_1<Rr1​<R. Ienc=∫0r12πb r′2 dr′=2πb r133I_\mathrm{enc}=\displaystyle\int_0^{r_1}2\pi b\,r'^{2}\,dr'=\dfrac{2\pi b\,r_1^{3}}{3}Ienc​=∫0r1​​2πbr′2dr′=32πbr13​​. Then 2πr1B=μ02πb r1332\pi r_1B=\mu_0\dfrac{2\pi b\,r_1^{3}}{3}2πr1​B=μ0​32πbr13​​, so B=μ0b r123B=\dfrac{\mu_0 b\,r_1^{2}}{3}B=3μ0​br12​​.
  4. Total current. Setting the upper limit to RRR: I=2πbR33I=\dfrac{2\pi bR^{3}}{3}I=32πbR3​, i.e. b=3I2πR3b=\dfrac{3I}{2\pi R^{3}}b=2πR33I​. In terms of III: B(r1)=μ0I r122πR3B(r_1)=\dfrac{\mu_0 I\,r_1^{2}}{2\pi R^{3}}B(r1​)=2πR3μ0​Ir12​​.
  5. (b) Outside, r2>Rr_2>Rr2​>R. The loop encloses all the current, Ienc=I=2πbR33I_\mathrm{enc}=I=\dfrac{2\pi bR^{3}}{3}Ienc​=I=32πbR3​, so B=μ0I2πr2=μ0bR33r2B=\dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r_2}=\dfrac{\mu_0 b R^{3}}{3r_2}B=2πr2​μ0​I​=3r2​μ0​bR3​.
  6. Check. At r=Rr=Rr=R the two results agree: μ0bR2/3\mu_0 bR^{2}/3μ0​bR2/3. Inside the field grows as r2r^{2}r2 (near the axis this rises more slowly than the uniform-wire B∝rB\propto rB∝r, because current is piled toward the edge); outside it falls as 1/r1/r1/r, the same as for a thin wire.
  7. Pitfall check. Do not use Ienc=Jπr2I_\mathrm{enc}=J\pi r^{2}Ienc​=Jπr2 for a nonuniform JJJ, and do not integrate past RRR when the loop is outside the conductor.
  8. Final answer. (a) B=μ0b r123B=\dfrac{\mu_0 b\,r_1^{2}}{3}B=3μ0​br12​​; (b) B=μ0b R33r2=μ0I2πr2B=\dfrac{\mu_0 b\,R^{3}}{3r_2}=\dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r_2}B=3r2​μ0​bR3​=2πr2​μ0​I​.
