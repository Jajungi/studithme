Net field of two antiparallel long wires at three points

반대 방향 전류가 흐르는 두 긴 도선의 세 점에서의 알짜 자기장

The two long parallel wires in the figure are separated by d=10.0 cmd=10.0\,\mathrm{cm}d=10.0cm and carry currents of I=5.00 AI=5.00\,\mathrm{A}I=5.00A in opposite directions (the left wire carries current up, the right wire down). Find the magnitude and direction of the net magnetic field (a) at a point midway between the wires; (b) at point P1P_1P1​, 10.0 cm10.0\,\mathrm{cm}10.0cm to the right of the right wire; and (c) at point P2P_2P2​, 2d=20.0 cm2d=20.0\,\mathrm{cm}2d=20.0cm to the left of the left wire. All points lie in the plane of the wires, and μ0=4π×10−7 T m/A\mu_0=4\pi\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}μ0​=4π×10−7Tm/A.
한글
그림의 평행한 두 긴 도선은 d=10.0 cmd=10.0\,\mathrm{cm}d=10.0cm 떨어져 있고 서로 반대 방향으로 I=5.00 AI=5.00\,\mathrm{A}I=5.00A가 흐른다(왼쪽 도선은 위로, 오른쪽 도선은 아래로). 알짜 자기장의 크기와 방향을 (a) 두 도선의 한가운데, (b) 오른쪽 도선에서 오른쪽으로 10.0 cm10.0\,\mathrm{cm}10.0cm인 점 P1P_1P1​, (c) 왼쪽 도선에서 왼쪽으로 2d=20.0 cm2d=20.0\,\mathrm{cm}2d=20.0cm인 점 P2P_2P2​에서 구하라. 모든 점은 도선 평면 안에 있고 μ0=4π×10−7 T m/A\mu_0=4\pi\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}μ0​=4π×10−7Tm/A이다.

Hints
  1. Each long wire alone gives B=μ0I/2πrB=\mu_0 I/2\pi rB=μ0​I/2πr (Ampère's law with a circular loop). The net field is the vector sum of the two.
  2. Use the right-hand rule per wire: for an upward current the field points into the page on its right and out of the page on its left; a downward current is the reverse.
  3. Between antiparallel wires the two fields add; outside both wires they oppose, and the nearer wire wins.
한글 힌트
  1. 긴 도선 하나의 장은 B=μ0I/2πrB=\mu_0 I/2\pi rB=μ0​I/2πr(원형 앙페르 경로)이다. 알짜는 두 장의 벡터 합이다.
  2. 도선마다 오른손 규칙: 위로 가는 전류는 오른쪽에서 지면 안, 왼쪽에서 지면 밖이고, 아래로 가는 전류는 반대이다.
  3. 반대 방향 두 도선 사이에서는 두 장이 더해지고, 두 도선 바깥에서는 서로 반대여서 가까운 도선이 이긴다.

Answer

  1. Concept. Ampère's law with a circular loop around one long wire gives B=μ0I/2πrB=\mu_0 I/2\pi rB=μ0​I/2πr. With two wires, find each field separately (magnitude by 1/r1/r1/r, direction by the right-hand rule) and add them as vectors.
  2. Setup. Left wire at x=0x=0x=0 carrying current up, right wire at x=dx=dx=d carrying current down, d=0.100 md=0.100\,\mathrm{m}d=0.100m, I=5.00 AI=5.00\,\mathrm{A}I=5.00A. Use μ0I/2π=(2×10−7)(5.00)=1.00×10−6 T m\mu_0 I/2\pi=(2\times10^{-7})(5.00)=1.00\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{T\,m}μ0​I/2π=(2×10−7)(5.00)=1.00×10−6Tm, so B=(1.00×10−6)/rB=(1.00\times10^{-6})/rB=(1.00×10−6)/r.
  3. Directions. Up-current wire: field into the page to its right, out of the page to its left. Down-current wire: field out of the page to its right, into the page to its left.
  4. (a) Midpoint, r=0.0500 mr=0.0500\,\mathrm{m}r=0.0500m from each. The point is right of the left wire (into the page) and left of the right wire (also into the page), so the fields add: B=2×1.00×10−60.0500=4.00×10−5 TB=2\times\dfrac{1.00\times10^{-6}}{0.0500}=4.00\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}B=2×0.05001.00×10−6​=4.00×10−5T, into the page.
  5. (b) P1P_1P1​. Distances are 0.100 m0.100\,\mathrm{m}0.100m (right wire) and 0.200 m0.200\,\mathrm{m}0.200m (left wire). Right wire: 1.00×10−60.100=1.00×10−5 T\dfrac{1.00\times10^{-6}}{0.100}=1.00\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}0.1001.00×10−6​=1.00×10−5T out of the page. Net: B=1.00×10−5−5.00×10−6=5.00×10−6 TB=1.00\times10^{-5}-5.00\times10^{-6}=5.00\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{T}B=1.00×10−5−5.00×10−6=5.00×10−6T, out of the page.
  6. (c) P2P_2P2​. Distances are 0.200 m0.200\,\mathrm{m}0.200m (left wire) and 0.300 m0.300\,\mathrm{m}0.300m (right wire). Left wire: 5.00×10−6 T5.00\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{T}5.00×10−6T out of the page (point is to its left). Right wire: 1.00×10−60.300=3.33×10−6 T\dfrac{1.00\times10^{-6}}{0.300}=3.33\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{T}0.3001.00×10−6​=3.33×10−6T into the page (point is to its left).
