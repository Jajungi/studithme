Magnetic forces between two electrons at right angles

서로 수직으로 움직이는 두 전자의 자기력

Two electrons are (instantaneously) moving at right angles in the same plane with speeds v=3.0×106 m/sv=3.0\times10^{6}\,\mathrm{m/s}v=3.0×106m/s and v′=1.0×106 m/sv'=1.0\times10^{6}\,\mathrm{m/s}v′=1.0×106m/s. Their separation is r=0.80×10−10 mr=0.80\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm{m}r=0.80×10−10m (Figure). Calculate the magnetic force of the first electron on the second and that of the second on the first. Give magnitudes and directions. Use e=1.60×10−19 Ce=1.60\times10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}e=1.60×10−19C and μ0/4π=1.00×10−7 T m/A\mu_0/4\pi=1.00\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m/A}μ0​/4π=1.00×10−7Tm/A.

Hints
  1. Write B⃗\vec BB of the source electron, then F⃗=q(v⃗×B⃗)\vec F=q(\vec v\times\vec B)F=q(v×B) on the target.
  2. If v⃗∥r^\vec v\parallel\hat rv∥r^ for a source, that geometry contributes zero field.
  3. Both charges are negative — finish the cross products first, then apply the signs.
