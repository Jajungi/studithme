Field on the z-axis from wires joined by a semicircle

반원으로 이은 반무한 도선의 $z$축 자기장

Two semi-infinite parallel wires lie in the xxx–yyy plane and are joined by a semicircle of radius RRR whose center is the origin. The wires carry current III as in the figure. Find the xxx, yyy, and zzz components of B⃗\vec BB at a point on the zzz-axis.
한글
서로 평행한 두 반무한 도선이 반지름 RRR인 반원으로 연결되어 있다(중심이 원점). 그림처럼 전류 III가 흐를 때 zzz축 위 점에서 BxB_xBx​, ByB_yBy​, BzB_zBz​를 구하라.

Hints
  1. Treat two semi-infinite straights and one semicircular arc separately.
  2. Mirror symmetry through the yyy–zzz plane forces Bx=0B_x=0Bx​=0.
  3. The semicircle supplies the axial BzB_zBz​; straights mainly feed ByB_yBy​.
한글 힌트
  1. 반무한 직선 둘과 반원 호를 따로 계산한다.
  2. yyy–zzz면 대칭으로 Bx=0B_x=0Bx​=0.
  3. 반원이 BzB_zBz​의 주된 축 기여를 준다.
Worked solution
Answer
Bx=0B_x=0Bx​=0; Bz=μ0IR24(R2+z2)3/2B_z=\dfrac{\mu_0 IR^{2}}{4(R^{2}+z^{2})^{3/2}}Bz​=4(R2+z2)3/2μ0​IR2​; By=μ0IR2π[z(R2+z2)3/2−1zR2+z2]B_y=\dfrac{\mu_0 IR}{2\pi}\left[\dfrac{z}{(R^{2}+z^{2})^{3/2}}-\dfrac{1}{z\sqrt{R^{2}+z^{2}}}\right]By​=2πμ0​IR​[(R2+z2)3/2z​−zR2+z2​1​] (signs per figure)
  1. Concept. Biot–Savart superposition of two semi-infinite wires and one semicircular arc.
  2. Symmetry. Bx=0B_x=0Bx​=0 by mirror symmetry in the yyy–zzz plane.
  3. Semicircle axial piece. A semicircle is half a loop on axis: Bz=μ0IR24(R2+z2)3/2B_z=\dfrac{\mu_0 I R^{2}}{4(R^{2}+z^{2})^{3/2}}Bz​=4(R2+z2)3/2μ0​IR2​.
  4. yyy components. Combining the semi-infinite legs and the arc transverse contributions gives By=μ0IR2π[z(R2+z2)3/2−1zR2+z2]B_y=\dfrac{\mu_0 I R}{2\pi}\left[\dfrac{z}{(R^{2}+z^{2})^{3/2}}-\dfrac{1}{z\sqrt{R^{2}+z^{2}}}\right]By​=2πμ0​IR​[(R2+z2)3/2z​−zR2+z2​1​] (overall sign set by the figure's current arrows).
  5. Pitfall check. Do not treat the straights as infinite through the origin — they start at the semicircle ends.
  6. Final answer. Bx=0B_x=0Bx​=0, Bz=μ0IR24(R2+z2)3/2B_z=\dfrac{\mu_0 IR^{2}}{4(R^{2}+z^{2})^{3/2}}Bz​=4(R2+z2)3/2μ0​IR2​, By=μ0IR2π[z(R2+z2)3/2−1zR2+z2]B_y=\dfrac{\mu_0 IR}{2\pi}\left[\dfrac{z}{(R^{2}+z^{2})^{3/2}}-\dfrac{1}{z\sqrt{R^{2}+z^{2}}}\right]By​=2πμ0​IR​[(R2+z2)3/2z​−zR2+z2​1​] (signs per figure).
한글 답
Bx=0B_x=0Bx​=0; Bz=μ0IR24(R2+z2)3/2B_z=\dfrac{\mu_0 IR^{2}}{4(R^{2}+z^{2})^{3/2}}Bz​=4(R2+z2)3/2μ0​IR2​; ByB_yBy​는 직선+호 합 (그림 부호)
한글 해설
  1. 개념. Biot–Savart 중첩(반무한 직선 + 반원).
  2. 결과. Bx=0B_x=0Bx​=0, Bz=μ0IR24(R2+z2)3/2B_z=\dfrac{\mu_0 IR^{2}}{4(R^{2}+z^{2})^{3/2}}Bz​=4(R2+z2)3/2μ0​IR2​, By=μ0IR2π[z(R2+z2)3/2−1zR2+z2]B_y=\dfrac{\mu_0 IR}{2\pi}\left[\dfrac{z}{(R^{2}+z^{2})^{3/2}}-\dfrac{1}{z\sqrt{R^{2}+z^{2}}}\right]By​=2πμ0​IR​[(R2+z2)3/2z​−zR2+z2​1​] (부호는 그림 전류 방향).
  3. 최종 답. 위 성분식.
