Resistance of a long aluminum transmission cable

200 km 알루미늄 송전선 저항

A high-voltage transmission line uses an aluminum cable of diameter 3.0 cm3.0\,\mathrm{cm}3.0cm and length 200 km200\,\mathrm{km}200km. Using ρAl=2.8×10−8 Ω⋅m\rho_\mathrm{Al} = 2.8\times 10^{-8}\,\Omega\cdot\mathrm{m}ρAl​=2.8×10−8Ω⋅m, find the resistance of the cable.
한글
고압 송전선 알루미늄 케이블 지름 3.0 cm3.0\,\mathrm{cm}3.0cm, 길이 200 km200\,\mathrm{km}200km. ρAl=2.8×10−8 Ω⋅m\rho_\mathrm{Al} = 2.8\times 10^{-8}\,\Omega\cdot\mathrm{m}ρAl​=2.8×10−8Ω⋅m일 때 케이블 저항을 구하라.

Hints
  1. Convert 200 km200\,\mathrm{km}200km to meters and diameter to radius.
  2. R=ρL/AR=\rho L/AR=ρL/A with A=πr2A=\pi r^2A=πr2 for a solid uniform wire.
한글 힌트
  1. 200 km200\,\mathrm{km}200km를 m로, 지름을 반지름으로 바꾼다.
  2. R=ρL/AR=\rho L/AR=ρL/A, A=πr2A=\pi r^2A=πr2.
Worked solution
Answer
R≈7.9 ΩR \approx 7.9\,\OmegaR≈7.9Ω
  1. Goal. Find the DC resistance of a uniform aluminum cable from its resistivity and dimensions.
  2. Knowns and units. L=200 km=2.0×105 mL=200\,\mathrm{km}=2.0\times 10^5\,\mathrm{m}L=200km=2.0×105m, diameter 3.0 cm⇒r=1.5 cm=1.5×10−2 m3.0\,\mathrm{cm}\Rightarrow r=1.5\,\mathrm{cm}=1.5\times 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m}3.0cm⇒r=1.5cm=1.5×10−2m, ρ=2.8×10−8 Ω⋅m\rho=2.8\times 10^{-8}\,\Omega\cdot\mathrm{m}ρ=2.8×10−8Ω⋅m.
  3. Area. A=πr2=π(1.5×10−2)2 m2≈7.07×10−4 m2A=\pi r^2=\pi(1.5\times 10^{-2})^2\,\mathrm{m}^2\approx 7.07\times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m}^2A=πr2=π(1.5×10−2)2m2≈7.07×10−4m2.
  4. Resistance formula. For a uniform rod with end contacts on the full faces, R=ρL/AR=\rho L/AR=ρL/A.
  5. Substitute. R=(2.8×10−8)(2.0×105)/(7.07×10−4) ΩR=(2.8\times 10^{-8})(2.0\times 10^5)/(7.07\times 10^{-4})\,\OmegaR=(2.8×10−8)(2.0×105)/(7.07×10−4)Ω.
  6. Evaluate. R≈7.9 ΩR\approx 7.9\,\OmegaR≈7.9Ω—a modest resistance despite the huge length because aluminum has very small ρ\rhoρ and a centimeter-scale diameter.
  7. Pitfall check. Length must be in meters (2.0×1052.0\times 10^52.0×105, not 200200200). This is bulk DC resistance; skin effect and temperature rise are ignored as in the textbook model.
  8. Final answer. R≈7.9 ΩR \approx 7.9\,\OmegaR≈7.9Ω.
