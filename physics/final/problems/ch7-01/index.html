Potential drop along an iron lightning rod

철제 피뢰침 양단 전위차

A lightning rod of iron has diameter 0.80 cm0.80\,\mathrm{cm}0.80cm and length 0.50 m0.50\,\mathrm{m}0.50m. During a stroke it carries current I=1.0×104 AI = 1.0\times 10^4\,\mathrm{A}I=1.0×104A. Using ρiron=10×10−8 Ω⋅m\rho_\mathrm{iron} = 10\times 10^{-8}\,\Omega\cdot\mathrm{m}ρiron​=10×10−8Ω⋅m (Table 28.1), find the potential drop along the rod.
한글
철 피뢰침의 지름은 0.80 cm0.80\,\mathrm{cm}0.80cm, 길이는 0.50 m0.50\,\mathrm{m}0.50m이다. 낙뢰 시 전류 I=1.0×104 AI = 1.0\times 10^4\,\mathrm{A}I=1.0×104A가 흐른다. 표 28.1의 ρiron=10×10−8 Ω⋅m\rho_\mathrm{iron} = 10\times 10^{-8}\,\Omega\cdot\mathrm{m}ρiron​=10×10−8Ω⋅m를 쓰고, 막대 양단 전위차를 구하라.

Hints
  1. Convert diameter to radius and find cross-sectional area A=πr2A=\pi r^2A=πr2.
  2. Uniform rod: R=ρL/AR=\rho L/AR=ρL/A, then ΔV=IR\Delta V = IRΔV=IR.
  3. Keep SI units: centimeters →\to→ meters before area.
한글 힌트
  1. 지름을 반지름으로 바꾸고 A=πr2A=\pi r^2A=πr2를 구한다.
  2. 균일 막대: R=ρL/AR=\rho L/AR=ρL/A, ΔV=IR\Delta V = IRΔV=IR.
  3. 단위: cm →\to→ m 변환 후 넓이를 계산한다.
Worked solution
Answer
ΔV≈9.9 V\Delta V \approx 9.9\,\mathrm{V}ΔV≈9.9V
  1. Goal. Find the potential difference between the two ends of the iron rod when steady current I=1.0×104 AI=1.0\times 10^4\,\mathrm{A}I=1.0×104A flows through it.
  2. Knowns. Length L=0.50 mL=0.50\,\mathrm{m}L=0.50m, diameter d=0.80 cm=8.0×10−3 md=0.80\,\mathrm{cm}=8.0\times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}d=0.80cm=8.0×10−3m, so radius r=d/2=4.0×10−3 mr=d/2=4.0\times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}r=d/2=4.0×10−3m. Resistivity ρ=10×10−8 Ω⋅m=1.0×10−7 Ω⋅m\rho=10\times 10^{-8}\,\Omega\cdot\mathrm{m}=1.0\times 10^{-7}\,\Omega\cdot\mathrm{m}ρ=10×10−8Ω⋅m=1.0×10−7Ω⋅m.
  3. Cross-sectional area. The rod is uniform: A=πr2=π(4.0×10−3)2 m2≈5.03×10−5 m2A=\pi r^2=\pi(4.0\times 10^{-3})^2\,\mathrm{m}^2\approx 5.03\times 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m}^2A=πr2=π(4.0×10−3)2m2≈5.03×10−5m2.
  4. Resistance from resistivity. For a uniform conductor, R=ρL/AR=\rho L/AR=ρL/A. This follows from E=ρJE=\rho JE=ρJ, uniform field E=ΔV/LE=\Delta V/LE=ΔV/L, and J=I/AJ=I/AJ=I/A.
  5. Substitute. R=(1.0×10−7)(0.50)/(5.03×10−5) Ω≈9.9×10−4 ΩR=(1.0\times 10^{-7})(0.50)/(5.03\times 10^{-5})\,\Omega\approx 9.9\times 10^{-4}\,\OmegaR=(1.0×10−7)(0.50)/(5.03×10−5)Ω≈9.9×10−4Ω.
  6. Ohm’s law along the rod. With steady current, the end-to-end drop is ΔV=IR≈(1.0×104)(9.9×10−4) V\Delta V=IR\approx (1.0\times 10^4)(9.9\times 10^{-4})\,\mathrm{V}ΔV=IR≈(1.0×104)(9.9×10−4)V.
  7. Pitfall check. Use radius in A=πr2A=\pi r^2A=πr2, not diameter. The table gives resistivity, not resistance—you still need geometry.
  8. Final answer. ΔV≈9.9 V\Delta V\approx 9.9\,\mathrm{V}ΔV≈9.9V (about 10 V10\,\mathrm{V}10V to two significant figures).
