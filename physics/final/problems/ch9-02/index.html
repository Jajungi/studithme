Magnetic forces of two charges on perpendicular paths

서로 수직인 경로 위 두 양전하의 자기력

Two positive point charges qqq and q′q'q′ move along perpendicular paths that intersect. Their speeds are equal (v=v′v=v'v=v′), and they reach the intersection simultaneously (assume they pass through each other without disruption). Find the magnitude of the magnetic force on each when each charge is a distance ddd from the intersection. State the force directions before they reach the intersection and afterward.
한글
양전하 qqq와 q′q'q′가 서로 수직인 경로를 따라 움직이며 교차점에서 동시에 만난다(v=v′v=v'v=v′). 각 전하가 교차점에서 거리 ddd일 때 서로에게 미치는 자기력의 크기를 구하고, 교차 전·후의 방향을 설명하라.

Hints
  1. When each is a distance ddd from the crossing, the separation is r=d2r=d\sqrt{2}r=d2​.
  2. Use F⃗=(μ0/4π)(qq′/r2) v⃗×(v⃗′×r^)\vec F=(\mu_0/4\pi)(qq'/r^{2})\,\vec v\times(\vec v'\times\hat r)F=(μ0​/4π)(qq′/r2)v×(v′×r^) with r^\hat rr^ from source to target.
  3. After the crossing, rebuild r^\hat rr^; the magnitude is unchanged but the direction flips.
한글 힌트
  1. 각자가 교차점에서 ddd만큼 떨어져 있으면 거리는 r=d2r=d\sqrt{2}r=d2​.
  2. F⃗=(μ0/4π)(qq′/r2)v⃗×(v⃗′×r^)\vec F=(\mu_0/4\pi)(qq'/r^{2})\vec v\times(\vec v'\times\hat r)F=(μ0​/4π)(qq′/r2)v×(v′×r^)를 쓴다.
  3. 교차 뒤에는 r^\hat rr^을 다시 잡고 방향만 바꾼다.
Worked solution
Answer
F=μ04πqq′v222 d2F=\dfrac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\dfrac{qq'v^{2}}{2\sqrt{2}\,d^{2}}F=4πμ0​​22​d2qq′v2​; directions reverse before vs after the crossing
  1. Concept. Same double-cross magnetic-force formula; evaluate before and after the intersection with the correct r^\hat rr^.
  2. Geometry before. Take qqq at (−d,0)(-d,0)(−d,0) with v⃗=vx^\vec v=v\hat xv=vx^ and q′q'q′ at (0,−d)(0,-d)(0,−d) with v⃗′=vy^\vec v'=v\hat yv′=vy^​. Then r=d2r=d\sqrt{2}r=d2​ and r^=(q′→q)=(−x^+y^)/2\hat r=(q'\to q)=(-\hat x+\hat y)/\sqrt{2}r^=(q′→q)=(−x^+y^​)/2​.
  3. Cross products. v⃗′×r^=(v/2)z^\vec v'\times\hat r=(v/\sqrt{2})\hat zv′×r^=(v/2​)z^, then v⃗×(v⃗′×r^)=−(v2/2)y^\vec v\times(\vec v'\times\hat r)=-(v^{2}/\sqrt{2})\hat yv×(v′×r^)=−(v2/2​)y^​.
  4. Magnitude. F=(μ0/4π)(qq′/(2d2))(v2/2)=(μ0/4π)qq′v222 d2F=(\mu_0/4\pi)(qq'/(2d^{2}))(v^{2}/\sqrt{2})=(\mu_0/4\pi)\dfrac{qq'v^{2}}{2\sqrt{2}\,d^{2}}F=(μ0​/4π)(qq′/(2d2))(v2/2​)=(μ0​/4π)22​d2qq′v2​. The force on qqq is along −y^-\hat y−y^​; compute the partner separately (generally not equal-and-opposite).
  5. After the crossing. With qqq at (+d,0)(+d,0)(+d,0) and q′q'q′ at (0,+d)(0,+d)(0,+d) the magnitude is unchanged; the force on qqq flips to +y^+\hat y+y^​.
  6. Pitfall check. Do not assume Newton's third law for the magnetic pair alone.
  7. Final answer. F=μ04πqq′v222 d2F=\dfrac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\dfrac{qq'v^{2}}{2\sqrt{2}\,d^{2}}F=4πμ0​​22​d2qq′v2​ on each; directions reverse between the approach and departure legs.

한글 해설
  1. 개념. 이중 교차곱 자기력 식을 교차 전·후에 각각 적용한다.
  2. 교차 전. r=d2r=d\sqrt{2}r=d2​에서 F=(μ0/4π)qq′v222 d2F=(\mu_0/4\pi)\dfrac{qq'v^{2}}{2\sqrt{2}\,d^{2}}F=(μ0​/4π)22​d2qq′v2​.
  3. 방향. 교차 전과 후에 크기는 같고 방향은 뒤집힌다. 각 전하는 따로 계산한다.
  4. 실수 점검. 자기력만으로 뉴턴 제3법칙을 가정하지 않는다.
  5. 최종 답. F=μ04πqq′v222 d2F=\dfrac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\dfrac{qq'v^{2}}{2\sqrt{2}\,d^{2}}F=4πμ0​​22​d2qq′v2​; 방향은 교차 전·후 반대.
