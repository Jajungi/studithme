Center field of concentric semicircles

동심 반원 고리 중심의 자기장

A loop of wire has the shape of two concentric semicircles connected by two radial segments. The inner radius is RRR and the outer radius is 2R2R2R. The loop carries current III. Find the magnetic field at the common center.
한글
반지름 RRR과 2R2R2R인 두 동심 반원을 방사 선분으로 이은 고리에 전류 III가 흐른다. 공통 중심에서 자기장을 구하라.

Hints
  1. Radial segments contribute nothing at the center (dℓ⃗∥r^d\vec\ell\parallel\hat rdℓ∥r^).
  2. An arc of radius aaa subtending angle ϕ\phiϕ gives B=(μ0Iϕ)/(4πa)B=(\mu_0 I\phi)/(4\pi a)B=(μ0​Iϕ)/(4πa) at the center.
  3. Inner and outer arcs circulate in opposite senses relative to the center.
한글 힌트
  1. 방사 선분은 중심에서 기여 0 (dℓ⃗∥r^d\vec\ell\parallel\hat rdℓ∥r^).
  2. 중심에서 호: B=(μ0Iϕ)/(4πa)B=(\mu_0 I\phi)/(4\pi a)B=(μ0​Iϕ)/(4πa).
  3. 안쪽·바깥 호는 중심 기준으로 반대 방향.
Worked solution
Answer
B=μ0I8RB=\dfrac{\mu_0 I}{8R}B=8Rμ0​I​ (direction by the inner arc / RH rule)
  1. Concept. At a center, arcs give B=(μ0Iϕ)/(4πa)B=(\mu_0 I\phi)/(4\pi a)B=(μ0​Iϕ)/(4πa); radial legs give zero.
  2. Outer semicircle. ϕ=π\phi=\piϕ=π, a=2Ra=2Ra=2R ⇒\Rightarrow⇒ Bout=μ0I/(8R)B_{\mathrm{out}}=\mu_0 I/(8R)Bout​=μ0​I/(8R).
  3. Inner semicircle. ϕ=π\phi=\piϕ=π, a=Ra=Ra=R ⇒\Rightarrow⇒ Bin=μ0I/(4R)B_{\mathrm{in}}=\mu_0 I/(4R)Bin​=μ0​I/(4R).
  4. Net. Opposite senses leave B=Bin−Bout=μ0I/(8R)B=B_{\mathrm{in}}-B_{\mathrm{out}}=\mu_0 I/(8R)B=Bin​−Bout​=μ0​I/(8R), direction set by the dominant inner arc.
  5. Pitfall check. Do not assign the same direction to both arcs — the radials reverse the sense.
  6. Final answer. B=μ0I8RB=\dfrac{\mu_0 I}{8R}B=8Rμ0​I​ perpendicular to the plane (inner-arc RH direction).
한글 답
B=μ0I8RB=\dfrac{\mu_0 I}{8R}B=8Rμ0​I​ (안쪽 호 오른손 방향)
한글 해설
  1. 개념. 중심에서 호 공식 + 방사 선분 0.
  2. 바깥. Bout=μ0I/(8R)B_{\mathrm{out}}=\mu_0 I/(8R)Bout​=μ0​I/(8R), 안쪽 Bin=μ0I/(4R)B_{\mathrm{in}}=\mu_0 I/(4R)Bin​=μ0​I/(4R).
  3. 알짜. B=μ0I/(8R)B=\mu_0 I/(8R)B=μ0​I/(8R) (안쪽 호 방향).
  4. 최종 답. B=μ0I8RB=\dfrac{\mu_0 I}{8R}B=8Rμ0​I​.
