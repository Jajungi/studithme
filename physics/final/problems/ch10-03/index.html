Field of a thick-walled copper pipe at 3R/2 and 3R

두꺼운 구리 관의 3R/2, 3R에서의 자기장

A long copper pipe with thick walls has an inner radius RRR and an outer radius 2R2R2R. A current III flows along this wall, uniformly distributed over the volume of copper (that is, over the annular cross section). Find the magnetic field at the radius 32R\tfrac{3}{2}R23​R and at the radius 3R3R3R from the axis.
한글
안 반지름 RRR, 바깥 반지름 2R2R2R인 두꺼운 벽의 긴 구리 관에 전류 III가 구리 부피(고리 모양 단면)에 균일하게 흐른다. 축에서 32R\tfrac{3}{2}R23​R와 3R3R3R 떨어진 곳의 자기장을 구하라.

Hints
  1. The current density is uniform over the annulus R<r<2RR<r<2RR<r<2R: J=I/[π((2R)2−R2)]J=I/[\pi((2R)^2-R^2)]J=I/[π((2R)2−R2)].
  2. For r=32Rr=\tfrac32Rr=23​R the Amperian circle lies inside the wall: it encloses only the part of the annulus with radius between RRR and rrr.
  3. For r=3Rr=3Rr=3R the circle encloses the whole current.
한글 힌트
  1. 전류 밀도는 고리 단면 R<r<2RR<r<2RR<r<2R에서 균일: J=I/[π((2R)2−R2)]J=I/[\pi((2R)^2-R^2)]J=I/[π((2R)2−R2)].
  2. r=32Rr=\tfrac32Rr=23​R이면 앙페르 원이 벽 속에 있어 RRR과 rrr 사이의 고리 부분만 감싼다.
  3. r=3Rr=3Rr=3R이면 전체 전류를 감싼다.
Worked solution
Answer
B(32R)=5μ0I36πRB(\tfrac32R)=\dfrac{5\mu_0 I}{36\pi R}B(23​R)=36πR5μ0​I​; B(3R)=μ0I6πRB(3R)=\dfrac{\mu_0 I}{6\pi R}B(3R)=6πRμ0​I​
  1. Concept. Axial symmetry and a circular Amperian loop: 2πrB=μ0Ienc2\pi rB=\mu_0 I_\mathrm{enc}2πrB=μ0​Ienc​. Count the enclosed fraction of the annulus carefully; the hollow center carries no current.
  2. Current density. Cross-section area A=π[(2R)2−R2]=3πR2A=\pi\left[(2R)^2-R^2\right]=3\pi R^{2}A=π[(2R)2−R2]=3πR2, so J=I3πR2J=\dfrac{I}{3\pi R^{2}}J=3πR2I​.
  3. At r=32Rr=\tfrac32Rr=23​R. The enclosed annulus has area π[r2−R2]=π[94R2−R2]=54πR2\pi\left[r^{2}-R^{2}\right]=\pi\left[\tfrac94R^{2}-R^{2}\right]=\dfrac54\pi R^{2}π[r2−R2]=π[49​R2−R2]=45​πR2, so Ienc=J⋅54πR2=512II_\mathrm{enc}=J\cdot\dfrac54\pi R^{2}=\dfrac{5}{12}IIenc​=J⋅45​πR2=125​I.
  4. Field at 32R\tfrac32R23​R. B=μ0Ienc2πr=μ0 (5I/12)2π (3R/2)=5μ0I36πRB=\dfrac{\mu_0 I_\mathrm{enc}}{2\pi r}=\dfrac{\mu_0\,(5I/12)}{2\pi\,(3R/2)}=\dfrac{5\mu_0 I}{36\pi R}B=2πrμ0​Ienc​​=2π(3R/2)μ0​(5I/12)​=36πR5μ0​I​.
  5. At r=3Rr=3Rr=3R. The loop is outside the pipe, Ienc=II_\mathrm{enc}=IIenc​=I: B=μ0I2π(3R)=μ0I6πRB=\dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi(3R)}=\dfrac{\mu_0 I}{6\pi R}B=2π(3R)μ0​I​=6πRμ0​I​.
  6. Check. Using the general wall formula B(r)=μ0I2πrr2−R23R2B(r)=\dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}\dfrac{r^{2}-R^{2}}{3R^{2}}B(r)=2πrμ0​I​3R2r2−R2​ at r=2Rr=2Rr=2R gives μ0I/(4πR)\mu_0 I/(4\pi R)μ0​I/(4πR), which matches the exterior formula μ0I/2πr\mu_0I/2\pi rμ0​I/2πr at r=2Rr=2Rr=2R.
  7. Pitfall check. Using Ienc=I r2/(2R)2I_\mathrm{enc}=I\,r^{2}/(2R)^{2}Ienc​=Ir2/(2R)2 (a solid cylinder) ignores the empty hole and gives the wrong enclosed fraction.
  8. Final answer. B(32R)=5μ0I36πRB(\tfrac32R)=\dfrac{5\mu_0 I}{36\pi R}B(23​R)=36πR5μ0​I​ and B(3R)=μ0I6πRB(3R)=\dfrac{\mu_0 I}{6\pi R}B(3R)=6πRμ0​I​, both circulating around the axis (right-hand rule with the current).
