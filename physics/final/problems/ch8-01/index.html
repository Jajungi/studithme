Three resistors connected in parallel

병렬로 연결된 세 저항

Three resistors R1=3 ΩR_1=3\,\OmegaR1​=3Ω, R2=5 ΩR_2=5\,\OmegaR2​=5Ω, and R3=15 ΩR_3=15\,\OmegaR3​=15Ω are connected in parallel across a 9 V9\,\mathrm{V}9V source.

(a) Find the equivalent resistance. (b) Find the total current supplied by the source. (c) Find the current through and potential drop across each resistor.
한글
세 저항 R1=3 ΩR_1=3\,\OmegaR1​=3Ω, R2=5 ΩR_2=5\,\OmegaR2​=5Ω, R3=15 ΩR_3=15\,\OmegaR3​=15Ω이 9 V9\,\mathrm{V}9V 전원에 병렬로 연결되어 있다.

(a) 등가 저항, (b) 전원이 공급하는 전체 전류, (c) 각 저항을 흐르는 전류와 각 저항의 전위차를 구하라.

Hints
  1. For parallel resistors, 1/Req=1/R1+1/R2+1/R31/R_\mathrm{eq}=1/R_1+1/R_2+1/R_31/Req​=1/R1​+1/R2​+1/R3​.
  2. Every branch connected across the same two nodes has the same potential drop.
  3. Use Ik=V/RkI_k=V/R_kIk​=V/Rk​ and check that I=I1+I2+I3I=I_1+I_2+I_3I=I1​+I2​+I3​.
한글 힌트
  1. 병렬 연결에서는 1/Req=1/R1+1/R2+1/R31/R_\mathrm{eq}=1/R_1+1/R_2+1/R_31/Req​=1/R1​+1/R2​+1/R3​.
  2. 같은 두 노드 사이에 연결된 모든 가지의 전위차는 같다.
  3. Ik=V/RkI_k=V/R_kIk​=V/Rk​를 쓰고 I=I1+I2+I3I=I_1+I_2+I_3I=I1​+I2​+I3​인지 확인한다.
Worked solution
Answer
Req=5/3 Ω≈1.67 ΩR_\mathrm{eq}=5/3\,\Omega\approx1.67\,\OmegaReq​=5/3Ω≈1.67Ω; I=5.40 AI=5.40\,\mathrm{A}I=5.40A; (I1,I2,I3)=(3.0,1.8,0.6) A(I_1,I_2,I_3)=(3.0,1.8,0.6)\,\mathrm{A}(I1​,I2​,I3​)=(3.0,1.8,0.6)A
  1. Goal. Reduce the parallel network and determine both the source current and all branch quantities.
  2. Knowns. R1=3 ΩR_1=3\,\OmegaR1​=3Ω, R2=5 ΩR_2=5\,\OmegaR2​=5Ω, R3=15 ΩR_3=15\,\OmegaR3​=15Ω, and V=9 VV=9\,\mathrm{V}V=9V. All three resistors join the same pair of nodes.
  3. Equivalent resistance. 1Req=13+15+115=35 Ω−1\dfrac{1}{R_\mathrm{eq}}=\dfrac13+\dfrac15+\dfrac1{15}=\dfrac35\,\Omega^{-1}Req​1​=31​+51​+151​=53​Ω−1, so Req=53 Ω≈1.67 ΩR_\mathrm{eq}=\dfrac53\,\Omega\approx1.67\,\OmegaReq​=35​Ω≈1.67Ω.
  4. Total current. By Ohm’s law, I=V/Req=9/(5/3) A=5.40 AI=V/R_\mathrm{eq}=9/(5/3)\,\mathrm{A}=5.40\,\mathrm{A}I=V/Req​=9/(5/3)A=5.40A.
  5. Branch voltages. Parallel branches have identical endpoint potentials, so ΔV1=ΔV2=ΔV3=9.0 V\Delta V_1=\Delta V_2=\Delta V_3=9.0\,\mathrm{V}ΔV1​=ΔV2​=ΔV3​=9.0V.
  6. Branch currents. I1=9/3=3.0 AI_1=9/3=3.0\,\mathrm{A}I1​=9/3=3.0A, I2=9/5=1.8 AI_2=9/5=1.8\,\mathrm{A}I2​=9/5=1.8A, and I3=9/15=0.6 AI_3=9/15=0.6\,\mathrm{A}I3​=9/15=0.6A. Their sum is 5.4 A5.4\,\mathrm{A}5.4A, as required by charge conservation.
  7. Pitfall check. Do not add parallel resistances directly. Also, the current—not the voltage—is divided among these branches.
  8. Final answer. Req≈1.67 ΩR_\mathrm{eq}\approx1.67\,\OmegaReq​≈1.67Ω, I=5.40 AI=5.40\,\mathrm{A}I=5.40A; each resistor has 9.0 V9.0\,\mathrm{V}9.0V across it, with currents 3.0 A3.0\,\mathrm{A}3.0A, 1.8 A1.8\,\mathrm{A}1.8A, and 0.6 A0.6\,\mathrm{A}0.6A.
