A battery with ε=6.00 V\varepsilon=6.00\,\mathrm{V}ε=6.00V and no internal resistance supplies current to the circuit shown.

Read the figure as a wiring list. The battery occupies the left branch. The top wire runs from the battery through R1R_1R1​ to node ccc, then through a second resistor of the same value R2R_2R2​ to node ddd on the right-hand wire; from ddd the resistor R3R_3R3​ continues down to the bottom wire, which returns to the battery.
