Resistance of a solid truncated cone

원뿔대(frustum) 저항 공식

A solid truncated cone (frustum) of resistivity ρ\rhoρ has height hhh, radius aaa at one flat end, and radius bbb at the other (Figure 28.15). Current flows uniformly from one flat face to the other along the axis.

Derive a formula for the resistance RRR between the end faces.
한글
비저항 ρ\rhoρ인 원뿔대 높이 hhh, 한쪽 반지름 aaa, 다른 쪽 bbb. 축 방향으로 한 밑면에서 다른 밑면으로 전류가 균일하게 흐를 때, 저항 RRR 공식을 유도하라.

Worked solution
Answer
R=ρhπabR = \dfrac{\rho h}{\pi a b}R=πabρh​
  1. Goal. Derive RRR for a frustum when material resistivity ρ\rhoρ is uniform and current flows uniformly between the two circular faces.
  2. Setup. Place the aaa-radius face at x=0x=0x=0 and the bbb-radius face at x=hx=hx=h. Each slice at xxx has area A(x)=πr(x)2A(x)=\pi r(x)^2A(x)=πr(x)2 with r(x)=a+(b−a)x/hr(x)=a+(b-a)x/hr(x)=a+(b−a)x/h (linear taper).
  3. Differential resistance. A thin slab of thickness dxdxdx has dR=ρ dx/A(x)=ρ dx/[πr(x)2]dR=\rho\, dx/A(x)=\rho\, dx/[\pi r(x)^2]dR=ρdx/A(x)=ρdx/[πr(x)2]. This is the same R=ρL/AR=\rho L/AR=ρL/A idea applied locally when AAA varies slowly.
  4. Integrate along the axis. R=∫0hρ dxπr(x)2=ρπ∫0hdx[a+(b−a)x/h]2R=\int_0^h \frac{\rho\, dx}{\pi r(x)^2}=\frac{\rho}{\pi}\int_0^h \frac{dx}{[a+(b-a)x/h]^2}R=∫0h​πr(x)2ρdx​=πρ​∫0h​[a+(b−a)x/h]2dx​.
  5. Substitution. Let u=a+(b−a)x/hu=a+(b-a)x/hu=a+(b−a)x/h, so du=(b−a) dx/hdu=(b-a)\, dx/hdu=(b−a)dx/h and dx=(h/(b−a)) dudx=(h/(b-a))\, dudx=(h/(b−a))du. Limits: x=0⇒u=ax=0\Rightarrow u=ax=0⇒u=a; x=h⇒u=bx=h\Rightarrow u=bx=h⇒u=b.
  6. Evaluate the integral. R=ρπhb−a∫abduu2=ρhπ(b−a)[−1u]ab=ρhπ(b−a)(1a−1b)R=\frac{\rho}{\pi}\frac{h}{b-a}\int_a^b \frac{du}{u^2}=\frac{\rho h}{\pi(b-a)}\left[-\frac{1}{u}\right]_a^b=\frac{\rho h}{\pi(b-a)}\left(\frac{1}{a}-\frac{1}{b}\right)R=πρ​b−ah​∫ab​u2du​=π(b−a)ρh​[−u1​]ab​=π(b−a)ρh​(a1​−b1​).
  7. Algebra. 1a−1b=b−aab\frac{1}{a}-\frac{1}{b}=\frac{b-a}{ab}a1​−b1​=abb−a​, so the factors (b−a)(b-a)(b−a) cancel: R=ρhπabR=\dfrac{\rho h}{\pi a b}R=πabρh​.
  8. Checks and final answer. If a=ba=ba=b, then R=ρh/(πa2)R=\rho h/(\pi a^2)R=ρh/(πa2) (uniform cylinder). Result: R=ρhπabR=\dfrac{\rho h}{\pi a b}R=πabρh​.
한글 답
R=ρhπabR = \dfrac{\rho h}{\pi a b}R=πabρh​
한글 해설
  1. 목표. 균일 ρ\rhoρ, 균일 전류로 두 원형 면 사이 저항 RRR을 유도합니다.
  2. 좌표. x=0x=0x=0에서 반지름 aaa, x=hx=hx=h에서 bbb, r(x)=a+(b−a)x/hr(x)=a+(b-a)x/hr(x)=a+(b−a)x/h.
  3. 미소 저항. dR=ρ dx/[πr(x)2]dR=\rho\, dx/[\pi r(x)^2]dR=ρdx/[πr(x)2].
  4. 적분. R=ρπ∫0hdx[a+(b−a)x/h]2R=\frac{\rho}{\pi}\int_0^h \frac{dx}{[a+(b-a)x/h]^2}R=πρ​∫0h​[a+(b−a)x/h]2dx​.
  5. 치환. u=a+(b−a)x/hu=a+(b-a)x/hu=a+(b−a)x/h, dx=(h/(b−a))dudx=(h/(b-a))dudx=(h/(b−a))du, 적분 구간 a→ba\to ba→b.
  6. 적분값. R=ρhπ(b−a)(1a−1b)R=\frac{\rho h}{\pi(b-a)}\left(\frac{1}{a}-\frac{1}{b}\right)R=π(b−a)ρh​(a1​−b1​).
  7. 정리. 1a−1b=b−aab\frac{1}{a}-\frac{1}{b}=\frac{b-a}{ab}a1​−b1​=abb−a​이므로 R=ρhπabR=\dfrac{\rho h}{\pi a b}R=πabρh​.
  8. 확인·답. a=ba=ba=b이면 R=ρh/(πa2)R=\rho h/(\pi a^2)R=ρh/(πa2). 결과: R=ρhπabR=\dfrac{\rho h}{\pi a b}R=πabρh​.
