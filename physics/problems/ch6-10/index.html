Charged plate inserted between grounded plates

Two large parallel metal plates, each of area AAA, are separated by 3d3d3d and joined by a grounded wire (initially uncharged). A third identical plate carrying charge QQQ is inserted parallel between them, a distance ddd from the upper plate (see figure).

(a) Induced charge on each of the two original plates?
(b) Potential difference between the middle plate and each outer plate?
