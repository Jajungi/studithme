Why electrostatic force dominates at atomic scales

원자 스케일에서 전기력이 우세한 이유

Compare the magnitudes of the electric and gravitational forces between an electron and a proton separated by r=1.0 A˚r = 1.0\,\mathrm{\AA}r=1.0A˚. Which dominates? Why do we still feel gravity in daily life?

Worked solution
Answer
Fe/Fg∼1039F_e/F_g \sim 10^{39}Fe​/Fg​∼1039; gravity felt because matter is neutral macroscopically
  1. Goal. Compare electric and gravitational force magnitudes between an electron and a proton at r=1.0 A˚r=1.0\,\mathrm{\AA}r=1.0A˚, decide which dominates, and explain why we still feel gravity in daily life.
  2. Knowns. At separation rrr, use Fe=ke2/r2F_e=k e^2/r^2Fe​=ke2/r2 and Fg=Gmemp/r2F_g=G m_e m_p/r^2Fg​=Gme​mp​/r2 with standard constants (kkk, GGG, eee, mem_eme​, mpm_pmp​). The numerical distance 1 A˚1\,\mathrm{\AA}1A˚ enters both forces the same way.
  3. Why compare a ratio. Both forces scale as 1/r21/r^21/r2, so Fe/Fg=[ke2/(Gmemp)]F_e/F_g=[k e^2/(G m_e m_p)]Fe​/Fg​=[ke2/(Gme​mp​)] is independent of rrr for this pair. You may compute the ratio once and it applies at any separation.
  4. Evaluate the ratio. Fe/Fg=ke2/(Gmemp)≈2×1039F_e/F_g=k e^2/(G m_e m_p)\approx 2\times 10^{39}Fe​/Fg​=ke2/(Gme​mp​)≈2×1039. Electricity is overwhelmingly larger at atomic scales.
  5. Microscopic consequence. Atoms are held together by Coulomb attraction (with quantum mechanics fixing the size). Gravity between one electron and one proton inside an atom is utterly negligible.
  6. Macroscopic twist. Ordinary objects contain nearly equal positive and negative charge, so net electric forces cancel to extraordinary precision. Mass, however, adds, so gravity remains.
  7. Final answer. Fe/Fg∼1039F_e/F_g\sim 10^{39}Fe​/Fg​∼1039; Coulomb forces dominate atomic physics, while we feel gravity because bulk matter is electrically neutral overall.
