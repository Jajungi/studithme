Why E ≠ 0 in a current-carrying wire

전류가 흐르는 도체 안의 E

When current flows in a wire between source and sink of charge (battery terminals), there is an electric field inside the wire even though it is a conductor. Why does the usual conclusion “E⃗=0\vec E=0E=0 inside a conductor” not apply?
한글
전류가 흐르는 도선 안에 E가 있는 이유. ‘도체 안 E=0’이 안 적용되는 까닭은?

Hints
  1. That theorem assumes electrostatic equilibrium.
한글 힌트
  1. 정전기 평형 가정.
Worked solution
Answer
E=0 theorem needs electrostatic equilibrium; current is steady-state non-equilibrium
  1. E⃗=0\vec E=0E=0 inside a conductor is for electrostatics: charges have stopped moving; any residual field would rearrange free charges until the field cancels.
  2. A steady current is not electrostatic equilibrium — charges drift continuously.
  3. A small internal E⃗\vec EE (from the battery’s surface-charge gradient along the wire) is needed to maintain J=σEJ=\sigma EJ=σE.
