Dielectric slabs stacked (series)

유전체 적층(직렬)

A parallel-plate capacitor of plate area AAA and spacing ddd is filled with two parallel slabs of equal thickness d/2d/2d/2 with dielectric constants κ1\kappa_1κ1​ and κ2\kappa_2κ2​ (see figure). What is the capacitance? (Hint: equivalent to two capacitors in series.)

Hints
  1. Each half: Ci=κiε0A/(d/2)C_i=\kappa_i\varepsilon_0 A/(d/2)Ci​=κi​ε0​A/(d/2).
  2. Series: 1/C=1/C1+1/C21/C=1/C_1+1/C_21/C=1/C1​+1/C2​.
한글 힌트
  1. 각 반쪽: Ci=κiε0A/(d/2)C_i=\kappa_i\varepsilon_0 A/(d/2)Ci​=κi​ε0​A/(d/2).
  2. 직렬: 1/C=1/C1+1/C21/C=1/C_1+1/C_21/C=1/C1​+1/C2​.
Worked solution
Answer
C=2ε0Aκ1κ2d(κ1+κ2)C=\dfrac{2\varepsilon_0 A\kappa_1\kappa_2}{d(\kappa_1+\kappa_2)}C=d(κ1​+κ2​)2ε0​Aκ1​κ2​​
  1. Goal. Find the capacitance of a parallel-plate capacitor (area AAA, spacing ddd) filled with two dielectric slabs of thickness d/2d/2d/2 and constants κ1\kappa_1κ1​, κ2\kappa_2κ2​ stacked along the electric field.
  2. Series picture. The slab interface carries the same free charge as the plates, while the total voltage is the sum of drops in each half. That is exactly capacitors in series, not parallel.
  3. Half capacitors. Slab iii has thickness d/2d/2d/2 and area AAA, so Ci=κiε0A/(d/2)=2κiε0A/dC_i=\kappa_i\varepsilon_0 A/(d/2)=2\kappa_i\varepsilon_0 A/dCi​=κi​ε0​A/(d/2)=2κi​ε0​A/d.
  4. Series formula. 1C=1C1+1C2=d2ε0A(1κ1+1κ2)\dfrac{1}{C}=\dfrac{1}{C_1}+\dfrac{1}{C_2}=\dfrac{d}{2\varepsilon_0 A}\left(\dfrac{1}{\kappa_1}+\dfrac{1}{\kappa_2}\right)C1​=C1​1​+C2​1​=2ε0​Ad​(κ1​1​+κ2​1​).
  5. Solve for CCC. Inverting gives C=2ε0Aκ1κ2d(κ1+κ2)C=\dfrac{2\varepsilon_0 A\kappa_1\kappa_2}{d(\kappa_1+\kappa_2)}C=d(κ1​+κ2​)2ε0​Aκ1​κ2​​.
  6. Limiting cases. If κ1=κ2=κ\kappa_1=\kappa_2=\kappaκ1​=κ2​=κ, then C=κε0A/dC=\kappa\varepsilon_0 A/dC=κε0​A/d as for a uniform dielectric. The weaker slab dominates when one κ\kappaκ is much smaller.
  7. Pitfall. Do not add κ1+κ2\kappa_1+\kappa_2κ1​+κ2​ in the numerator without the series structure; stacked slabs along E⃗\vec EE add reciprocals of CCC, not CCC directly.
  8. Final answer. C=2ε0Aκ1κ2d(κ1+κ2)C=\dfrac{2\varepsilon_0 A\kappa_1\kappa_2}{d(\kappa_1+\kappa_2)}C=d(κ1​+κ2​)2ε0​Aκ1​κ2​​.

  1. 목표. 면적 AAA, 전체 간격 ddd인 평행판을 두께 d/2d/2d/2씩 κ1\kappa_1κ1​, κ2\kappa_2κ2​ 유전체가 전기장 방향으로 겹쳐 채웠을 때 CCC를 구한다.
  2. 직렬 그림. 슬래브 경계에는 같은 자유전하가 실리고, 전압은 각 반쪽에서의 강하 합이다. 따라서 직렬 두 축전기와 같다.
  3. 반쪽 용량. iii번 슬래브: Ci=κiε0A/(d/2)=2κiε0A/dC_i=\kappa_i\varepsilon_0 A/(d/2)=2\kappa_i\varepsilon_0 A/dCi​=κi​ε0​A/(d/2)=2κi​ε0​A/d.
  4. 직렬 공식. 1C=1C1+1C2=d2ε0A(1κ1+1κ2)\dfrac{1}{C}=\dfrac{1}{C_1}+\dfrac{1}{C_2}=\dfrac{d}{2\varepsilon_0 A}\left(\dfrac{1}{\kappa_1}+\dfrac{1}{\kappa_2}\right)C1​=C1​1​+C2​1​=2ε0​Ad​(κ1​1​+κ2​1​).
  5. CCC 정리. C=2ε0Aκ1κ2d(κ1+κ2)C=\dfrac{2\varepsilon_0 A\kappa_1\kappa_2}{d(\kappa_1+\kappa_2)}C=d(κ1​+κ2​)2ε0​Aκ1​κ2​​.
  6. 극한. κ1=κ2=κ\kappa_1=\kappa_2=\kappaκ1​=κ2​=κ이면 C=κε0A/dC=\kappa\varepsilon_0 A/dC=κε0​A/d로 균일 유전체와 일치한다. 한쪽 κ\kappaκ가 훨씬 작으면 그쪽이 직렬을 지배한다.
  7. 실수. E⃗\vec EE 방향으로 쌓인 슬래브는 CCC를 더하는 병렬이 아니라 1/C1/C1/C를 더하는 직렬이다.
  8. 답. C=2ε0Aκ1κ2d(κ1+κ2)C=\dfrac{2\varepsilon_0 A\kappa_1\kappa_2}{d(\kappa_1+\kappa_2)}C=d(κ1​+κ2​)2ε0​Aκ1​κ2​​.
