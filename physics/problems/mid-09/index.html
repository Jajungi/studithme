  1. (i) F⃗21=k(Q)(2Q)/D2\vec F_{21}=k(Q)(2Q)/D^2F21​=k(Q)(2Q)/D2 toward −x-x−x if Q1Q_1Q1​ is on the left (like charges). F⃗23=k(2Q)(3Q)/D2\vec F_{23}=k(2Q)(3Q)/D^2F23​=k(2Q)(3Q)/D2 toward +x+x+x (attraction to −3Q-3Q−3Q on the right). Net Fx=k(6Q2)/D2−k(2Q2)/D2=4kQ2/D2F_x=k(6Q^2)/D^2-k(2Q^2)/D^2=4kQ^2/D^2Fx​=k(6Q2)/D2−k(2Q2)/D2=4kQ2/D2 in the direction toward Q3Q_3Q3​.
  2. With x^\hat xx^ from Q1Q_1Q1​ to Q3Q_3Q3​: F⃗Q2=14πε04Q2D2x^\vec F_{Q_2}=\dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\dfrac{4Q^2}{D^2}\hat xFQ2​​=4πε0​1​D24Q2​x^.
