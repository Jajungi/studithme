Electrons in one cup of water vs protons in another

물 두 컵: 전자–양성자 쿨롱력

Each cup holds 250 g250\,\mathrm{g}250g of water (MH2O=18 g/molM_\mathrm{H_2O} = 18\,\mathrm{g/mol}MH2​O​=18g/mol, NA=6.02×1023 mol−1N_A = 6.02\times 10^{23}\,\mathrm{mol^{-1}}NA​=6.02×1023mol−1). Approximate each molecule as having 10 electrons and 10 protons. Estimate the magnitude of the electric force that all electrons in cup A exert on all protons in cup B when the cups are separated by 10 m10\,\mathrm{m}10m (treat each cup’s relevant charges as concentrated at its center).

Hints
  1. Nmol=(0.250/18)NAN_\mathrm{mol} = (0.250/18)N_ANmol​=(0.250/18)NA​ molecules per cup.
  2. F≈k(Ne)(Ne)/r2F \approx k (N e)(N e)/r^2F≈k(Ne)(Ne)/r2 with N=10NmolN = 10 N_\mathrm{mol}N=10Nmol​.
한글 힌트
  1. Nmol=(0.250/18)NAN_\mathrm{mol}=(0.250/18)N_ANmol​=(0.250/18)NA​.
  2. F≈k(10Nmole)2/r2F \approx k(10N_\mathrm{mol}e)^2/r^2F≈k(10Nmol​e)2/r2.
Worked solution
Answer
F≈1.6×1020 NF \approx 1.6\times 10^{20}\,\mathrm{N}F≈1.6×1020N (attractive)
  1. Molecules per cup. n=0.250/18≈13.9 moln = 0.250/18 \approx 13.9\,\mathrm{mol}n=0.250/18≈13.9mol, so Nmol=nNA≈8.4×1024N_\mathrm{mol} = n N_A \approx 8.4\times 10^{24}Nmol​=nNA​≈8.4×1024.
  2. Total charge magnitudes. Electrons in A: Qe=(10Nmol)eQ_e = (10 N_\mathrm{mol}) eQe​=(10Nmol​)e. Protons in B: Qp=(10Nmol)eQ_p = (10 N_\mathrm{mol}) eQp​=(10Nmol​)e.
  3. Point-charge estimate. F=kQeQp/r2=k(10Nmole)2/r2F = k Q_e Q_p/r^2 = k (10 N_\mathrm{mol} e)^2/r^2F=kQe​Qp​/r2=k(10Nmol​e)2/r2 with r=10 mr=10\,\mathrm{m}r=10m.
  4. Plug in. e≈1.60×10−19 Ce \approx 1.60\times 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}e≈1.60×10−19C, k≈8.99×109 N⋅m2/C2k \approx 8.99\times 10^9\,\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/C^2}k≈8.99×109N⋅m2/C2.
  5. Order of magnitude. F∼1020 NF \sim 10^{20}\,\mathrm{N}F∼1020N — enormous compared with everyday forces, illustrating how strong Coulomb interactions are before cancellation in neutral matter.
