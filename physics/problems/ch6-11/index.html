Spherical capacitor with dielectric — free/bound σ

구형 축전기 유전체 — 자유/속박 σ

A spherical capacitor: metal sphere radius R1R_1R1​ and concentric thin shell radius R2R_2R2​, filled with dielectric κ\kappaκ. The free surface-charge density on the inner sphere is σfree(1)\sigma_{\mathrm{free}}(1)σfree​(1).

(a) Free surface-charge density on the shell at R2R_2R2​?
(b) Bound surface-charge density on the dielectric at R1R_1R1​?
(c) Bound surface-charge density on the dielectric at R2R_2R2​?

Worked solution
Answer
(a) −σfree(1)(R1/R2)2-\sigma_{\mathrm{free}}(1)(R_1/R_2)^2−σfree​(1)(R1​/R2​)2; (b) −σfree(1)(1−1/κ)-\sigma_{\mathrm{free}}(1)(1-1/\kappa)−σfree​(1)(1−1/κ); (c) +σfree(1)(1−1/κ)(R1/R2)2+\sigma_{\mathrm{free}}(1)(1-1/\kappa)(R_1/R_2)^2+σfree​(1)(1−1/κ)(R1​/R2​)2
  1. (a) Gauss: outer free charge cancels inner free charge ⇒ σfree(2)=−σfree(1) (R1/R2)2\sigma_{\mathrm{free}}(2)=-\sigma_{\mathrm{free}}(1)\,(R_1/R_2)^2σfree​(2)=−σfree​(1)(R1​/R2​)2.
  2. Field in dielectric. D=σfree(1)(R1/r)2r^D=\sigma_{\mathrm{free}}(1)(R_1/r)^2\hat rD=σfree​(1)(R1​/r)2r^, E=D/(κε0)E=D/(\kappa\varepsilon_0)E=D/(κε0​).
  3. (b) At R1R_1R1​, bound σb=−P⋅r^=−σfree(1)(1−1/κ)\sigma_b=-P\cdot\hat r=-\sigma_{\mathrm{free}}(1)\left(1-1/\kappa\right)σb​=−P⋅r^=−σfree​(1)(1−1/κ).
  4. (c) At R2R_2R2​ (outward normal of dielectric toward decreasing rrr careful with sign): σb=+σfree(1)(1−1/κ)(R1/R2)2\sigma_b=+\sigma_{\mathrm{free}}(1)\left(1-1/\kappa\right)(R_1/R_2)^2σb​=+σfree​(1)(1−1/κ)(R1​/R2​)2.
한글 답
(a) −σ1(R1/R2)2-\sigma_1(R_1/R_2)^2−σ1​(R1​/R2​)2; (b)(c) ±σ1(1−1/κ)⋯\pm\sigma_1(1-1/\kappa)\cdots±σ1​(1−1/κ)⋯
한글 해설
  1. (a) σfree(2)=−σfree(1)(R1/R2)2\sigma_{\mathrm{free}}(2)=-\sigma_{\mathrm{free}}(1)(R_1/R_2)^2σfree​(2)=−σfree​(1)(R1​/R2​)2.
  2. (b)(c) σb=±σfree(1)(1−1/κ)\sigma_b=\pm\sigma_{\mathrm{free}}(1)(1-1/\kappa)σb​=±σfree​(1)(1−1/κ) (면적비 포함).
