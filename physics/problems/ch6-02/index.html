Worked solution
Answer
Parallel: Ceq=9 μFC_\mathrm{eq}=9\,\mu\mathrm{F}Ceq​=9μF, Q=1.08×10−4 CQ=1.08\times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{C}Q=1.08×10−4C; series: Ceq=2 μFC_\mathrm{eq}=2\,\mu\mathrm{F}Ceq​=2μF, Q=2.4×10−5 CQ=2.4\times 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{C}Q=2.4×10−5C
  1. Parallel rule. Same voltage across each: Ceq=C1+C2=9.0 μFC_\mathrm{eq}=C_1+C_2=9.0\,\mu\mathrm{F}Ceq​=C1​+C2​=9.0μF.
  2. Parallel charge. Qtot=CeqV=(9.0×10−6)(12)=1.08×10−4 CQ_\mathrm{tot}=C_\mathrm{eq}V=(9.0\times 10^{-6})(12)=1.08\times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{C}Qtot​=Ceq​V=(9.0×10−6)(12)=1.08×10−4C.
  3. Series rule. Same charge on each: 1/Ceq=1/C1+1/C2⇒Ceq=2.0 μF1/C_\mathrm{eq}=1/C_1+1/C_2 \Rightarrow C_\mathrm{eq}=2.0\,\mu\mathrm{F}1/Ceq​=1/C1​+1/C2​⇒Ceq​=2.0μF.
  4. Series charge. Q=CeqV=(2.0×10−6)(12)=2.4×10−5 CQ=C_\mathrm{eq}V=(2.0\times 10^{-6})(12)=2.4\times 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{C}Q=Ceq​V=(2.0×10−6)(12)=2.4×10−5C (same on each plate pair).
  5. Compare. Parallel stores more charge at the same VVV because effective plate area/stacking increases free charge capacity.
