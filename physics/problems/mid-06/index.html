Nonconducting slab — E and spherical cavity

대전 슬래브와 구형 공동

A flat slab of nonconducting material has volume charge density ρ\rhoρ and thickness ddd (small compared with lateral size). Express answers in terms of given quantities and constants.

(i) Electric field inside the slab (magnitude and direction).
(ii) Electric field outside the slab.
(iii) A spherical cavity of radius d/2d/2d/2 is cut with center in the midplane (see figure). Find E⃗\vec EE inside the cavity.
한글

  2. (i) Inside at distance xxx from midplane: Ex=ρx/ε0E_x=\rho x/\varepsilon_0Ex​=ρx/ε0​ (odd in xxx; toward outside if ρ>0\rho>0ρ>0).
